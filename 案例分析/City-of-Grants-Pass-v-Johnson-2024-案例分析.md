# City of Grants Pass v. Johnson (2024) 案例分析：无家可归者在公共土地睡觉，城市能不能罚

> 个人学习笔记，不构成法律建议。Personal study notes, not legal advice.

## 一、事实与程序：俄勒冈小城的露营禁令与第九巡回的 Martin 规则

### 中文
案名与出处先钉准：City of Grants Pass v. Johnson, 603 U.S. 520 (2024)，美国联邦最高法院，2024 年 6 月 28 日以 6 比 3 判决，Gorsuch 大法官执笔多数意见。俄勒冈州 Grants Pass 市的市政条例禁止在公共土地露营与在公园过夜，初犯罚款 295 美元，反复违规可被禁止进入公园，其后再犯可按刑事侵入处理，最高面临 30 天监禁与 1,250 美元罚金——数字以判决书所载条例后果为准。无家可归的原告 Gloria Johnson 等人提起集体诉讼，主张对没有庇护床位可用的人执行这类条例，等于因「无家可归」这一身份施加残忍与异常惩罚，违反第八修正案。下级审理的关键背景在第九巡回：Martin v. City of Boise, 920 F.3d 584 (9th Cir. 2019) 曾定下规则，只要市内可用的庇护床位少于无家可归者人数，城市就不得对在公共场所睡觉的人执行露营禁令。Grants Pass 一案的地方法院与第九巡回都按 Martin 判原告胜诉，最高法院同意调卷后推翻。本案与本仓讲「身份不能入罪」的旧案传统同线，又亲手给那条线划了边界，是理解第八修正案「惩罚什么」与加州露营条例为何在 2024 年后纷纷改写的第一案。

### English
Fix the citation first: City of Grants Pass v. Johnson, 603 U.S. 520 (2024), decided by the U.S. Supreme Court on June 28, 2024, by a 6-to-3 vote, with Justice Gorsuch writing for the majority. Municipal ordinances in Grants Pass, Oregon, barred camping on public land and overnight parking in parks; a first violation drew a fine of $295, repeat violations could lead to exclusion from the parks, and a further violation could be treated as criminal trespass carrying up to 30 days in jail and a $1,250 fine — figures taken from the consequences described in the opinion itself. Gloria Johnson and other homeless plaintiffs sued as a class, arguing that enforcing such ordinances against people with no available shelter bed punishes the status of homelessness and so imposes cruel and unusual punishment under the Eighth Amendment. The lower courts worked in the shadow of Martin v. City of Boise, 920 F.3d 584 (9th Cir. 2019), which had held that a city may not enforce anti-camping ordinances against people sleeping in public when available shelter beds are fewer than the number of homeless persons. The district court and the Ninth Circuit ruled for the plaintiffs under Martin; the Supreme Court granted review and reversed. The case both joins and limits the older line that a status cannot be made a crime, and it is the first case to read when asking why California camping ordinances were rewritten after 2024. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

### Español (traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión nativa)
Primero la cita exacta: City of Grants Pass v. Johnson, 603 U.S. 520 (2024), resuelto por la Corte Suprema de EE. UU. el 28 de junio de 2024 por 6 a 3, con opinión mayoritaria del juez Gorsuch. Las ordenanzas de Grants Pass, Oregón, prohibían acampar en suelo público y pernoctar en parques; la primera infracción costaba 295 dólares, las reiteradas podían llevar a la exclusión de los parques y una nueva infracción podía tratarse como allanamiento penal con hasta 30 días de cárcel y 1.250 dólares de multa, cifras tomadas de las consecuencias descritas en la propia sentencia. Gloria Johnson y otras personas sin hogar demandaron colectivamente: aplicar esas ordenanzas contra quien no tiene cama de refugio disponible castiga la condición de no tener hogar y viola la prohibición de castigos crueles e inusuales de la Octava Enmienda. Los tribunales inferiores trabajaron bajo Martin v. City of Boise, 920 F.3d 584 (9th Cir. 2019), que impedía aplicar las prohibiciones de acampada cuando las camas disponibles eran menos que las personas sin hogar. El tribunal de distrito y el Noveno Circuito dieron la razón a los demandantes; la Corte Suprema revisó y revocó. El caso continúa y a la vez limita la vieja línea de que una condición no puede ser delito, y explica por qué tantas ordenanzas californianas se reescribieron tras 2024. ## 二、多数意见：第八修正案管的是惩罚方式，不是替城市写无家可归政策

### 中文
多数意见的推理分三层。第一层回到文本与历史：第八修正案的残忍与异常惩罚条款，传统上管的是定罪之后用什么方式惩罚——历史上那些野蛮的刑罚方法与明显不成比例的刑期，而不是先去决定某种行为能不能入罪；能不能入罪，原则上是立法机关的民主决定。第二层处理 Robinson v. California, 370 U.S. 660 (1962)：该案推翻加州把「吸毒成瘾」本身定罪的法律，因为它罚的纯粹是身份（status）；多数意见把 Robinson 读得很窄——它禁的是以身份为唯一对象的定罪，Grants Pass 的条例罚的是在公共土地露营这一行为（conduct），而且对任何人都一体适用，不是只针对无家可归者。Powell v. Texas, 392 U.S. 514 (1968) 当年拒绝把 Robinson 扩到公共场所醉酒行为，本案沿着 Powell 的克制走。第三层是制度能力：Martin 规则要求联邦法官逐市清点床位与人数、判断床位算不算「可用」，等于让法院长期托管地方无家可归政策；多数意见直说这类政策难题应由人民与民选代表处理，全国各地已有的实验与分歧不该被一条第八修正案规则冻结。注意它没有说的：判决没有宣布城市必须清理营地，也没有碰正当程序、第一修正案等其他可能的挑战路径，它只关掉了 Martin 这一扇第八修正案的门。

### English
The majority reasons in three layers. First, text and history: the Eighth Amendment's Cruel and Unusual Punishments Clause traditionally governs the method of punishment imposed after conviction — barbaric modes of punishment and grossly disproportionate terms — rather than deciding in advance which conduct a legislature may criminalize at all; that choice belongs in principle to democratic lawmaking. Second, Robinson v. California, 370 U.S. 660 (1962), which struck down a California law making drug addiction itself a crime, is read narrowly: Robinson bars convictions whose sole object is a status, while the Grants Pass ordinances punish the conduct of camping on public land, apply to everyone alike, and do not single out homeless persons. The Court follows the restraint of Powell v. Texas, 392 U.S. 514 (1968), which had refused to extend Robinson to public drunkenness. Third, institutional capacity: the Martin rule required federal judges to count beds and people city by city and decide which beds count as available, in effect putting federal courts in long-term charge of local homelessness policy; the majority says such policy problems belong to the people and their elected representatives, and that local experiments should not be frozen by a single Eighth Amendment rule. Note what the opinion does not say: it does not order any city to clear encampments, and it leaves other possible challenges — due process or the First Amendment, for example — untouched; it closes only the Eighth Amendment door that Martin had opened. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

### Español (traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión nativa)
La mayoría razona en tres capas. Primera, texto e historia: la cláusula de castigos crueles e inusuales de la Octava Enmienda regula tradicionalmente el método de castigo tras la condena — modos bárbaros y penas groseramente desproporcionadas — y no decide de antemano qué conducta puede penalizar el legislador; esa elección corresponde en principio a la democracia legislativa. Segunda, Robinson v. California, 370 U.S. 660 (1962), que anuló la ley californiana que convertía la adicción misma en delito, se lee en sentido estrecho: Robinson prohíbe condenas cuyo único objeto es una condición, mientras las ordenanzas de Grants Pass castigan la conducta de acampar en suelo público, se aplican a todos por igual y no señalan solo a las personas sin hogar. La Corte sigue la contención de Powell v. Texas, 392 U.S. 514 (1968), que se negó a extender Robinson a la embriaguez en público. Tercera, capacidad institucional: la regla Martin obligaba a los jueces federales a contar camas y personas ciudad por ciudad y a decidir qué cama cuenta como disponible, dejando a los tribunales a cargo duradero de la política local sobre sinhogarismo; la mayoría responde que esos problemas pertenecen al pueblo y a sus representantes electos. La sentencia no ordena limpiar campamentos ni cierra otras vías como el debido proceso o la Primera Enmienda: solo cierra la puerta de la Octava Enmienda que Martin había abierto. ## 三、异议：睡觉是生理必需，罚无处可睡的人就是在罚身份

### 中文
Sotomayor 大法官的异议（Breyer 退休后由 Jackson、Kagan 加入，共 3 票）从一个朴素事实出发：睡眠是人的生理必需，不是可选择的消费行为；当一个人没有任何合法的地方可以睡，罚他在公共场所睡觉，实际效果就是罚他「无家可归」本身，与 Robinson 罚成瘾身份只是隔了一层纸。异议还点出条例的实际运作并不中立——有房子的人在自家院里露营不会被罚，条例的执法对象在现实中几乎全是无家可归者，罚款累积、公园禁入与刑事侵入的升级链条只会把人越推越远，离稳定住所更远。多数意见担心的「法官清点床位太难」，异议认为是 Martin 施行几年里法院已经在做的可操作工作，难度不等于违宪分析可以缺席。读本案要两边一起读才完整：多数赢在「第八修正案的射程与法院的角色」，异议赢在「行为与身份在这类案件里到底能不能分开」的直觉——后续各州立法与市政辩论里被反复引用的，恰恰常是异议的这几句。考试与写作时把票型写清：6 比 3，多数 Gorsuch，异议 Sotomayor，不要只写「最高法院允许罚露营」一句了事，那会把 Robinson 被限缩而非推翻这一关键细节丢掉。

### English
Justice Sotomayor's dissent (joined by Justices Jackson and Kagan — three votes) starts from a plain fact: sleep is a biological necessity, not a chosen consumer act; when a person has no lawful place to sleep, punishing public sleeping in practice punishes homelessness itself, separated from Robinson's punishment of addiction status by a sheet of paper. The dissent adds that the ordinances do not operate neutrally in the real world — a housed person camping in a private yard is not fined, the people actually cited are almost always homeless, and the ladder of accumulating fines, park exclusions and criminal trespass pushes people further from stable housing, not closer. As for the majority's worry that counting beds is too hard for judges, the dissent answers that courts had done that work operably in the years Martin was in force, and difficulty is no excuse for abandoning constitutional analysis. The case must be read whole: the majority prevails on the reach of the Eighth Amendment and the judicial role, while the dissent prevails, rhetorically, on whether conduct and status can really be separated here — and it is the dissent's lines that state legislatures and city debates keep quoting. In an exam or memo, state the lineup — 6 to 3, Gorsuch for the majority, Sotomayor in dissent — and do not reduce the case to the Supreme Court allows camping fines; that would lose the key point that Robinson was narrowed, not overruled. ## 四、遗产与加州落点：Martin 退场之后，清理营地的边界改由州法与程序划

### 中文

### English
The case's direct legacy is the end of Martin v. City of Boise within the Ninth Circuit: the federal constitutional argument that a shortage of beds blocks camping enforcement no longer works, and cities in California and the other Ninth Circuit states regained room to rewrite their ordinances. California's chain reaction was fast — on July 25, 2024, after the decision, the Governor issued an executive order directing the clearing of encampments on state land and urging local governments to follow, and many cities then adopted or revived public-camping and sidewalk-obstruction ordinances. Keep the boundaries straight when reading those moves: Grants Pass answers only the Eighth Amendment, while enforcement still runs into other sources of law — seizure and destruction of a homeless person's belongings still raise Fourth and Fourteenth Amendment problems (the Ninth Circuit's property-protection line runs through Lavan v. City of Los Angeles, 693 F.3d 1022 (9th Cir. 2012)), and notice periods, property storage and retrieval, and reasonable accommodations for people with disabilities under the ADA and section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act are not erased by this judgment; California adds its state constitution and municipal hearing procedures on top. Three closing exam points: the holding is that enforcing generally applicable laws regulating camping on public property does not violate the Eighth Amendment; Robinson survives in the limited form that pure status crimes remain barred; Powell is the waystation, and Grants Pass is the case that keeps Robinson inside the status box. Anyone who reads the case as cities may clear encampments at will will lose the Lavan and procedure points. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

### 한국어 (초역, 원어민 감수 대기)
이 사건의 직접적 유산은 제9순회 안에서 Martin v. City of Boise의 종료입니다. 침상이 부족하다는 이유로 캠핑 단속을 막는 연방 헌법 논거는 더 이상 통하지 않게 됐고, 캘리포니아와 제9순회 각 주의 도시들은 조례를 다시 쓸 공간을 되찾았습니다. 캘리포니아의 연쇄 반응은 빨랐습니다. 2024년 7월 25일 판결 뒤 주지사가 행정명령으로 주 소유 토지의 캠프 정리를 지시하고 지방정부의 동참을 촉구했고, 여러 도시가 공공 캠핑과 보도 점거 조례를 새로 만들거나 부활시켰습니다. 이런 후속 움직임을 읽을 때는 경계를 분명히 해야 합니다. Grants Pass가 답한 것은 수정헌법 제8조뿐이고, 집행은 여전히 다른 법원에 부딪힙니다. 노숙인의 소지품을 압수·폐기하는 문제는 수정헌법 제4조와 제14조의 문제를 계속 일으키고(제9순회의 재산 보호 노선은 Lavan v. City of Los Angeles, 693 F.3d 1022 (9th Cir. 2012)를 지납니다), 정리 통지 기간, 소지품 보관과 반환 절차, ADA와 재활법 제504조에 따른 장애인의 합리적 편의는 이 판결로 지워지지 않습니다. 캘리포니아에서는 주 헌법과 시 청문 절차까지 겹칩니다. 시험용 마무리 셋: 판결 요지는 공공재산 캠핑을 규율하는 일반적용 법률의 집행이 수정헌법 제8조를 위반하지 않는다는 것, Robinson은 순수 신분 범죄 금지라는 좁은 범위에서 살아남았다는 것, 그리고 이 사건을 '도시가 마음대로 캠프를 치워도 된다'로 읽으면 Lavan과 절차 쟁점에서 점수를 잃는다는 것입니다. 개인 학습 노트이며 법률 자문이 아닙니다. (초역·待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)
Di sản trực tiếp của vụ án là sự kết thúc của Martin v. City of Boise trong phạm vi Tuần hoàn số 9: lập luận hiến pháp liên bang rằng thiếu giường tạm trú thì không được thi hành lệnh cấm cắm trại không còn đứng vững, và các thành phố ở California cùng các bang khác thuộc tuần hoàn này lấy lại khoảng trống để viết lại sắc lệnh. Phản ứng dây chuyền ở California đến rất nhanh — ngày 25-7-2024, sau phán quyết, thống đốc ban hành lệnh hành pháp chỉ đạo dọn dẹp các khu trại trên đất của bang và thúc giục chính quyền địa phương làm theo, và nhiều thành phố sau đó ban hành mới hoặc khôi phục các sắc lệnh về cắm trại nơi công cộng và cản trở vỉa hè. Khi đọc những động thái tiếp theo đó, cần giữ rõ ranh giới: Grants Pass chỉ trả lời Tu chính án 8, còn việc thi hành vẫn va chạm các nguồn luật khác — việc tịch thu và tiêu hủy đồ đạc của người vô gia cư vẫn đặt ra vấn đề của Tu chính án 4 và 14 (đường lối bảo vệ tài sản của Tuần hoàn số 9 đi qua Lavan v. City of Los Angeles, 693 F.3d 1022 (9th Cir. 2012)), và thời hạn thông báo dọn dẹp, việc lưu giữ và trả lại đồ đạc, cùng điều chỉnh hợp lý cho người khuyết tật theo ADA và Mục 504 của Đạo luật Phục hồi chức năng đều không bị bản án này xóa bỏ; California còn chồng thêm hiến pháp bang và thủ tục điều trần của thành phố. Ba điểm chốt để đi thi: phán quyết cốt lõi là việc thi hành các đạo luật áp dụng chung điều chỉnh cắm trại trên tài sản công không vi phạm Tu chính án 8; Robinson sống sót ở phạm vi hẹp rằng tội thuần túy về tình trạng vẫn bị cấm; và ai đọc vụ án này thành thành phố được tùy ý dẹp trại thì sẽ mất điểm ở phần Lavan và thủ tục. Ghi chú học tập cá nhân, không phải tư vấn pháp lý. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, मूल-भाषी समीक्षा लंबित)
इस मामले की प्रत्यक्ष विरासत Ninth Circuit के भीतर Martin v. City of Boise का अंत है: beds की कमी camping-प्रवर्तन को रोकती है, यह संघीय संवैधानिक तर्क अब नहीं चलता, और California तथा अन्य Ninth Circuit राज्यों के शहरों को अपने अध्यादेश फिर लिखने की गुंजाइश मिल गई। California की शृंखला-प्रतिक्रिया तेज़ आई — 25 जुलाई 2024 को, निर्णय के बाद, गवर्नर ने कार्यकारी आदेश से राज्य-भूमि पर camps की सफ़ाई का निर्देश दिया और स्थानीय सरकारों से अनुसरण का आग्रह किया, और कई शहरों ने सार्वजनिक camping व फुटपाथ-बाधा अध्यादेश बनाए या पुनर्जीवित किए। इन कदमों को पढ़ते समय सीमाएँ स्पष्ट रखें: Grants Pass केवल आठवें संशोधन का उत्तर देता है, जबकि प्रवर्तन अन्य विधि-स्रोतों से अब भी टकराता है — बेघर व्यक्ति के सामान की ज़ब्ती व नष्ट करना चौथे व चौदहवें संशोधन की समस्याएँ खड़ी करता रहता है (Ninth Circuit की संपत्ति-संरक्षण परंपरा Lavan v. City of Los Angeles, 693 F.3d 1022 (9th Cir. 2012) से गुज़रती है), और सफ़ाई-नोटिस की अवधि, सामान का भंडारण व वापसी, तथा ADA और Rehabilitation Act की धारा 504 के तहत विकलांगों के लिए reasonable accommodation इस निर्णय से मिटते नहीं; California में राज्य संविधान और नगरपालिका सुनवाई-प्रक्रियाएँ और जुड़ती हैं। तीन समापन-परीक्षा बिंदु: धारणा यह है कि सार्वजनिक संपत्ति पर camping को नियंत्रित करने वाले सामान्य रूप से लागू कानूनों का प्रवर्तन आठवें संशोधन का उल्लंघन नहीं करता; Robinson इस सीमित रूप में जीवित है कि शुद्ध status अपराध अब भी वर्जित है; और जो इस मामले को शहर मनमाने ढंग से camp हटा सकते हैं ऐसा पढ़ेगा, वह Lavan और प्रक्रिया के अंक गँवाएगा। व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन नोट; विधिक सलाह नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
