# United States v. Skrmetti, 605 U.S. 495 (2025) 案例分析：给未成年人的药按用途分线，为什么过不了中度审查这一关

> 板块：案例分析｜案名：United States v. Skrmetti, 605 U.S. 495 (2025)，美国联邦最高法院，案号 No. 23-477，2024 年 12 月 4 日辩论、2025 年 6 月 18 日判决，首席大法官 Roberts 执笔，6 比 3 维持第六巡回。

## 一、事实与程序：同一味药，治什么病决定能不能开

### 中文

2023 年，田纳西州通过参议院第 1 号法案（SB1），编入《田纳西法典注释》第 68-33-101 条起（Tenn. Code Ann. sec. 68-33-101 et seq.）。这部法的划线方式很特别：它不按药分，按目的分。医疗提供者不得向未成年人开具、给予青春期阻断剂或激素，目的若是让未成年人以与其生理性别不一致的身份认同生活、或治疗性别焦虑、性别身份障碍、性别不一致；但同一味药，用来治疗先天缺陷、性早熟、疾病或身体损伤，则完全合法。违反者的执法与执照纪律写在同章第 68-33-105 条一带，由州总检察长执行、执照机关处分。

三名未成年原告、其父母与一名医生在法案生效前提起执行前挑战，美国联邦政府介入支持原告一方，主张 SB1 违反第十四修正案平等保护条款。田纳西中区联邦地区法院按中度审查（heightened scrutiny）发了初步禁令，先拦下激素与阻断剂部分的执行。第六巡回在 L.W. v. Skrmetti, 83 F.4th 460 (6th Cir. 2023) 中推翻：该法不触发高度审查，按理性审查也站得住。最高法院调卷审理。

时间先算清：第六巡回判决日为 2023 年 9 月 28 日，最高法院判决日为 2025 年 6 月 18 日，两日相距 629 天（2025-06-18 减 2023-09-28，本地复算两遍一致）。这 629 天里，争点始终只有一个：SB1 到底要过哪一级审查。

### English

In 2023 Tennessee enacted Senate Bill 1 (SB1), codified at Tenn. Code Ann. sec. 68-33-101 et seq. The statute draws its line by purpose, not by drug: a provider may not prescribe or administer puberty blockers or hormones to a minor to enable identification inconsistent with the minor's sex or to treat gender dysphoria, gender identity disorder, or gender incongruence, while the same medications remain lawful to treat a congenital defect, precocious puberty, disease, or physical injury. Enforcement and licensing discipline sit around sec. 68-33-105.

Three transgender minors, their parents, and a physician brought a pre-enforcement challenge, with the United States intervening on their side, arguing that SB1 violates the Equal Protection Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment. The district court applied heightened scrutiny and preliminarily enjoined the hormone and puberty-blocker provisions. The Sixth Circuit reversed in L.W. v. Skrmetti, 83 F.4th 460 (6th Cir. 2023). The Supreme Court granted certiorari (No. 23-477). The Sixth Circuit ruled on September 28, 2023, and the Supreme Court decided on June 18, 2025 - 629 days apart (recomputed locally, twice, same result).

## 二、判决：先定分类，再定审查，最后才谈理由

### 中文

2025 年 6 月 18 日，最高法院以 6 比 3 维持第六巡回。Roberts 首席大法官执笔多数意见，Thomas、Gorsuch、Kavanaugh、Barrett 全数加入，Alito 只加入第一部分与 II-B 部分。推理分三步。

第一步，SB1 不按性别分类。它按两条线分类：年龄线（未满 18 岁）与医疗用途线（同药异用，待遇不同）。多数意见援引 Geduldig v. Aiello, 417 U.S. 484 (1974) 的老逻辑：当年加州残障保险排除妊娠相关残障，法院说那分的是孕妇与非孕妇、不是男女；本案同理，分的是为哪种病开药，不是谁来吃药。男女未成年人一视同仁地被挡在性别焦虑用途之外，性别不是分类轴。

第二步，SB1 也不按跨性别身份分类，多数意见没有把跨性别身份宣布为可疑或准可疑分类（suspect / quasi-suspect class），因此不触发中度审查。Barrett 大法官的协同意见（Thomas 加入）正面补强：跨性别身份不满足准可疑分类的判准。原告请来的 Bostock v. Clayton County, 590 U.S. 644 (2020) 被明确挡回：那是《民权法》第七编的文本问题，多数意见明言其推理不移植到平等保护分析，其他语境的问题留待他案。

第三步，理性审查（rational basis）过关。这一级审查只问：分类是否与正当政府利益理性相关，支持分类的事实只需达到任何可合理设想的事实状态（any reasonably conceivable state of facts，见 Heller v. Doe, 509 U.S. 312 (1993) 一线）即可，不要求州方举证到科学定论。田纳西给出的保护未成年人、面对疗效与不可逆后果的医学不确定性等理由，足够通过。Sotomayor 大法官的异议（Kagan、Jackson 加入）认为这就是性别分类、应过中度审查且过不了；Kagan 与 Jackson 另有异议文字。

### English

On June 18, 2025, the Court affirmed 6-3. Chief Justice Roberts wrote for the majority, joined in full by Thomas, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh, and Barrett; Alito joined Parts I and II-B. First, SB1 classifies by age and by medical use, not by sex: the same drug is treated differently depending on the condition treated, echoing Geduldig v. Aiello, 417 U.S. 484 (1974). Second, SB1 does not classify on the basis of transgender status either, and the Court did not treat transgender status as a suspect or quasi-suspect class, so heightened scrutiny is not triggered; Justice Barrett's concurrence (joined by Thomas) argues that point directly. Bostock v. Clayton County, 590 U.S. 644 (2020), was confined to Title VII and expressly not carried over. Third, SB1 survives rational-basis review: protecting minors amid medical uncertainty about contested, potentially irreversible treatments is a legitimate interest rationally served, under the forgiving any reasonably conceivable state of facts standard of Heller v. Doe, 509 U.S. 312 (1993). Justice Sotomayor dissented, joined by Kagan and Jackson.

## 三、射程与加州落点：联邦只定地板，父母权利那一问没有答

### 中文

读本案先读准三条边界。第一，它定的只是联邦平等保护的地板：限制型州法今后在联邦法院大体按理性审查受审，但州仍可依本州宪法与州法提供更高保护，本案没有堵这条路。第二，父母代未成年子女决定医疗的正当程序主张没有进入本案争点，法院没有裁决，别把本案读成父母权利的答案。第三，Bostock 在第七编之外的命运被明确保留，性别相关分类在其他成文法语境下怎么审，仍是开放问题。Thomas 的协同意见另从医学权威与专家共识的可信度切入，质疑法院对所谓医学共识的倚重，这条线对后续州立法攻防的影响值得单独跟踪。

加州落点单独注明：加州走的是相反方向。2022 年的 SB 107（Stats. 2022, ch. 810）是庇护型立法，限制本州机关配合外州对此类医疗的调查与追责，并把相关安排写进《家庭法典》第 3424 条一带（紧急管辖）等条文。Skrmetti 之后，加州这套保护不因本案自动失效——本案压的是联邦平等保护能给限制型法律多低的审查，不禁止州把保护做高；但在加州州内挑战此类保护法的人，也不能直接从 Skrmetti 推出保护法违宪。两边的诉讼都要回到各自的文本与审查框架里打，这正是联邦制在本案留下的真实图景。

### English

Three limits define the holding. It sets only a federal equal-protection floor: restrictive state laws will generally face rational-basis review in federal court, while states remain free to protect more under their own constitutions and statutes. The parents' substantive-due-process claim was not before the Court and was not decided. And Bostock's reach beyond Title VII was expressly reserved. Justice Thomas concurred separately to question judicial reliance on claimed medical consensus. California sits on the other side of the line: SB 107 (Stats. 2022, ch. 810) is a shield law limiting state cooperation with out-of-state investigations of such care, with related provisions around Cal. Fam. Code sec. 3424 (emergency jurisdiction). Skrmetti does not itself invalidate that framework, nor does it make protective laws unconstitutional; each side must still litigate within its own text and tier of scrutiny.

### Español (初译待校)

Tres límites definen el fallo. Fija solo un piso federal de igual protección: las leyes estatales restrictivas recibirán en general revisión de base racional en tribunales federales, pero los estados pueden proteger más bajo sus propias constituciones. La reclamación de debido proceso de los padres no estaba ante la Corte y no fue decidida. Y el alcance de Bostock fuera del Título VII quedó expresamente reservado. California está en el lado opuesto: la SB 107 (Stats. 2022, ch. 810) es una ley de protección que limita la cooperación estatal con investigaciones de otros estados sobre esta atención médica, con disposiciones conexas en torno a Cal. Fam. Code sec. 3424. Skrmetti no invalida por sí solo ese marco ni vuelve inconstitucionales las leyes protectoras.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

판결의 경계는 세 가지다. 첫째, 연방 평등보호의 최저선만 정한다. 제한형 주법은 연방 법원에서 대체로 합리성 심사를 받지만, 각 주는 주 헌법과 주법에 따라 더 높은 보호를 제공할 수 있다. 둘째, 부모의 실체적 적법절차 주장은 이 사건의 쟁점이 아니었고 판단되지 않았다. 셋째, Bostock의 Title VII 밖 효력은 명시적으로 유보되었다. 캘리포니아는 반대편에 있다. SB 107(Stats. 2022, ch. 810)은 타주의 조사에 대한 주 기관의 협조를 제한하는 보호법으로, Cal. Fam. Code sec. 3424(긴급 관할) 부근에 관련 규정을 둔다. Skrmetti만으로 그 틀이 무효가 되거나 보호법이 위헌이 되는 것은 아니다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Ba giới hạn định hình phán quyết. Nó chỉ đặt ra mức sàn liên bang về bảo vệ bình đẳng: các luật hạn chế của bang nhìn chung sẽ chịu mức xem xét hợp lý tại tòa liên bang, nhưng các bang vẫn có thể bảo vệ cao hơn theo hiến pháp của mình. California đứng ở phía đối diện: SB 107 (Stats. 2022, ch. 810) là luật bảo vệ, hạn chế sự hợp tác của cơ quan bang với các cuộc điều tra của bang khác về loại hình chăm sóc này, với các quy định liên quan quanh Cal. Fam. Code sec. 3424. Riêng Skrmetti không làm mất hiệu lực khung đó, cũng không khiến các luật bảo vệ trở nên vi hiến.
