# Yick Wo v. Hopkins, 118 U.S. 356 (1886) 案例分析：法令中立、执行歧视与平等保护

> 个人学习笔记，不构成法律建议。案情与引文以判决原文为准。

## 一、案情：洗衣店许可与一边倒的拒绝

### 中文
19 世纪 80 年代的旧金山，大量洗衣店由华人经营，多为木结构建筑。市政当局通过条例，要求在木建筑内经营洗衣店须先取得监事会 (Board of Supervisors) 许可。Yick Wo 已在同一地点经营洗衣店多年，其建筑经消防与卫生检查符合要求，却被拒绝许可；同期非华人申请者绝大多数获准，他与其他华人经营者则几乎全数被拒。Yick Wo 无证继续经营被定罪罚款，拒绝缴纳后被羁押，遂申请人身保护令 (habeas corpus) 一路上诉到联邦最高法院，案号 118 U.S. 356 (1886)，由 Justice Matthews 执笔全院一致意见。本案要分开看两件事：条例文本本身没有写种族，问题出在执行——同一标准下，一群人几乎全过、另一群人几乎全不过。

### English
In 1880s San Francisco, many laundries were run by Chinese operators and housed in wooden buildings. A city ordinance required Board of Supervisors consent to operate a laundry in a wooden building. Yick Wo had run his laundry at the same location for years, and his premises passed fire and health inspections, yet consent was denied; non-Chinese applicants were almost all approved while he and other Chinese operators were almost all refused. He kept operating, was convicted and fined, refused to pay, was jailed, and petitioned for habeas corpus up to the U.S. Supreme Court: Yick Wo v. Hopkins, 118 U.S. 356 (1886), a unanimous opinion by Justice Matthews. Keep two things apart: the ordinance's text named no race; the defect was in its administration — under one standard, one group passed almost entirely and another failed almost entirely.

### Español (译稿)

Una ordenanza exigía el consentimiento de la Junta de Supervisores para operar una lavandería en un edificio de madera. Yick Wo llevaba años en el mismo local y su establecimiento superaba las inspecciones de bomberos y sanidad, pero le denegaron el consentimiento; los solicitantes no chinos fueron aprobados casi en su totalidad, mientras que él y otros operadores chinos fueron rechazados casi en su totalidad. Siguió operando, fue condenado y multado, se negó a pagar, fue encarcelado y pidió habeas corpus hasta la Corte Suprema: Yick Wo v. Hopkins, 118 U.S. 356 (1886), opinión unánime del juez Matthews. Distinga dos planos: el texto de la ordenanza no mencionaba raza alguna; el vicio estaba en su aplicación —con un mismo estándar, un grupo pasaba casi entero y el otro caía casi entero.

## 二、判旨：以不平等的眼执行，就是拒绝平等保护

### 中文
法院的推理分三步。第一，第十四修正案的平等保护与正当程序用语是「任何人」(any person)，不限于公民——Yick Wo 虽为中国臣民，在美合法居留，同样受保护。第二，条例授予的裁量本身近乎不受约束：许可没有客观标准，监事会可以任意准驳，这种结构为歧视性执行敞开了门。第三，也是最著名的一步：即使法令在表面上是公正的 (fair on its face)，如果它被以「不平等的眼与手」(with an evil eye and an unequal hand) 执行，使实际效果只落在特定人群身上，仍然是对平等正义的拒绝，违反平等保护。统计对比在这里就是证据本身：约两百名华人申请者几乎无一获准、非华人申请者中只有极个别人被拒，法院认为这种分布除了种族歧视无法解释。结论是撤销羁押依据，申请人获释。

### English
The reasoning has three steps. First, the Fourteenth Amendment speaks of "any person" in its Due Process and Equal Protection Clauses — not citizens only — so Yick Wo, a subject of China lawfully residing in the United States, was protected. Second, the discretion the ordinance conferred was nearly unbounded: consent had no objective standards, so the Board could grant or refuse at will, a structure inviting discriminatory administration. Third — the famous step — even a law "fair on its face" violates equal protection if it is applied "with an evil eye and an unequal hand" so that its practical effect falls on one class alone. The statistics were themselves the proof: of roughly two hundred Chinese applicants almost none were approved, while among non-Chinese applicants only a handful were denied — a distribution the Court found inexplicable except by race. The detention therefore could not stand, and the petitioners were discharged.

### Español (译稿)
El razonamiento tiene tres pasos. Primero, la Decimocuarta Enmienda habla de «toda persona» (any person) en sus cláusulas de debido proceso e igual protección, no solo de ciudadanos: Yick Wo, súbdito chino residente legal, estaba protegido. Segundo, la discreción que confería la ordenanza era casi ilimitada: el consentimiento carecía de estándares objetivos, de modo que la Junta podía conceder o denegar a voluntad, estructura que invitaba a una aplicación discriminatoria. Tercero, el paso célebre: aunque una norma sea «justa en su faz» (fair on its face), si se aplica «con ojo maligno y mano desigual» (with an evil eye and an unequal hand) de modo que su efecto práctico recaiga sobre una sola clase, niega la igual protección. Las estadísticas eran la propia prueba: de unos doscientos solicitantes chinos casi ninguno fue aprobado, y entre los no chinos solo unos pocos fueron denegados, distribución que la Corte consideró inexplicable salvo por raza. La detención no podía sostenerse y los peticionarios fueron liberados.

## 三、后世地位：选择性执法的祖型与证明难题

### 中文
Yick Wo 是三条线的源头。其一，文本中立不等于合宪执行：后来的差别影响与选择性执法 (selective enforcement) 主张都从这里出发，执法结果的分布可以反推目的。其二，它和 Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977) 接得上：Arlington Heights 列出的歧视目的证据因素（影响的悬殊分布、历史背景、程序偏离等）正是 Yick Wo 方法的成文化。其三，它也有边界：McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987) 在死刑语境拒绝仅凭全州统计推定个案歧视目的，要求证明本案决策者的目的——可见 Yick Wo 式的「分布即证据」在执法裁量极端悬殊时最有力，到了个案归因就被收紧。备考答题要同时给出这一张一弛：原则看 Yick Wo，证明门槛看 McCleskey 与 Arlington Heights。另注意程序姿态：本案经人身保护令攻击州定罪的羁押，是联邦法院审查州刑事羁押的早期通道，与后来的 28 U.S.C. § 2254 制度隔着一个多世纪的演变，不要把现代穷尽救济规则倒读回 1886 年。

### English
Yick Wo originates three lines. First, a neutral text is not the same as constitutional administration: later disparate-impact and selective-enforcement claims start here, because the distribution of enforcement outcomes can prove purpose. Second, it connects directly to Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977), whose evidentiary factors for discriminatory purpose — stark statistical impact, historical background, departures from procedure — codify the Yick Wo method. Third, it has limits: McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987), refused in the capital context to infer an individual discriminatory purpose from statewide statistics alone, demanding proof about the decisionmakers in the defendant's own case. So the distribution-as-proof move is strongest where administrative disparity is extreme, and narrows at individual attribution. On an exam, give both the reach and the limit: the principle from Yick Wo, the proof thresholds from Arlington Heights and McCleskey. Note also the posture: habeas attacking detention under a state conviction was an early channel for federal review, a century of development away from modern 28 U.S.C. § 2254 exhaustion doctrine — do not read today's rules back into 1886.

### Español (譯稿)
Yick Wo origina tres líneas. Primera: un texto neutral no equivale a aplicación constitucional; las posteriores demandas por impacto dispar y aplicación selectiva (selective enforcement) nacen aquí, porque la distribución de resultados puede probar el propósito. Segunda: enlaza con Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977), cuyos factores probatorios del propósito discriminatorio —impacto estadístico marcado, contexto histórico, desviaciones procedimentales— codifican el método de Yick Wo. Tercera: tiene límites; McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987) se negó, en contexto de pena capital, a inferir un propósito individual solo de estadísticas estatales, exigiendo prueba sobre los decisores del propio caso. La distribución como prueba es más fuerte donde la disparidad administrativa es extrema y se estrecha en la atribución individual. En examen dé alcance y límite: principio de Yick Wo, umbrales de Arlington Heights y McCleskey. Y note la postura procesal: el habeas contra una detención estatal fue una vía temprana de revisión federal, a un siglo de distancia de la doctrina moderna de agotamiento del 28 U.S.C. § 2254; no proyecte las reglas actuales sobre 1886.

### 한국어 (초역 · 初译待校)
Yick Wo는 세 흐름의 원점입니다. 첫째, 문언이 중립적이라고 집행까지 합헌인 것은 아닙니다. 이후의 차별적 영향과 선택적 집행(selective enforcement) 주장이 여기서 출발하며, 집행 결과의 분포가 목적을 입증할 수 있습니다. 둘째, Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977)와 직결됩니다. 그 판결이 열거한 차별 목적의 증거 요소(현저한 통계적 영향, 역사적 배경, 절차 이탈)는 Yick Wo 방법의 성문화입니다. 셋째, 한계도 있습니다. McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987)는 사형 맥락에서 주 전체 통계만으로 개별 차별 목적을 추론하기를 거부하고, 해당 사건 결정권자에 대한 증명을 요구했습니다. 분포 자체를 증거로 삼는 논법은 행정적 격차가 극단적일 때 가장 강하고, 개별 귀속 단계에서는 좁아집니다. 시험에서는 원칙은 Yick Wo, 증명 문턱은 Arlington Heights와 McCleskey로 함께 제시하십시오. 절차적 자세도 중요합니다. 주 유죄판결에 따른 구금을 인신보호로 공격한 것은 연방 심사의 초기 통로이며, 현대 28 U.S.C. § 2254의 소진 법리와는 한 세기의 거리가 있으니 오늘 규칙을 1886년으로 소급해 읽지 마십시오. (초역·待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ · 初译待校)
Yick Wo là nguồn của ba dòng. Thứ nhất, văn bản trung lập không đồng nghĩa thi hành hợp hiến: các khiếu nại sau này về tác động khác biệt và thi hành chọn lọc (selective enforcement) đều xuất phát từ đây, vì phân bố kết quả thi hành có thể chứng minh mục đích. Thứ hai, nó nối trực tiếp với Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977), mà các yếu tố bằng chứng về mục đích phân biệt — tác động thống kê rõ rệt, bối cảnh lịch sử, lệch khỏi thủ tục — chính là phương pháp Yick Wo được hệ thống hóa. Thứ ba, nó có giới hạn: McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987) từ chối, trong bối cảnh tử hình, suy ra mục đích phân biệt cá nhân chỉ từ thống kê toàn bang, đòi bằng chứng về người ra quyết định trong chính vụ án. Lập luận «phân bố là bằng chứng» mạnh nhất khi chênh lệch hành chính cực đoan, và hẹp lại ở bước quy cho cá nhân. Khi làm bài hãy nêu cả tầm và giới hạn: nguyên tắc từ Yick Wo, ngưỡng chứng minh từ Arlington Heights và McCleskey. Cũng lưu ý tư thế tố tụng: habeas tấn công việc giam giữ theo bản án bang là kênh sớm của xét lại liên bang, cách học thuyết cạn kiệt biện pháp hiện đại của 28 U.S.C. § 2254 cả một thế kỷ — đừng đọc ngược quy tắc hôm nay vào năm 1886. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद · 初译待校)
Yick Wo तीन धाराओं का मूल है। पहली: तटस्थ पाठ का अर्थ संवैधानिक क्रियान्वयन नहीं; बाद के भिन्न-प्रभाव और चयनात्मक प्रवर्तन (selective enforcement) के दावे यहीं से शुरू होते हैं, क्योंकि प्रवर्तन परिणामों का वितरण उद्देश्य सिद्ध कर सकता है। दूसरी: यह सीधे Village of Arlington Heights v. Metropolitan Housing, 429 U.S. 252 (1977) से जुड़ता है, जिसके भेदभावपूर्ण उद्देश्य के साक्ष्य-कारक — स्पष्ट सांख्यिकीय प्रभाव, ऐतिहासिक पृष्ठभूमि, प्रक्रिया से विचलन — Yick Wo पद्धति का संहिताकरण हैं। तीसरी: इसकी सीमाएँ हैं; McCleskey v. Kemp, 481 U.S. 279 (1987) ने मृत्युदंड संदर्भ में केवल राज्यव्यापी आँकड़ों से व्यक्तिगत भेदभावपूर्ण उद्देश्य अनुमानित करने से इनकार किया और स्वयं के मामले के निर्णयकर्ताओं के बारे में प्रमाण माँगा। वितरण-ही-प्रमाण वाला तर्क वहाँ सबसे मजबूत है जहाँ प्रशासनिक असमानता चरम हो, और व्यक्तिगत आरोपण पर संकीर्ण हो जाता है। परीक्षा में विस्तार और सीमा दोनों दें: सिद्धांत Yick Wo से, प्रमाण-दहलीज Arlington Heights और McCleskey से। प्रक्रियात्मक स्थिति भी नोट करें: राज्य दोषसिद्धि के तहत कैद पर habeas आक्रमण संघीय पुनरावलोकन का प्रारंभिक मार्ग था, आधुनिक 28 U.S.C. § 2254 समाप्ति-सिद्धांत से एक शताब्दी दूर — आज के नियमों को 1886 में वापस न पढ़ें। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
