# United States v. Sokolow（1989）案例分析：毒品信使侧写与合理怀疑的总量检验

> 事实依据：United States v. Sokolow, 490 U.S. 1 (1989)，最高法院 7 比 2，Rehnquist 首席大法官执笔多数意见；Terry v. Ohio, 392 U.S. 1 (1968)；Florida v. Royer, 460 U.S. 491 (1983)。本篇拆判决本身，同题常识篇管机场与街头盘查的自查口径，两篇以指针分工。

## 一、案情：一组单看都合法的事实

### 中文主文

1984 年，Sokolow 在檀香山机场被缉毒人员拦下。探员注意到的事实包括：他用 2,100 美元现金（从一卷 20 美元钞票里）买了两张往返旧金山的机票；票面名字与他电话登记的名字不一致；往返行程很短、在以毒品来源地闻名的城市只停留约 48 小时；没有托运行李；衣着与表现显得紧张。探员拦下盘问后请缉毒犬嗅闻其行李，犬只示警，随后取得搜查令并查获毒品。争点只有一个：拦下的那一刻，探员有没有第四修正案要求的合理怀疑（reasonable suspicion）。

声明：个人学习笔记，不构成法律/税务/医疗建议。

### English

In 1984, agents stopped Sokolow at Honolulu airport. The facts they relied on included: he paid $2,100 in cash, from a roll of $20 bills, for two round-trip tickets to San Francisco; the name on the tickets differed from the name on his telephone listing; the trip was short, with only about 48 hours in a city known as a drug source city; he had no checked luggage; and he appeared nervous. After the stop, a drug dog alerted on his luggage, agents obtained a warrant, and drugs were found. The only issue was whether, at the moment of the stop, the agents had the reasonable suspicion the Fourth Amendment requires.

## 二、判决：侧写不是清单打分，而是总量判断

### 中文主文

最高法院 7 比 2 维持拦查有效。多数意见的要点有三。第一，合理怀疑看的是事实总量（totality of the circumstances），不是把每个因素单独拿出来、发现单看合法就逐个划掉——单看合法的行为凑在一起，可以形成合理怀疑。第二，所谓毒品信使侧写（drug courier profile）不是独立的法律标准，它只是一组因素的概括说法；法院评的仍是具体事实本身。第三，合理怀疑的门槛明显低于优势证据，更低于相当理由（probable cause）；本案事实组合已过门槛。异议则担心：这些因素能套住太多无辜旅客，总量口径会把门槛稀释掉。

声明：个人学习笔记，不构成法律/税务/医疗建议。

### English

The Supreme Court upheld the stop, 7 to 2. The majority made three points. First, reasonable suspicion is judged on the totality of the circumstances, not by striking each factor one by one because it looks innocent in isolation; conduct innocent in isolation can, in combination, amount to reasonable suspicion. Second, the so-called drug courier profile is not a freestanding legal standard; it is shorthand for a set of factors, and courts still assess the concrete facts. Third, reasonable suspicion is a considerably lower bar than a preponderance, and lower than probable cause; the combination here cleared it. The dissent warned that these factors fit too many innocent travelers and that a totality approach dilutes the threshold.

Note: Personal study notes; not legal, tax, or medical advice.

### Español

La Corte Suprema validó la detención por 7 a 2. La mayoría sostuvo tres ideas. Primero, la sospecha razonable se juzga por la totalidad de las circunstancias, no tachando cada factor por parecer inocente por separado; conductas inocentes por separado pueden, combinadas, fundar sospecha razonable. Segundo, el llamado perfil del correo de drogas no es un estándar jurídico autónomo, sino una forma abreviada de referirse a un conjunto de factores; el tribunal evalúa los hechos concretos. Tercero, la sospecha razonable es un umbral claramente inferior a la preponderancia y a la causa probable; la combinación del caso lo superaba. La disidencia advirtió que esos factores describen a demasiados viajeros inocentes.

## 三、怎么用：答题与实务的落点

### 中文主文

答题时按三步走：先列全部客观事实，再做总量合成（禁止逐项划掉），最后把门槛说清——合理怀疑低于相当理由，拦查时长与手段还须与 Terry 的范围相称。实务上要注意两条边界：侧写因素不能替代具体事实，执法记录里写不出具体事实，总量就无从谈起；种族、国籍等因素不能单独或作为决定性因素支撑拦查，加州法院与联邦第九巡回对此均有否定判例，答题时应单列说明。加州口径：加州宪法第一条第 13 款下的拦查分析与联邦总量检验大体一致。

声明：个人学习笔记，不构成法律/税务/医疗建议。

### English

For exam answers, work in three steps: list all objective facts, synthesize them as a totality (never strike them one by one), and state the threshold precisely—reasonable suspicion is lower than probable cause, and the stop's duration and means must still fit Terry's scope. Two practical limits matter: profile factors cannot replace concrete facts—if the record contains no specific facts, there is no totality to weigh—and race or national origin cannot alone, or as the decisive factor, support a stop; California courts and the Ninth Circuit have both rejected such use, so flag it separately. In California, stop analysis under article I, section 13 of the state constitution broadly tracks the federal totality test.

Note: Personal study notes; not legal, tax, or medical advice.

### Español

En un examen, siga tres pasos: enumere todos los hechos objetivos, sintetice la totalidad (nunca tache uno por uno) y precise el umbral: la sospecha razonable es inferior a la causa probable, y la duración y los medios de la detención deben ajustarse al alcance de Terry. Dos límites prácticos importan: los factores del perfil no sustituyen hechos concretos, y la raza o la nacionalidad no pueden, solas o como factor decisivo, fundar una detención; los tribunales de California y el Noveno Circuito lo han rechazado. En California, el análisis bajo la sección 13 del artículo I de la constitución estatal sigue en lo esencial el examen federal de totalidad.

Nota: Apuntes personales de estudio; no constituyen asesoría legal, fiscal ni médica.
