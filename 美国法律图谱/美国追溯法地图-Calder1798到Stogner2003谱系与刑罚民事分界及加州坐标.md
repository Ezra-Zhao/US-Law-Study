# 美国追溯法地图：Calder 1798 到 Stogner 2003 谱系、刑罚与民事分界版图及加州坐标

> 板块：美国法律图谱｜主题：把追溯法禁令画成一张图——哪条线管国会、哪条线管各州，两个世纪里法院怎样把 Calder 的四类清单守住又收窄，2003 年同一年里 Stogner 与 Smith 怎样把刑罚与民事的分界画死。本篇与《追溯法备考核心》同题成对：备考篇管答题流水线，本篇只管谱系与版图。
## 一、谱系四段：从四类清单到同年双案

### 中文

谱系分四段。第一段，1798 年立清单：Calder v. Bull, 3 U.S. 386 (1798) 的 Chase 四类成为此后所有讨论的坐标纸，禁令文本分置第一条第九款（管国会）与第十款（管各州）。第二段，1937 到 1987 年填第三类：Lindsey v. Washington, 301 U.S. 397 (1937) 的强制刑、Weaver v. Graham, 450 U.S. 24 (1981) 的善行折抵、Miller v. Florida, 482 U.S. 423 (1987) 的量刑指南抬高，逐个把刑罚加大画进第三类。第三段，1990 年收窄第四类：Collins v. Youngblood, 497 U.S. 37 (1990) 把单纯程序变化移出禁令射程。第四段，2003 年同年双案定分界：Stogner v. California, 539 U.S. 607 (2003) 判复活已届满时效的追诉无效，Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003) 判性犯罪登记属民事监管，两案同年、方向相反，合起来才是完整的图。时间账：2003－1798＝205，从立清单到定分界隔了 205 年（本地复算一致）。

### English

Four eras. First, the 1798 checklist: Chase's four categories in Calder v. Bull, 3 U.S. 386 (1798) became the grid paper for everything after, with the ban split between Article I, Section 9 for Congress and Section 10 for the states. Second, filling category three from 1937 to 1987: Lindsey's mandatory penalty, Weaver's gain time, and Miller v. Florida, 482 U.S. 423 (1987) on raised guidelines. Third, narrowing category four in 1990: Collins v. Youngblood, 497 U.S. 37 (1990) moved merely procedural changes outside the ban. Fourth, the twin 2003 cases: Stogner v. California, 539 U.S. 607 (2003) voided revival of expired limitations, while Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003) held registration civil — opposite directions in one year that together complete the map. Arithmetic: 2003 - 1798 = 205 years from checklist to boundary.

## 二、版图四片：按回头加的是什么切

### 中文

版图不按法院切，按回头加的东西切四片。定罪片：行为时无罪、回头入罪，或行为时轻罪、回头重罪，对应 Calder 第一、二类，射程最硬、无衡量余地。刑期片：幅度抬高、强制刑出现、善行折抵削减、指南版本换高，对应第三类，Weaver 与 Peugh v. United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) 是两端——实定规则与咨询性指南都算，看的是更重刑期的实际风险。时钟片：追诉时效，线只有一条——未届满可延长、已届满不可复活，Stogner 把这片的边界钉死。外衣片：登记、通知、资格限制这类挂着民事名字的回头负担，先走 Smith 的两步（立法意图是不是民事、效果是否明显刑罚到足以推翻意图），过不了第一步才进禁令。读图顺序：先问回头加的是定罪、刑期、时钟还是外衣，四片的检验各不相同，混片是读这张图最常见的错法。

### English

The map is cut not by court but by what the retroactive law adds, in four zones. The conviction zone: criminalizing or aggravating, Calder categories one and two, the hardest edge with no balancing. The sentence zone: raised ranges, new mandatory terms, cut gain time, and higher guideline versions, category three, with Weaver and Peugh v. United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) at the two ends — binding rules and advisory guidelines both count, measured by the real risk of a heavier sentence. The clock zone: limitations, with one line only — extension before expiry is allowed, revival after expiry is not, fixed by Stogner. The civil-label zone: registration, notification, and similar retroactive burdens under a civil name, tested first under Smith's two steps of intent and effects. Reading order: ask first whether what was added is a conviction, a sentence, a clock, or a civil label; each zone has its own test, and mixing zones is the most common misreading.

### Español

Zona de pena: rangos elevados, penas obligatorias nuevas, creditos recortados y versiones mas altas de guias, categoria tres, con Weaver y Peugh v. United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) en los dos extremos. Zona del reloj: la prescripcion, con una sola linea: extender antes del vencimiento se permite, revivir despues no, fijado por Stogner. Zona de etiqueta civil: registro, notificacion y cargas retroactivas similares bajo nombre civil, examinadas primero con los dos pasos de Smith, intencion y efectos. Orden de lectura: pregunte primero si lo anadido es una condena, una pena, un reloj o una etiqueta civil; cada zona tiene su propia prueba.

## 三、接口与灰区：邻线分工和没有画死的地方

### 中文

接口三条要分清。其一，与合同条款分工：第一条第十款同一句里并列的合同条款管州法回头损害合同义务，追溯法管回头加重刑罚，同一部州法可能同时踩两条，检验却不同。其二，与剥夺公权法案（bill of attainder）分工：剥夺公权是立法机关不经审判直接对特定人定罪处罚，追溯法是回头改一般规则的时间问题，一个管谁来定罪、一个管规则何时生效。其三，与正当程序分工：民事法律回头适用（税法、经济 regulation）一般不走追溯法，改走正当程序的合理性审查，门槛低得多，不要把两条的强度混写。灰区两块标灰不写死：咨询性指南在 Peugh 之后算到什么程度（显著风险的量化没有统一刻度），以及登记类负担越长越重时 Smith 的效果判断何时翻面（各州登记年限与公开范围差异很大），图上只标方向、不给死线。

### English

Three interfaces. First, the Contracts Clause sits in the same sentence of Article I, Section 10 but governs retroactive impairment of contract obligations, while ex post facto governs retroactive punishment; one state law can trip both, under different tests. Second, bills of attainder: those bar a legislature from convicting named persons without trial — a who-decides question — while ex post facto is a when-rules-take-effect question. Third, due process: retroactive civil laws such as tax and economic regulation generally face only due process rationality review, a far lower bar, and the two intensities must not be merged. Two grey zones stay grey: how far Peugh's significant-risk idea quantifies for advisory guidelines, and when long, heavy registration burdens flip Smith's effects inquiry. The map marks directions there, not dead lines.

### Español

Tres interfaces. Primera, la Clausula de Contratos comparte frase en el Articulo I, Seccion 10 pero rige el dano retroactivo a obligaciones contractuales, mientras la clausula ex post facto rige el castigo retroactivo; una misma ley estatal puede activar ambas con pruebas distintas. Segunda, los bills of attainder prohiben que la legislatura condene a personas nombradas sin juicio, una pregunta de quien decide, mientras ex post facto es una pregunta de cuando rigen las reglas. Tercera, el debido proceso: las leyes civiles retroactivas, como impuestos y regulacion economica, suelen enfrentar solo un examen de racionalidad, mucho mas bajo. Dos zonas grises quedan grises: hasta donde se cuantifica el riesgo significativo de Peugh y cuando las cargas registrales largas y pesadas invierten el analisis de efectos de Smith.

## 四、加州坐标与三步读图法

### 中文

加州坐标三点。第一，州宪法同禁：《加州宪法》第一条第九款与联邦第一条第十款并行禁止追溯法，加州法院审州法时两条可同时引。第二，默认不溯及：《加州刑法典》第 3 条定州刑法典各规定不溯及既往、除非明文声明——这是解释规则，先于宪法判断适用，能用第 3 条挡住的回头适用，不必升到宪法层。第三，Stogner 本身就是加州案：539 U.S. 607 (2003) 打掉的正是加州回头复活儿童性犯罪追诉的立法（原《刑法典》第 803 条第 (g) 款安排），所以加州在这张图上既是坐标原点之一、也是红线的出生地；此后加州延长时效只能往未届满处延长。三步读图法收尾：一定性（回头加的是定罪、刑期、时钟还是民事外衣）、二定片（落到四片中的哪一片、套该片检验）、三定接口（合同、正当程序、剥夺公权三条邻线有没有同时被踩）。按这三步走，两个世纪的判例不会读成一团。

### English

California coordinates, three points. First, a parallel state ban: article I, section 9 of the California Constitution bars ex post facto laws alongside the federal clause. Second, a default against retroactivity: Penal Code section 3 makes the Penal Code prospective unless retroactivity is expressly declared — an interpretive rule that applies before the constitutional question, so retroactive applications stopped by section 3 never need to reach the Constitution. Third, Stogner itself is a California case: 539 U.S. 607 (2003) struck down California's own revival legislation for child sex prosecutions (the former Penal Code section 803(g) scheme), making California both a coordinate and the birthplace of the red line; since then, California may extend limitations only where the period has not yet expired. A three-step reading method closes the map: characterize what was added, place it in its zone and apply that zone's test, then check the three neighboring interfaces — contracts, due process, and attainder.

### Español

Coordenadas de California, tres puntos. Primera, una prohibicion estatal paralela: el Articulo I, Seccion 9 de la Constitucion de California prohibe leyes ex post facto junto a la clausula federal. Segunda, una regla por defecto contra la retroactividad: el Codigo Penal, Seccion 3, hace el Codigo prospectivo salvo declaracion expresa, una regla interpretativa anterior a la pregunta constitucional. Tercera, Stogner es en si un caso de California: 539 U.S. 607 (2003) derribo la propia legislacion californiana que revivia persecuciones por delitos sexuales infantiles, de modo que California es a la vez coordenada y lugar de nacimiento de la linea roja. Metodo de lectura en tres pasos: caracterice lo anadido, ubiquelo en su zona y aplique su prueba, y revise las tres interfaces vecinas: contratos, debido proceso y attainder.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

California निर्देशांक तीन हैं। पहला, समानांतर राज्य प्रतिबंध: California संविधान का अनुच्छेद I, धारा 9 संघीय खंड के साथ पूर्वव्यापी कानून रोकता है। दूसरा, पूर्वव्यापिता के विरुद्ध डिफ़ॉल्ट: दंड संहिता धारा 3 संहिता को भविष्यलक्षी बनाती है जब तक पूर्वव्यापिता स्पष्ट घोषित न हो — एक व्याख्या नियम जो संवैधानिक प्रश्न से पहले लागू होता है। तीसरा, Stogner स्वयं एक California मामला है: 539 U.S. 607 (2003) ने बाल यौन अभियोजनों को पुनर्जीवित करने वाले California के अपने कानून को गिराया, जिससे California निर्देशांक भी है और लाल रेखा का जन्मस्थान भी। तीन-चरण पठन विधि: जोड़े गए को परिभाषित करें, उसे उसके क्षेत्र में रखें और उस क्षेत्र की कसौटी लगाएँ, फिर तीन पड़ोसी इंटरफ़ेस — अनुबंध, उचित प्रक्रिया और attainder — जाँचें।
