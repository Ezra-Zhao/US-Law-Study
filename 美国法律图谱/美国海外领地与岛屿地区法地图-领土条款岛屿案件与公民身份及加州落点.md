# 美国海外领地与岛屿地区法地图：领土条款、岛屿案件与公民身份，及加州落点

美国的法律地图不只画在五十个州上。大西洋和太平洋里还有五块有人居住的海外领地：波多黎各、关岛、美属维尔京群岛、美属萨摩亚、北马里亚纳群岛。它们在美国主权之下，却不是州，也不是外国——宪法权利哪些自动适用、出生的人算不算公民、要不要交联邦所得税、国会有没有表决权，五块地五个答案。这张图按四层拆：宪法底盘（领土条款与岛屿案件）→ 公民身份与自治文件 → 联邦待遇（税、福利、选票、PROMESA）→ 加州落点。

依据：U.S. Const. art. IV, § 3, cl. 2（领土条款）；Insular Cases；8 U.S.C. §§ 1402、1406、1407、1408；48 U.S.C. §§ 1421 起、1541 起、1661、2101 起；I.R.C. §§ 932、933、935

## 一、版图与宪法底盘：领土条款与五块领地的来历

### 中文

有人居住的海外领地共五块：波多黎各、关岛、美属维尔京群岛、美属萨摩亚、北马里亚纳群岛（CNMI），另有一批无人或只有少量驻守人员的离岛（如中途岛、威克岛一带）。权力底盘是宪法第四条第三款第二项的领土条款（Territory Clause）：国会有权处置属于美国的领土与其他财产，并制定一切必要的规则与条例——这是领地法全部问题的总源头。五块领地的取得方式各异：波多黎各与关岛是 1898 年美西战争后《巴黎条约》的割让；维尔京群岛是 1917 年美国向丹麦购得；萨摩亚来自 1900 年与 1904 年当地酋长的割让条约；CNMI 是二战后联合国托管地，经 1975 年签署的《盟约》（Covenant，国会以 Pub. L. 94-241 批准）自愿与美国组成政治联合。取得方式影响了后来自治文件的形态，但不改变共同底盘：领地不是州、不是外国，是主权之下、建制之外的地区——下一节的「未建制」概念由此而来。

### English

There are five inhabited overseas territories: Puerto Rico, Guam, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and the Northern Mariana Islands (CNMI), plus a set of uninhabited or lightly staffed minor outlying islands. The power base is the Territory Clause, U.S. Const. art. IV, section 3, clause 2: Congress may dispose of and make all needful rules respecting territory or other property belonging to the United States — the single source from which every territorial-law question flows. The five arrived differently: Puerto Rico and Guam were ceded by Spain under the Treaty of Paris (1898) after the Spanish-American War; the Virgin Islands were purchased from Denmark in 1917; American Samoa came through treaties of cession signed by local chiefs in 1900 and 1904; and the CNMI, a post-war U.N. trust territory, voluntarily entered political union under the Covenant signed in 1975 and approved by Congress in Pub. L. 94-241. The route in shaped each organic framework, but the shared floor is constant: a territory is neither a state nor a foreign country, but an area under U.S. sovereignty outside the state system — the seed of the "unincorporated" idea taken up next.

## 二、岛屿案件：已建制与未建制，及哪些宪法权利自动跟随

### 中文

定调判例是 1901 年起的一组「岛屿案件」（Insular Cases）。Downes v. Bidwell, 182 U.S. 244 (1901) 区分两种领地：已建制（incorporated）领地被看作走在建州路上，宪法原则上全面适用；未建制（unincorporated）领地只有「根本性」（fundamental）的宪法权利自动适用，其余权利要等国会明文扩展才跟过去。Balzac v. Porto Rico, 258 U.S. 298 (1922) 据此认定：陪审团审判不属于根本性权利，波多黎各居民在本地刑事案件中没有联邦宪法上的陪审团审判权。阿拉斯加、夏威夷当年属已建制，后来成为州；现存五块领地全部属未建制，所以同一部宪法在领地里的「自动适用清单」比在州里短。这套框架一个多世纪以来批评不断：United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022) 中，法院以合理基础审查维持了补充保障收入（SSI）不覆盖波多黎各居民的合宪性，Gorsuch 大法官的协同意见则直斥岛屿案件根基有误、其推理带有帝国与种族色彩、应当推翻——但截至目前，多数意见并未动手，岛屿案件仍是现行法，答题与实务都先按它走、再注明争议。

### English

The framing precedent is the Insular Cases, beginning in 1901. Downes v. Bidwell, 182 U.S. 244 (1901), split territories in two: incorporated territories are treated as on the road to statehood, with the Constitution applying in principle across the board; in unincorporated territories only "fundamental" constitutional rights apply of their own force, and the rest follow only when Congress expressly extends them. Balzac v. Porto Rico, 258 U.S. 298 (1922), applied that logic to hold jury trial non-fundamental, so Puerto Rico residents had no federal constitutional jury-trial right in local criminal cases. Alaska and Hawaii were incorporated territories and later became states; all five current territories are unincorporated, so the list of constitutional provisions that apply automatically is shorter there than in a state. The framework has drawn criticism for over a century: in United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022), the Court applied rational-basis review to uphold the exclusion of Puerto Rico residents from Supplemental Security Income (SSI), while Justice Gorsuch's concurrence called the Insular Cases' foundations wrong and their reasoning tainted by imperial and racial premises that should be overruled — but the majority has not done so, and the Insular Cases remain governing law: work with them first, and flag the controversy.

### Español (初译待校)

El precedente marco son los Casos Insulares, desde 1901. Downes v. Balzac v. Alaska y Hawái fueron territorios incorporados y luego estados; los cinco territorios actuales son no incorporados, así que la lista de normas constitucionales de aplicación automática allí es más corta que en un estado. El marco lleva un siglo de críticas: en United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022), la Corte mantuvo con revisión de base racional la exclusión de los residentes de Puerto Rico del SSI, mientras el voto concurrente de Gorsuch tachó los fundamentos de los Casos Insulares de erróneos y teñidos de imperialismo y racismo, dignos de ser revocados; pero la mayoría no lo ha hecho y siguen siendo derecho vigente. ### 한국어 (初译待校)

틀을 정한 판례는 1901년부터의 섬 사건(Insular Cases)입니다. Downes v. Bidwell, 182 U.S. 244 (1901)는 영토를 둘로 나눴습니다. 편입(incorporated) 영토는 주로 가는 길에 있는 것으로 취급해 헌법이 원칙적으로 전면 적용되고, 미편입(unincorporated) 영토에서는 「근본적(fundamental)」 헌법 권리만 저절로 적용되며 나머지는 의회가 명문으로 확장할 때만 따라갑니다. United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022)에서 대법원은 합리적 근거 심사로 푸에르토리코 주민을 생활보조금(SSI)에서 제외한 것을 합헌으로 유지했지만, Gorsuch 대법관의 동의의견은 섬 사건의 토대가 잘못되었고 그 논증에 제국주의와 인종적 전제가 배어 있어 뒤집어야 한다고 직격했습니다. 그러나 다수의견은 아직 손대지 않았으므로 섬 사건은 현행법입니다. (초역·待校)

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Tiền lệ định khung là nhóm Án lệ Đảo (Insular Cases), từ năm 1901. Downes v. Bidwell, 182 U.S. 244 (1901), chia lãnh thổ thành hai: lãnh thổ đã hợp nhất (incorporated) được coi là đang trên đường thành bang, Hiến pháp về nguyên tắc áp dụng toàn bộ; ở lãnh thổ chưa hợp nhất (unincorporated), chỉ những quyền hiến định «căn bản» (fundamental) mới tự động áp dụng, phần còn lại chỉ theo khi Quốc hội mở rộng rõ ràng. Balzac v. Porto Rico, 258 U.S. 298 (1922), áp dụng logic đó để phán quyền xét xử bởi bồi thẩm đoàn không phải quyền căn bản, nên cư dân Puerto Rico không có quyền hiến định liên bang về bồi thẩm đoàn trong các vụ án hình sự địa phương. Alaska và Hawaii từng là lãnh thổ đã hợp nhất rồi thành bang; cả năm lãnh thổ hiện nay đều chưa hợp nhất, nên danh sách các điều khoản hiến pháp tự động áp dụng ở đó ngắn hơn ở một bang. Khung này bị phê phán hơn một thế kỷ: trong United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022), Tòa dùng rà soát cơ sở hợp lý để giữ nguyên việc loại cư dân Puerto Rico khỏi chương trình SSI, trong khi ý kiến đồng thuận của Thẩm phán Gorsuch gọi nền tảng của Insular Cases là sai lầm và lý luận của chúng nhuốm màu đế quốc và chủng tộc, cần bị lật ngược — nhưng đa số chưa làm vậy, và Insular Cases vẫn là luật hiện hành. ### हिन्दी (初译待校)

ढाँचा तय करने वाला पूर्ववर्ती है 1901 से चला Insular Cases समूह। Downes v. Bidwell, 182 U.S. 244 (1901) ने क्षेत्रों को दो भागों में बाँटा: समाविष्ट (incorporated) क्षेत्रों को राज्य बनने के रास्ते पर माना जाता है और संविधान सिद्धांततः पूरी तरह लागू होता है; असमाविष्ट (unincorporated) क्षेत्रों में केवल «मौलिक» (fundamental) संवैधानिक अधिकार स्वयं लागू होते हैं, बाकी तभी आते हैं जब कांग्रेस स्पष्ट रूप से विस्तार दे। Balzac v. Porto Rico, 258 U.S. 298 (1922) ने इसी तर्क से जूरी विचारण को ग़ैर-मौलिक ठहराया, इसलिए प्यूर्टो रिको के निवासियों को स्थानीय आपराधिक मामलों में संघीय संवैधानिक जूरी-अधिकार नहीं था। अलास्का और हवाई समाविष्ट क्षेत्र थे और बाद में राज्य बने; आज के पाँचों क्षेत्र असमाविष्ट हैं, इसलिए वहाँ स्वतः लागू संवैधानिक प्रावधानों की सूची किसी राज्य से छोटी है। इस ढाँचे की एक सदी से आलोचना हो रही है: United States v. Vaello Madero, 596 U.S. 159 (2022) में न्यायालय ने तर्कसंगत-आधार समीक्षा से प्यूर्टो रिको निवासियों को SSI से बाहर रखने को बरकरार रखा, जबकि न्यायमूर्ति Gorsuch की सहमति-राय ने Insular Cases की नींव को ग़लत और उसकी तर्कणा को साम्राज्यवादी व नस्लीय धारणाओं से दूषित कहकर पलटने योग्य कहा — पर बहुमत ने अभी ऐसा नहीं किया, और Insular Cases वर्तमान कानून बने हुए हैं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, मूल-भाषी समीक्षा लंबित)

## 三、公民身份与自治文件：四块地是公民，萨摩亚是唯一的「国民非公民」

### 中文

在领地出生的人是什么身份，四块与一块的答案不同。波多黎各（8 U.S.C. § 1402）、美属维尔京群岛（§ 1406）、关岛（§ 1407）出生者依成文法是出生即有的美国公民；CNMI 出生者的公民身份写在 1975 年《盟约》第 301 条。唯一的例外是美属萨摩亚：当地出生者依第 1408 条是美国国民（national）而非公民——可以持美国护照、自由入境美国、在各州居留工作，但不能在联邦或州选举中投票，部分联邦职位与州执照资格亦受限制。Fitisemanu v. United States, 1 F.4th 862 (10th Cir. 2021) 拒绝把第十四修正案的出生公民权扩展到萨摩亚，联邦最高法院 2022 年拒绝调卷，现行口径不变；也要注意，萨摩亚本地舆论对改变现状多有保留，担心冲击 fa'a Samoa 的土地保有与血统（matai）制度，所以这不是一个只等法院点头的单向议题。自治文件各表一枝：波多黎各经 1950 年 Pub. L. 600 授权自行制定宪法（1952 年生效，「自由邦 / Commonwealth」的称谓由此而来）；关岛看 1950 年《关岛组织法》（48 U.S.C. § 1421 起）；维尔京群岛看 1954 年《修订组织法》（48 U.S.C. § 1541 起）；萨摩亚没有国会组织法，依割让条约与 1929 年国会追认（48 U.S.C. § 1661）由内政部监督、按本地宪法自治；CNMI 的政治地位直接写在《盟约》里。

### English

Those born in Puerto Rico (8 U.S.C. section 1402), the U.S. Virgin Islands (section 1406) and Guam (section 1407) are statutory U.S. citizens at birth; citizenship for those born in the CNMI is written into section 301 of the 1975 Covenant. The sole exception is American Samoa: a person born there is, under section 1408, a U.S. national but not a citizen — entitled to a U.S. passport, free entry, and residence and work anywhere in the United States, but barred from voting in federal and state elections and restricted in some federal jobs and licenses. Fitisemanu v. United States, 1 F.4th 862 (10th Cir. 2021), declined to extend Fourteenth Amendment birthright citizenship to Samoa, and the Supreme Court denied certiorari in 2022, leaving the rule in place; note too that Samoan opinion itself is largely cautious about change, fearing pressure on fa'a Samoa land tenure and the matai system, so this is not a one-way issue awaiting only a court's nod. The organic documents differ by territory: Puerto Rico drafted its own constitution under Pub. L. 600 (1950), effective 1952 — the source of the "Commonwealth / Estado Libre Asociado" label; Guam is governed by the Guam Organic Act of 1950 (48 U.S.C. section 1421 et seq.); the Virgin Islands by the Revised Organic Act of 1954 (48 U.S.C. section 1541 et seq.); American Samoa has no congressional organic act and is administered under the Interior Department per the cession treaties and Congress's 1929 ratification (48 U.S.C. section 1661), with a local constitution; and the CNMI's political status is written directly into the Covenant.

## 四、联邦待遇：税、福利、选票与波多黎各的 PROMESA 财政专章

### 中文

税先看来源：领地居民就其领地来源收入，一般不缴联邦所得税，改按领地本地税制纳税——波多黎各看《国内收入法典》§ 933、维尔京群岛看 § 932、关岛与 CNMI 看 § 935 的「镜像」安排（本地税法大体镜像联邦法典、收入归领地财政）；但美国本土来源收入照常进入联邦课税，搬到领地住不等于全球免税。选票算例最能说明地位（本地复算一致）：总统大选的选举人团总票＝众议院席位 435＋参议院席位 100＋哥伦比亚特区依第二十三修正案享有的 3＝538，而五块领地的贡献是 0——领地居民在领地居住期间不能投总统票，国会里只有没有全体会议表决权的驻地专员（波多黎各）与代表（其余四地），只能在委员会里参与；同一居民迁居到任一州并完成登记，即与其他州民完全同权。福利与联邦计划常给领地另设一档：如 SSI 不覆盖波多黎各（见第二节 Vaello Madero）、Medicaid 对领地是封顶拨款而非州式的开放匹配，逐个计划看条文，不能按州的口径推。波多黎各另有财政专章：债务危机后，国会 2016 年通过 PROMESA（48 U.S.C. § 2101 起），设财政监督与管理委员会主导债务重组与预算监督；该委员会委员由总统任命而未经参议院同意的合宪性，在 Financial Oversight and Management Board v. Aurelius Investment, LLC, 590 U.S. 448 (2020) 中被维持——法院认定委员属行使地方权力的官员，不受联邦官员任命条款的约束。

### English

Tax turns on source: residents of a territory generally owe no federal income tax on territory-source income and are taxed instead under the local system — Puerto Rico under I.R.C. section 933, the Virgin Islands under section 932, and Guam and the CNMI under the "mirror" arrangement of section 935, under which the local code largely mirrors the federal one and the revenue stays with the territory; U.S.-mainland-source income remains fully taxable federally, so moving to a territory is not worldwide exemption. The voting arithmetic states the status most plainly (recomputed and consistent): the Electoral College totals House seats 435 + Senate seats 100 + the District of Columbia's 3 under the Twenty-Third Amendment = 538, and the five territories contribute 0 — residents voting from a territory cast no presidential vote, and their voice in Congress is a Resident Commissioner (Puerto Rico) or Delegates (the other four) with no vote on the House floor, though they participate in committees; the same resident who relocates to any state and registers votes on fully equal terms. Federal benefit programs frequently set territories on a separate track: SSI does not cover Puerto Rico (see Vaello Madero above), and Medicaid funding for territories is a capped allotment rather than the states' open-ended match — check each program's statute rather than assuming state treatment. Puerto Rico has one more chapter, fiscal: after its debt crisis, Congress enacted PROMESA in 2016 (48 U.S.C. section 2101 et seq.), creating a Financial Oversight and Management Board to lead debt restructuring and budget supervision; the constitutionality of its presidentially appointed, non-Senate-confirmed members was upheld in Financial Oversight and Management Board v. Aurelius Investment, LLC, 590 U.S. 448 (2020), on the ground that the members are officers exercising local power, outside the federal Appointments Clause.

## 五、加州落点与使用这张图的方法

### 中文

加州没有「领地法」这一层，本图与加州的接触面只在人动起来之后：其一，领地居民迁入加州，从迁入之日起按《收入与税法典》（Revenue and Taxation Code）§ 17014 的居民定义判断州税居民身份、在领地期间的联邦待遇问题不因迁入而改写，仍归联邦与领地法处理；其二，在加州的法院与行政程序里遇到领地问题（例如当事人在关岛取得的执照、波多黎各法院的判决），处理框架仍是联邦法与领地组织文件，加州法只提供程序通道，判决承认等问题另按完全诚实信用与相关成文法逐项看；其三，加州居民反向迁往领地工作，联邦税上的 § 933／§ 935 排除与领地税申报要在动身前一年就规划，落地后再补往往来不及。用图口诀：先定位（哪块领地、已建制还是未建制——现存五块都是未建制）、再分层（权利看岛屿案件、身份看 § 1402 至 § 1408 与《盟约》、自治看组织文件、待遇看具体计划条文）、最后落加州（只管迁入之后的事）。一句话收束：领地法的一切特殊性，都从「主权之内、州体系之外」这八个字长出来。

### English

California has no territorial-law layer of its own; this map touches California only once people move. First, a territory resident who relocates to California is judged for state tax residence from the move under the residence definition of Revenue and Taxation Code section 17014, and the federal treatment of the territory years is not rewritten by the move — it stays with federal and territorial law. Second, when a California court or agency meets a territorial question (a license obtained in Guam, a judgment from a Puerto Rico court), the governing frame remains federal law and the territory's organic documents, with California law supplying procedure only, and recognition questions handled item by item under full faith and credit and the relevant statutes. Third, a California resident moving the other way for work should plan the section 933 / section 935 exclusions and territorial filings a year before departure; fixing them after landing is usually too late. A mnemonic for the map: locate first (which territory, incorporated or not — all five today are unincorporated), then layer (rights through the Insular Cases, status through sections 1402 to 1408 and the Covenant, self-government through the organic acts, treatment through each program's statute), and only then land in California (which governs only what happens after the move). In one line: every peculiarity of territorial law grows out of eight words — within the sovereignty, outside the state system.

Một dòng kết: mọi nét đặc thù của luật lãnh thổ đều mọc ra từ «ở trong chủ quyền, ở ngoài hệ thống các bang». (Bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

कैलिफ़ोर्निया की अपनी कोई «क्षेत्रीय कानून» परत नहीं है; यह नक्शा कैलिफ़ोर्निया को तभी छूता है जब लोग आते-जाते हैं। पहला, क्षेत्र से कैलिफ़ोर्निया आया निवासी स्थानांतरण से Revenue and Taxation Code § 17014 की निवासी-परिभाषा से राज्य कर-निवास के लिए जाँचा जाता है, और क्षेत्र वाले वर्षों का संघीय व्यवहार उस स्थानांतरण से फिर नहीं लिखा जाता — वह संघीय व क्षेत्रीय कानून में रहता है। दूसरा, जब कैलिफ़ोर्निया का कोई न्यायालय या अभिकरण क्षेत्रीय प्रश्न से मिलता है (गुआम में लिया लाइसेंस, प्यूर्टो रिको न्यायालय का निर्णय), तो शासी ढाँचा संघीय कानून और उस क्षेत्र के संगठनात्मक दस्तावेज़ ही रहते हैं, कैलिफ़ोर्निया कानून केवल प्रक्रिया देता है, और मान्यता के प्रश्न full faith and credit व संबंधित कानूनों से मदवार देखे जाते हैं। तीसरा, उलटी दिशा में काम के लिए जाने वाला कैलिफ़ोर्निया निवासी §§ 933 व 935 के अपवर्जन और क्षेत्रीय कर-दाखिलों की योजना प्रस्थान से एक वर्ष पहले बनाए; पहुँचकर सुधारना प्रायः देर कर देता है। नक्शे का स्मरण-सूत्र: पहले स्थान तय करें (कौन सा क्षेत्र, समाविष्ट या असमाविष्ट — आज पाँचों असमाविष्ट हैं), फिर परतें अलग करें (अधिकार Insular Cases से, स्थिति §§ 1402 से 1408 व Covenant से, स्वशासन संगठन अधिनियमों से, व्यवहार हर कार्यक्रम के कानून से), और अंत में कैलिफ़ोर्निया पर आएँ (जो केवल स्थानांतरण के बाद की बात देखता है)। एक पंक्ति में: क्षेत्रीय कानून की हर विशेषता «संप्रभुता के भीतर, राज्य-व्यवस्था के बाहर» से उगती है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, मूल-भाषी समीक्षा लंबित)
