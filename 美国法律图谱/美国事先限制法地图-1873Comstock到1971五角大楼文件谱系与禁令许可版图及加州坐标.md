# 美国事先限制法地图：1873 年 Comstock 背景到 1971 年五角大楼文件谱系、禁令与许可版图及加州坐标

分工：仓内已有 Near 与五角大楼文件两篇案例分析、公开审判准入一篇图谱，本篇只画事先限制（prior restraint）整幅地图：谱系怎么长出来、版图分几片、与事后处罚和内容审查怎么接口。与本轮备考篇《事先限制备考核心》成对：备考给答题框架，本篇给谱系与坐标。判例均写明案名、年份与法院（均为美国联邦最高法院），数字本地复算。

## 中文主文

### 一、谱系四段：从邮政扣押的旧世界，到沉重推定定版

第一段，旧世界（19 世纪）：事先审查是常态——印刷许可的记忆写在第一修正案的反面，联邦层面 1873 年《Comstock 法》以邮政通道管制淫秽品流通，手段多是扣押与禁运，先拦再说。第二段，定版（1931）：Near v. Minnesota, 283 U.S. 697 (1931) 第一次把事先限制整体放上宪法秤——明尼苏达以「公共妨害」为名用禁令封杀整份刊物，法院判该法违宪，立下沉重推定与战时窄例外，距今 2026 − 1931 ＝ 95 年。第三段，展开（1938–1965）：许可线逐格成型——Lovell v. City of Griffin, 303 U.S. 444 (1938) 打掉发传单的市政许可、Freedman v. Maryland, 380 U.S. 51 (1965) 给审查制度立程序三件套，两案相距 1965 − 1938 ＝ 27 年；同期 Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) 把非正式施压纳入版图。第四段，顶点与收边（1967–2004）：Walker v. City of Birmingham, 388 U.S. 307 (1967) 立附带后果、New York Times Co. v. United States, 403 U.S. 713 (1971) 在国家安全语境守住推定（距 Near 正好 40 年，1971 − 1931 ＝ 40）；此后 FW/PBS, Inc. v. City of Dallas, 493 U.S. 215 (1990) 与 City of Littleton v. Z.J. Gifts D-4, L.L.C., 541 U.S. 774 (2004) 只在许可程序的刻度上收边，推定本身未动。

### 二、版图三片：禁令地、许可地、施压地

第一片，禁令地：法院以命令封出版、封发行，Near 与五角大楼文件案是两块界碑；进入条件是命令落在发表之前，救济走紧急上诉与暂缓，附带后果原则（Walker）决定能不能先发再说——答案是原则上不能。第二片，许可地：出版、放映、游行、特种营业先证后行，审查两把尺——标准须窄而客观（Lovell、Shuttlesworth v. City of Birmingham, 394 U.S. 147 (1969)），程序须合 Freedman 三件套（审查者限期起诉或放行、迅速司法裁决、政府负举证）。第三片，施压地：无正式命令、只有官方函件、名单与暗示性威胁，Bantam Books 定版看实质——书商因委员会来函而下架，与被禁令下架在宪法上同格。三片的共同底盘只有一条：政府在发表之前动手，举证责任即翻转到政府一边。

### 三、接口三条：与事后处罚、内容审查、扣押程序怎么分

接口一，事后处罚：诽谤、淫秽、煽动的追诉在发表之后进行，走各自框架（诽谤看 New York Times Co. v. Sullivan, 376 U.S. 254 (1964) 的实际恶意）；Near 的分工逻辑是宁可事后诉、不可事前封。接口二，内容审查：事先限制问「何时拦」，内容型审查问「拦什么」——同一措施两格都进时先走事先限制，推定先行。接口三，扣押程序：查扣出版物库存介于两者之间，Marcus v. Search Warrant, 367 U.S. 717 (1961) 判未审先扣全部库存的程序过不了关，Kingsley Books, Inc. v. Brown, 354 U.S. 436 (1957) 则容许经司法程序后的禁售——分界在有没有先行的司法判断与对全部表达的一网打尽。

### 四、加州坐标：州宪法第二条与地方许可层

加州宪法第 1 条第 2 款第 (a) 项文字比联邦第一修正案更宽，州法院对事先限制同样适用沉重推定（定性口径）。坐标落点有三：其一，市县游行、集会与街头表演许可在各市市政法典，翻车点是标准含混与拖延不决；其二，成人娱乐、按摩、烟草零售等特种许可走 Freedman 程序一线；其三，州层面的出版与发行没有联邦式的出版前审查制度，州的角色主要是事后追诉与许可程序两格。读图口诀：先认是不是发表之前，再分禁令、许可、施压三片，最后才谈内容。

## English

This map traces prior restraint in four stretches. The old world of the nineteenth century treated prepublication control as normal, with the federal Comstock Act of 1873 working through the mails by seizure and exclusion. Near v. Minnesota, 283 U.S. 697 (1931), now 95 years old (2026 − 1931 = 95), constitutionalized the field: a nuisance injunction shutting down a whole periodical failed under a heavy presumption against validity, with only a narrow wartime exception. The licensing line then filled in — Lovell v. City of Griffin, 303 U.S. 444 (1938) against leaflet permits and Freedman v. Maryland, 380 U.S. 51 (1965) imposing procedural safeguards, 27 years apart (1965 − 1938 = 27) — while Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) brought informal official pressure inside the map. Walker v. City of Birmingham, 388 U.S. 307 (1967) added the collateral bar rule, and New York Times Co. v. United States, 403 U.S. 713 (1971), exactly 40 years after Near (1971 − 1931 = 40), held the presumption in a national-security case; FW/PBS, Inc. v. City of Dallas, 493 U.S. 215 (1990) and City of Littleton v. Z.J. Gifts D-4, L.L.C., 541 U.S. 774 (2004) later adjusted only the licensing-procedure calibration. The territory has three regions — injunctions, licensing, and informal pressure — sharing one foundation: once the government acts before publication, the burden flips to the government. Interfaces: subsequent punishment (libel under New York Times Co. v. Sullivan, 376 U.S. 254 (1964)) proceeds after publication under its own framework; content review asks what is restricted while prior restraint asks when; and seizures of stock sit between the two, with Marcus v. Search Warrant, 367 U.S. 717 (1961) and Kingsley Books, Inc. v. Brown, 354 U.S. 436 (1957) marking the procedural line. California's article I, section 2(a) speech clause is textually broader than the First Amendment, and the live coordinates are local parade permits and specialty-business licensing. *Personal study notes, not legal advice.*

## Español (初译待校)

Este mapa recorre la restricción previa en cuatro tramos. El mundo antiguo del siglo XIX trataba el control previo como algo normal, y la ley federal Comstock de 1873 actuaba por vía postal mediante incautación y exclusión. Near v. Minnesota, 283 U.S. 697 (1931), hoy con 95 años (2026 − 1931 = 95), constitucionalizó el campo: una orden por alteración del orden público que cerraba una publicación entera cayó bajo una fuerte presunción de invalidez, con solo una estrecha excepción bélica. La línea de licencias se completó después —Lovell v. City of Griffin, 303 U.S. 444 (1938) contra los permisos para repartir folletos y Freedman v. Maryland, 380 U.S. 51 (1965) con garantías procesales, a 27 años de distancia (1965 − 1938 = 27)— y Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) incorporó la presión oficial informal al mapa. Walker v. City of Birmingham, 388 U.S. 307 (1967) añadió la barrera colateral, y New York Times Co. v. United States, 403 U.S. 713 (1971), exactamente 40 años después de Near (1971 − 1931 = 40), mantuvo la presunción en un caso de seguridad nacional; FW/PBS, Inc. v. City of Dallas, 493 U.S. 215 (1990) y City of Littleton v. Z.J. Gifts D-4, L.L.C., 541 U.S. 774 (2004) solo ajustaron la calibración procesal de las licencias. El territorio tiene tres regiones —órdenes judiciales, licencias y presión informal— con un fundamento común: cuando el gobierno actúa antes de la publicación, la carga pasa al gobierno. La cláusula de expresión de California, artículo I, sección 2(a), es textualmente más amplia que la Primera Enmienda. (初译待校) *Notas personales de estudio, no asesoría legal.*

## Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Bản đồ này vẽ hạn chế trước theo bốn chặng. Thế giới cũ của thế kỷ XIX coi kiểm soát trước xuất bản là bình thường, và Đạo luật Comstock liên bang năm 1873 vận hành qua đường bưu chính bằng tịch thu và loại trừ. Near v. Minnesota, 283 U.S. 697 (1931), nay đã 95 năm (2026 − 1931 = 95), đã hiến pháp hóa lĩnh vực này: một lệnh cấm theo lý do gây phiền hà đóng cửa toàn bộ một ấn phẩm đã sụp đổ dưới suy đoán nặng về tính vi hiến, chỉ chừa ngoại lệ hẹp thời chiến. Nhánh cấp phép được lấp đầy sau đó — Lovell v. City of Griffin, 303 U.S. 444 (1938) chống giấy phép phát tờ rơi và Freedman v. Maryland, 380 U.S. 51 (1965) đặt ra bảo đảm thủ tục, cách nhau 27 năm (1965 − 1938 = 27) — và Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) đưa áp lực chính thức không chính thức vào bản đồ. Walker v. City of Birmingham, 388 U.S. 307 (1967) thêm quy tắc rào cản phụ, và New York Times Co. v. United States, 403 U.S. 713 (1971), đúng 40 năm sau Near (1971 − 1931 = 40), giữ vững suy đoán trong một vụ an ninh quốc gia; FW/PBS, Inc. v. City of Dallas, 493 U.S. 215 (1990) và City of Littleton v. Z.J. Gifts D-4, L.L.C., 541 U.S. 774 (2004) sau đó chỉ điều chỉnh thang thủ tục cấp phép. Lãnh thổ gồm ba vùng — lệnh cấm, cấp phép và áp lực không chính thức — chung một nền: hễ chính phủ hành động trước khi xuất bản, gánh nặng chuyển sang chính phủ. Điều I, mục 2(a) Hiến pháp California theo văn bản rộng hơn Tu chính án thứ nhất. (初译待校) *Ghi chú học tập cá nhân, không phải tư vấn pháp lý.*

## हिन्दी (初译待校)

यह मानचित्र पूर्व प्रतिबंध को चार खंडों में खींचता है। उन्नीसवीं सदी की पुरानी दुनिया प्रकाशन-पूर्व नियंत्रण को सामान्य मानती थी, और 1873 का संघीय Comstock Act डाक मार्ग से ज़ब्ती और बहिष्करण द्वारा चलता था। Near v. Minnesota, 283 U.S. 697 (1931), अब 95 वर्ष पुराना (2026 − 1931 = 95), ने इस क्षेत्र को संवैधानिक बनाया: पूरे पत्र को बंद करने वाली उपद्रव-निषेधाज्ञा असंवैधानिकता की भारी परिकल्पना के नीचे गिर गई, केवल संकीर्ण युद्धकालीन छूट बची। लाइसेंस रेखा बाद में भरी — Lovell v. City of Griffin, 303 U.S. 444 (1938) पर्चा-परमिट के विरुद्ध और Freedman v. Maryland, 380 U.S. 51 (1965) प्रक्रियात्मक सुरक्षा के साथ, 27 वर्ष के अंतर पर (1965 − 1938 = 27) — और Bantam Books, Inc. v. Sullivan, 372 U.S. 58 (1963) ने अनौपचारिक आधिकारिक दबाव को मानचित्र में शामिल किया। Walker v. City of Birmingham, 388 U.S. 307 (1967) ने collateral bar जोड़ा, और New York Times Co. v. United States, 403 U.S. 713 (1971), Near के ठीक 40 वर्ष बाद (1971 − 1931 = 40), ने राष्ट्रीय सुरक्षा के मामले में परिकल्पना बनाए रखी; FW/PBS, Inc. v. City of Dallas, 493 U.S. 215 (1990) और City of Littleton v. Z.J. Gifts D-4, L.L.C., 541 U.S. 774 (2004) ने बाद में केवल लाइसेंस-प्रक्रिया का पैमाना समायोजित किया। क्षेत्र के तीन भाग हैं — निषेधाज्ञा, लाइसेंस और अनौपचारिक दबाव — और एक साझा आधार: सरकार प्रकाशन से पहले कदम उठाए तो बोझ सरकार पर चला जाता है। California के अनुच्छेद I, धारा 2(a) का वाक् खंड पाठतः प्रथम संशोधन से व्यापक है। (初译待校) *व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन नोट; कानूनी सलाह नहीं।*
