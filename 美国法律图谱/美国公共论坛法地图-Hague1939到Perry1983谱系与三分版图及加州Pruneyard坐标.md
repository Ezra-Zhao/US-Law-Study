# 美国公共论坛法地图：Hague 1939 到 Perry 1983 谱系、三分版图与加州 Pruneyard 坐标

分工：仓内已有事先限制、平等保护等相邻图谱，本篇只画政府财产上言论准入这一张图：谱系从 Hague 的信托名句走到 Perry 的三分定版，再逐格钉边界案，最后标政府言论邻界与加州坐标。与本轮备考篇《公共论坛备考核心》成对：备考给答题框架，本篇给谱系与版图，两篇以指针分工。法条与判例均写明出处，数字本地复算。

## 中文主文

### 一、源头：Hague 1939，街道公园是信托，不是市长的后院

谱系的起点是一场封杀。1937 年前后，新泽西州 Jersey City 市长 Frank Hague 动用市政条例，系统性地阻止产业工会组织（CIO）在公共场所集会、散发传单。Hague v. Committee for Industrial Organization, 307 U.S. 496 (1939)（美国联邦最高法院，距今 2026 − 1939 ＝ 87 年）判市政条例无效。Roberts 大法官的多元意见写下全图的第一句：无论街道与公园的产权名义归谁，它们自古以来即被信托持有、供公众使用，自古即用于集会、交流思想与讨论公共问题——读图时注意，这段话出自多元意见而非整齐的多数意见，但后来被反复引用，成了传统公共论坛的定义源头。这一格的宪法含义是：政府对街道公园是管理人，不是房东；可以为通行与秩序立规矩，不能按内容挑谁能说话。

### 二、定版：Perry 1983，三分法成图

从 Hague 到定版隔了 44 年（1983 − 1939 ＝ 44）。Perry Education Association v. Perry Local Educators' Association, 460 U.S. 37 (1983) 以 5 比 4（White 大法官执笔）把散点判例收成三格：传统公共论坛（街道、公园一类自古开放的场所）、指定公共论坛（政府自愿向表达活动开放的公产，开着期间按传统论坛同标准管辖）、非公共论坛（其余公产，政府可保留给本职用途，限制只需合理且不歧视观点）。本案落在第三格：学区只准签约工会使用校内邮件系统、把 rival 工会挡在门外，法院认定差别待遇出于身份与职能、不是观点，成立。三分法此后四十余年未被推翻，后面的案子都是往格子里钉界碑，而不是重画格子。

### 三、非公共论坛一格的界碑：工作场所、邮局、机场、基地

第三格的边界靠一串案子钉出来。Cornelius v. NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, Inc., 473 U.S. 788 (1985)：联邦慈善联合募捐的工作场所是非公共论坛，政府可以只放慈善类组织进来。United States v. Kokinda, 497 U.S. 720 (1990)：邮局门前人行道（多元意见）是非公共论坛，禁止在邮政场所拉票募捐的规矩合理成立——注意它与街道人行道只隔一条产权与用途线，专为邮政业务的人行道不因「长得像人行道」就升格。International Society for Krishna Consciousness, Inc. v. Lee, 505 U.S. 672 (1992)：机场航站楼是非公共论坛，禁止反复募捐成立；但同日的姊妹案对全面禁止在航站楼内分发传单判不合理——同格之内，限制手段仍要过合理性这一关。更早的 Greer v. Spock, 424 U.S. 828 (1976)（军事基地）与 Adderley v. Florida, 385 U.S. 39 (1966)（监狱外场地）同属此格：财产的本职功能越专门，政府保留用途的权限越大。

### 四、有限论坛一格的界碑：圈内不得挑观点

指定论坛的亚型有限论坛，界碑钉在大学校园。Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches Union Free School District, 508 U.S. 384 (1993) 与 Good News Club v. Milford Central School, 533 U.S. 98 (2001)：学校把课后时段向社区团体开放后，不能独拒带宗教观点的使用者。Rosenberger v. Rector and Visitors of University of Virginia, 515 U.S. 819 (1995)：学生活动费是有限论坛，资助各类学生刊物却独拒宗教刊物，是观点歧视。Christian Legal Society Chapter v. Martinez, 561 U.S. 661 (2010)（距 Rosenberger 2010 − 1995 ＝ 15 年）从反面钉界：注册学生组织的有限论坛里，对所有组织一视同仁的 all-comers 准入条件合理且观点中立，成立。一条线读下来：政府可以划圈（定主题、定使用者类别），不能在圈里按观点挑人；要收紧，只能靠对全圈一视同仁的规则。

### 五、邻界：政府言论这块飞地，论坛图到此为止

版图边缘有一块不归论坛管的飞地：政府自己说话。Pleasant Grove City v. Summum, 555 U.S. 460 (2009)：市政公园里长期陈列的纪念碑是政府言论，市政府可以拒绝再立一块。Walker v. Texas Division, Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc., 576 U.S. 200 (2015)（距 Perry 2015 − 1983 ＝ 32 年）：特种车牌是政府言论，州可以拒绝邦联旗图案。飞地的边界由两案守住：Matal v. Tam, 582 U.S. 218 (2017) 判商标注册不是政府言论——注册簿不是政府在说话，贬损条款按观点歧视无效；Shurtleff v. City of Boston, 596 U.S. 243 (2022) 判波士顿市政厅前的旗杆升旗计划在案情下不是政府言论（市政府对升什么旗几乎不加控制），拒升宗教旗构成观点歧视。辨认飞地看三样：该表达形式历来是否被当作政府发声、公众会把话算在谁头上、政府对内容实际控制多深。

### 六、新场地：互联网只得了个比喻，没得格子

数字时代的新问题是社交平台算哪一格。Packingham v. North Carolina, 582 U.S. 98 (2017) 里 Kennedy 大法官把社交媒体称作「现代公共广场」，但那是修辞与类比，不是分类：该案打掉的是北卡州禁止性罪犯使用社交网站的州法，走的是内容中立限制的中间审查，没有把私人平台判成公共论坛。私人平台的删帖、封号原则上不触发第一修正案（平台不是政府，另见州行动学说图谱）；政府官员在平台上开设的互动空间另论，下级法院曾认定官员账号的回复区可构成指定公共论坛，相关案件后因情事变更被联邦最高法院以无实际意义撤销原判，联邦层至今没有定版答案。读图口径：互联网一格在版图上是留白，不是第四格。

### 七、加州坐标：Pruneyard 双层，联邦关门处州宪法开门

加州在全国版图上的位置很醒目：同一块私人购物中心的空地，联邦图上是关着的，州图上是开着的。联邦线：Lloyd Corp. v. Tanner, 407 U.S. 551 (1972) 与 Hudgens v. NLRB, 424 U.S. 507 (1976) 定版，购物中心不是联邦宪法上的公共论坛，业主可以排除散发传单与劳工纠察者（Marsh 的公司镇逻辑不外扩）。加州线：州宪法第 1 条第 2 款第 (a) 项被读得更宽，Robins v. Pruneyard Shopping Center, 23 Cal.3d 899 (1979)（加州最高法院）判购物中心须容忍请愿签名活动，Pruneyard Shopping Center v. Robins, 447 U.S. 74 (1980)（联邦最高法院）确认州这样要求不违反业主的联邦权利。读图收尾三句：分类先看 Perry 三格，观点歧视各格通杀，政府言论是图外飞地；到了加州，还要在联邦图的结论上再叠一层州宪法。

## English

The map begins with a shutdown: Jersey City's mayor used city ordinances to keep a labor organization from meeting and leafleting in public places, and Hague v. Committee for Industrial Organization, 307 U.S. 496 (1939), now 87 years old (2026 − 1939 = 87), struck the ordinances down — Justice Roberts's plurality wrote that streets and parks, whoever holds title, have immemorially been held in trust for the use of the public. Forty-four years later (1983 − 1939 = 44), Perry Education Association v. Perry Local Educators' Association, 460 U.S. 37 (1983) organized the field into three boxes: traditional public forums, designated public forums governed by the same standards while open, and nonpublic forums where restrictions need only be reasonable and viewpoint-neutral; the school mail system in Perry fell in the third box. The nonpublic boundary was then fixed property by property — a federal charity drive in Cornelius v. NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, Inc., 473 U.S. 788 (1985), a post-office sidewalk in United States v. Kokinda, 497 U.S. 720 (1990), airport terminals in International Society for Krishna Consciousness, Inc. v. Lee, 505 U.S. 672 (1992), with military bases and jail grounds in Greer v. Spock, 424 U.S. 828 (1976) and Adderley v. Florida, 385 U.S. 39 (1966). The limited-forum boundary was fixed on campus: Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches Union Free School District, 508 U.S. 384 (1993), Rosenberger v. Rector and Visitors of University of Virginia, 515 U.S. 819 (1995), and Good News Club v. Milford Central School, 533 U.S. 98 (2001) bar viewpoint exclusions inside a forum the government created, while the even-handed all-comers rule in Christian Legal Society Chapter v. Martinez, 561 U.S. 661 (2010), 15 years after Rosenberger (2010 − 1995 = 15), survived. At the edge of the map sits government speech — park monuments in Pleasant Grove City v. Summum, 555 U.S. 460 (2009) and specialty license plates in Walker v. Texas Division, Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc., 576 U.S. 200 (2015) — fenced in by Matal v. Tam, 582 U.S. 218 (2017) and Shurtleff v. City of Boston, 596 U.S. 243 (2022). The internet received a metaphor, not a box: Packingham v. North Carolina, 582 U.S. 98 (2017) called social media the modern public square while deciding the case on intermediate scrutiny. California is drawn in two layers: federal law after Lloyd Corp. v. Tanner, 407 U.S. 551 (1972) and Hudgens v. NLRB, 424 U.S. 507 (1976) lets shopping-center owners exclude speakers, while Robins v. Pruneyard Shopping Center, 23 Cal.3d 899 (1979), affirmed in Pruneyard Shopping Center v. Robins, 447 U.S. 74 (1980), opens the same ground under the state constitution. *Personal study notes, not legal advice.*

## Español (初译待校)

El mapa comienza con una clausura: el alcalde de Jersey City usó ordenanzas municipales para impedir que una organización sindical se reuniera y repartiera folletos en lugares públicos, y Hague v. Committee for Industrial Organization, 307 U.S. 496 (1939), hoy con 87 años (2026 − 1939 = 87), las anuló: la pluralidad del juez Roberts escribió que las calles y los parques, quienquiera que ostente el título, han sido tenidos desde tiempo inmemorial en fideicomiso para uso del público. Cuarenta y cuatro años después (1983 − 1939 = 44), Perry Education Association v. Perry Local Educators' Association, 460 U.S. 37 (1983) organizó el campo en tres casillas: foros públicos tradicionales, foros públicos designados regidos por los mismos estándares mientras estén abiertos, y foros no públicos donde las restricciones solo deben ser razonables y neutras en cuanto al punto de vista; el sistema de correo escolar de Perry cayó en la tercera casilla. El límite no público se fijó luego propiedad por propiedad: una campaña benéfica federal en Cornelius v. NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, Inc., 473 U.S. 788 (1985), una acera de oficina postal en United States v. Kokinda, 497 U.S. 720 (1990), terminales aeroportuarias en International Society for Krishna Consciousness, Inc. v. Lee, 505 U.S. 672 (1992), con bases militares y terrenos carcelarios en Greer v. Spock, 424 U.S. 828 (1976) y Adderley v. Florida, 385 U.S. 39 (1966). El límite del foro limitado se fijó en los campus: Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches Union Free School District, 508 U.S. 384 (1993), Rosenberger v. Rector and Visitors of University of Virginia, 515 U.S. 819 (1995) y Good News Club v. Milford Central School, 533 U.S. 98 (2001) prohíben exclusiones por punto de vista dentro de un foro creado por el gobierno, mientras la regla equitativa de admisión abierta de Christian Legal Society Chapter v. Martinez, 561 U.S. 661 (2010), 15 años después de Rosenberger (2010 − 1995 = 15), sobrevivió. Al borde del mapa está el discurso gubernamental —monumentos en parques en Pleasant Grove City v. Summum, 555 U.S. 460 (2009) y placas especiales en Walker v. Texas Division, Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc., 576 U.S. 200 (2015)—, cercado por Matal v. Tam, 582 U.S. 218 (2017) y Shurtleff v. City of Boston, 596 U.S. 243 (2022). Internet recibió una metáfora, no una casilla: Packingham v. North Carolina, 582 U.S. 98 (2017) llamó a las redes sociales la plaza pública moderna resolviendo el caso con escrutinio intermedio. California se dibuja en dos capas: el derecho federal tras Lloyd Corp. v. Tanner, 407 U.S. 551 (1972) y Hudgens v. NLRB, 424 U.S. 507 (1976) permite a los centros comerciales excluir oradores, mientras Robins v. Pruneyard Shopping Center, 23 Cal.3d 899 (1979), confirmado en Pruneyard Shopping Center v. Robins, 447 U.S. 74 (1980), abre el mismo terreno bajo la constitución estatal. (初译待校) *Notas personales de estudio, no asesoría legal.*

## Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Bản đồ này bắt đầu bằng một vụ phong tỏa: thị trưởng Jersey City dùng các sắc lệnh thành phố để ngăn một tổ chức lao động họp và phát truyền đơn ở nơi công cộng, và Hague v. Committee for Industrial Organization, 307 U.S. 496 (1939), nay đã 87 năm (2026 − 1939 = 87), đã tuyên các sắc lệnh đó vô hiệu — ý kiến đa số tương đối của Thẩm phán Roberts viết rằng đường phố và công viên, dù ai đứng tên sở hữu, từ thời xa xưa đã được giữ theo ủy thác cho công chúng sử dụng. Bốn mươi bốn năm sau (1983 − 1939 = 44), Perry Education Association v. Perry Local Educators' Association, 460 U.S. 37 (1983) sắp xếp lĩnh vực này thành ba ô: diễn đàn công truyền thống, diễn đàn công được chỉ định chịu cùng tiêu chuẩn chừng nào còn mở, và diễn đàn phi công nơi các hạn chế chỉ cần hợp lý và trung lập về quan điểm; hệ thống thư nội bộ trường học trong vụ Perry rơi vào ô thứ ba. Ranh giới phi công sau đó được cố định theo từng loại tài sản: chiến dịch quyên góp từ thiện liên bang trong Cornelius v. NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, Inc., 473 U.S. 788 (1985), vỉa hè bưu điện trong United States v. Kokinda, 497 U.S. 720 (1990), nhà ga sân bay trong International Society for Krishna Consciousness, Inc. v. Lee, 505 U.S. 672 (1992), cùng căn cứ quân sự và khuôn viên trại giam trong Greer v. Spock, 424 U.S. 828 (1976) và Adderley v. Florida, 385 U.S. 39 (1966). Ranh giới diễn đàn giới hạn được cố định trong trường học: Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches Union Free School District, 508 U.S. 384 (1993), Rosenberger v. Rector and Visitors of University of Virginia, 515 U.S. 819 (1995) và Good News Club v. Milford Central School, 533 U.S. 98 (2001) cấm loại trừ theo quan điểm bên trong diễn đàn do chính phủ tạo ra, trong khi quy tắc mở cho mọi người một cách đều tay trong Christian Legal Society Chapter v. Martinez, 561 U.S. 661 (2010), 15 năm sau Rosenberger (2010 − 1995 = 15), đã đứng vững. Ở rìa bản đồ là phát ngôn của chính phủ — tượng đài trong công viên ở Pleasant Grove City v. Summum, 555 U.S. 460 (2009) và biển số đặc biệt ở Walker v. Texas Division, Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc., 576 U.S. 200 (2015) — được rào lại bởi Matal v. ## हिन्दी (初译待校)

यह मानचित्र एक बंदी से शुरू होता है: Jersey City के महापौर ने नगर अध्यादेशों से एक श्रम संगठन को सार्वजनिक स्थानों पर सभा करने और पर्चे बाँटने से रोका, और Hague v. Committee for Industrial Organization, 307 U.S. 496 (1939), अब 87 वर्ष पुराना (2026 − 1939 = 87), ने उन अध्यादेशों को रद्द किया — न्यायमूर्ति Roberts के बहुलता मत ने लिखा कि गलियाँ और पार्क, स्वामित्व किसी के भी नाम हो, प्राचीन काल से जनता के उपयोग हेतु न्यास में धारित हैं। चौवालीस वर्ष बाद (1983 − 1939 = 44), Perry Education Association v. Perry Local Educators' Association, 460 U.S. 37 (1983) ने इस क्षेत्र को तीन खानों में व्यवस्थित किया: पारंपरिक सार्वजनिक मंच, नामित सार्वजनिक मंच जो खुले रहने तक उन्हीं मानकों से चलते हैं, और ग़ैर-सार्वजनिक मंच जहाँ प्रतिबंधों को केवल उचित और दृष्टिकोण-तटस्थ होना चाहिए; Perry की विद्यालय डाक प्रणाली तीसरे खाने में पड़ी। ग़ैर-सार्वजनिक सीमा फिर संपत्ति-दर-संपत्ति तय हुई — संघीय दान अभियान Cornelius v. NAACP Legal Defense and Educational Fund, Inc., 473 U.S. 788 (1985) में, डाकघर का फुटपाथ United States v. Kokinda, 497 U.S. 720 (1990) में, हवाईअड्डा टर्मिनल International Society for Krishna Consciousness, Inc. v. Lee, 505 U.S. 672 (1992) में, और सैन्य अड्डे व कारागार परिसर Greer v. Spock, 424 U.S. 828 (1976) व Adderley v. Florida, 385 U.S. 39 (1966) में। सीमित मंच की सीमा परिसरों में तय हुई: Lamb's Chapel v. Center Moriches Union Free School District, 508 U.S. 384 (1993), Rosenberger v. Rector and Visitors of University of Virginia, 515 U.S. 819 (1995) और Good News Club v. Milford Central School, 533 U.S. 98 (2001) सरकार-निर्मित मंच के भीतर दृष्टिकोण-आधारित बहिष्कार रोकते हैं, जबकि Christian Legal Society Chapter v. Martinez, 561 U.S. 661 (2010) का समान सर्व-प्रवेश नियम, Rosenberger के 15 वर्ष बाद (2010 − 1995 = 15), टिका रहा। मानचित्र के किनारे सरकारी वक्तव्य है — पार्क स्मारक Pleasant Grove City v. Summum, 555 U.S. 460 (2009) में और विशेष नंबर प्लेट Walker v. Texas Division, Sons of Confederate Veterans, Inc., 576 U.S. 200 (2015) में — जिसकी बाड़ Matal v. Tam, 582 U.S. 218 (2017) और Shurtleff v. City of Boston, 596 U.S. 243 (2022) लगाते हैं। इंटरनेट को खाना नहीं, रूपक मिला: Packingham v. North Carolina, 582 U.S. 98 (2017) ने सोशल मीडिया को आधुनिक सार्वजनिक चौक कहा, परंतु मामला मध्यम जाँच से तय किया। California दो परतों में बनता है: Lloyd Corp. v. Tanner, 407 U.S. 551 (1972) और Hudgens v. NLRB, 424 U.S. 507 (1976) के बाद संघीय विधि शॉपिंग केंद्रों को वक्ताओं को बाहर रखने देती है, जबकि Robins v. Pruneyard Shopping Center, 23 Cal.3d 899 (1979), जिसकी पुष्टि Pruneyard Shopping Center v. Robins, 447 U.S. 74 (1980) में हुई, राज्य संविधान के तहत वही भूमि खोलता है। (初译待校) *व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन नोट; कानूनी सलाह नहीं।*
