# 美国 Apprendi 规则法地图：Apprendi 2000 到 Erlinger 2024 谱系、上限下限罚金死刑版图及加州坐标

> 板块：美国法律图谱｜主题：刑罚的高度由谁说了算——本篇画 Apprendi 规则的地图：第六修正案陪审团审判权要求，除前科以外，凡把刑罚顶过法定最高刑或抬起强制最低刑的事实，都属犯罪要素、须由陪审团以排除合理怀疑认定。本篇与《Apprendi 规则备考核心》同题成对：备考篇管答题流水线，本篇只管谱系与版图。与联邦量刑图谱、死刑图谱分工：量刑图谱管指南体系，本篇管「哪些事实根本不归法官认定」这一条分界线本身。
## 一、谱系：二十四年四步扩张

### 中文

谱系按年份排四步。第一步立规：Apprendi v. New Jersey, 530 U.S. 466 (2000)，美国联邦最高法院 5 比 4，Stevens 大法官执笔——新泽西州恨罪加重把 10 年上限顶到 20 年，法官以优势证据认定偏见目的，判违宪，规则诞生。第二步进口子：Blakely v. Washington, 542 U.S. 296 (2004) 把「法定最高刑」重定义为仅凭陪审团认定事实可判的最高刑，州量刑体制同样受管；United States v. Booker, 543 U.S. 220 (2005) 随即把联邦量刑指南改成咨询性，免得整套指南被掀翻。第三步补地板：Alleyne v. United States, 570 U.S. 99 (2013) 把规则从天花板扩到强制最低刑，推翻 Harris 的旧规。第四步收边与续命：Erlinger v. United States, 602 U.S. 821 (2024) 把前科例外的边收窄——先行犯罪是否发生在不同场合也要陪审团一致认定。时间账：2024－2000＝24，从立规到最近一次收边共 24 年；2013－2000＝13，地板等了 13 年才补上。

### English

The lineage runs in four steps. Step one, the rule is born: Apprendi v. New Jersey, 530 U.S. 466 (2000), 5-4, by Justice Stevens — a New Jersey hate-crime enhancement raised a 10-year ceiling to 20 on a judge's preponderance finding of bias purpose, held unconstitutional. Step two, the definition and the federal settlement: Blakely v. Washington, 542 U.S. 296 (2004) redefined the statutory maximum as the most a judge may impose on jury-found facts alone, reaching state sentencing systems; United States v. Booker, 543 U.S. 220 (2005) then made the federal Guidelines advisory rather than let the whole system fall. Step three, the floor: Alleyne v. United States, 570 U.S. 99 (2013) extended the rule to mandatory minimums, overruling Harris. Step four, narrowing the carve-out: Erlinger v. United States, 602 U.S. 821 (2024) held that whether predicate offenses occurred on different occasions is also for a unanimous jury, trimming the prior-conviction exception. Arithmetic: 2024 - 2000 = 24 years from birth to the latest trimming; 2013 - 2000 = 13 years before the floor was added.

## 二、版图：按被顶动的东西切四片

### 中文

版图按「事实顶动了什么」切四片。上限片：事实把法定最高刑顶高——Apprendi 本案与 Blakely 是正中心，州法把加重情节交给法官优势认定的体制整片变色。下限片：事实把强制最低刑抬起——Alleyne 之后，18 U.S.C. 第 924(c) 条的挥舞、开枪等分档事实都要进起诉书、过陪审团；2013 年之前 Harris 时代法官认定的旧判决，是这片里最大一块存量争议地。死刑片：Ring v. Arizona, 536 U.S. 584 (2002) 定版——使被告具备死刑资格的加重情节是要素，各州死刑程序随之改成陪审团认定加重情节在先、量刑裁量在后。罚金片：Southern Union Co. v. United States, 567 U.S. 343 (2012) 定版——刑事罚金按天累积时，违法天数决定罚金上限，天数归陪审团。四片共用同一把尺：看这个事实有没有改变法律允许的刑罚区间，改变了，它就是要素；只是在区间内部帮法官拿主意的，仍是普通量刑裁量，不在图内。

### English

The map is cut by what the fact moves. The ceiling panel: facts that raise the statutory maximum — Apprendi itself and Blakely sit at the center, and state systems that handed aggravators to judges under a preponderance standard changed color across the whole panel. The floor panel: facts that raise a mandatory minimum — after Alleyne, the tiering facts of 18 U.S.C. section 924(c), such as brandishing or discharging, belong in the indictment and before the jury; pre-2013 Harris-era judge-found minimums are the panel's largest legacy dispute zone. The capital panel: Ring v. Arizona, 536 U.S. 584 (2002) — aggravators that make a defendant death-eligible are elements, and state capital procedure was rebuilt as jury-found aggravators first, sentencing discretion second. The fines panel: Southern Union Co. v. United States, 567 U.S. 343 (2012) — where criminal fines accrue per day, the number of violation days sets the fine ceiling, so the jury finds the days. One measure serves all four panels: if the fact changes the legally authorized punishment range, it is an element; if it only helps the judge choose inside the range, it remains ordinary sentencing discretion and sits outside the map.

## 三、接口与灰区：前科例外、认罪与指南

### 中文

接口三条要分清。第一，前科接口：Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998) 留下的例外让法官可以认定前科，但 Erlinger (2024) 已把「前科发生在不同场合」这类附带问题划回陪审团，例外还剩多宽是当前最热的灰区，图上标灰、不写死。第二，认罪接口：被告认罪或在协商中承认的事实不需要陪审团认定，所以实务中大量加重事实是靠认罪书里的事实基础过关的——接口的开关在被告自己的签字上。第三，指南接口：Booker 之后联邦量刑指南是咨询性的，法官在指南区间内依优势证据认定事实不违反 Apprendi，因为区间不再是法律边界；但法官认定的事实一旦被用来跨过成文法上限或触发强制最低，立刻回到图内。与邻线分工：陪审团一致性（Ramos 线）管裁决怎么形成，本图管哪些问题必须交给陪审团；证明标准（Winship 线）管要素要证到什么程度，本图管什么东西算要素。两条邻线在本图边缘交汇，但不要混读。

### English

Three interfaces must be kept apart. First, the prior-conviction interface: the Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998) carve-out still lets judges find priors, but Erlinger (2024) pulled satellite questions such as different occasions back to the jury; how wide the exception remains is the map's hottest gray zone, marked gray and not written as settled. Second, the plea interface: facts admitted in a guilty plea or plea agreement need no jury finding, so in practice most aggravators pass through the factual basis of a plea — the switch sits in the defendant's own signature. Third, the Guidelines interface: after Booker the federal Guidelines are advisory, so judge-found facts by a preponderance inside the guideline range do not violate Apprendi, because the range is no longer a legal boundary; the moment a judge-found fact is used to cross a statutory ceiling or trigger a mandatory minimum, it is back inside the map. Neighboring lines: the unanimity line (Ramos) governs how a verdict is formed, this map governs which questions must go to the jury; the burden line (Winship) governs how far elements must be proved, this map governs what counts as an element. The lines meet at the edges but should not be read as one.

### Español

Tres interfaces deben separarse. Primera, la de condenas previas: la excepcion de Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998) aun permite al juez hallar condenas previas, pero Erlinger (2024) devolvio al jurado cuestiones satelite como las ocasiones distintas; cuanto queda de la excepcion es la zona gris mas caliente del mapa, marcada en gris y no dada por cerrada. Segunda, la de la declaracion de culpabilidad: los hechos admitidos en un acuerdo no necesitan hallazgo del jurado, y en la practica la mayoria de las agravantes pasa por la base factual del acuerdo — el interruptor esta en la firma del propio acusado. Tercera, la de las Guias: tras Booker las Guias federales son consultivas, asi que los hechos hallados por el juez por preponderancia dentro del rango no violan Apprendi, porque el rango ya no es frontera legal; pero si un hecho asi se usa para cruzar un techo legal o activar una minima obligatoria, vuelve dentro del mapa. Lineas vecinas: la unanimidad (Ramos) gobierna como se forma el veredicto, este mapa que preguntas van al jurado; la carga de prueba (Winship) gobierna hasta donde se prueban los elementos, este mapa que cuenta como elemento.

접점은 셋으로 나누어 보십시오. 첫째 전과 접점입니다. Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998)의 예외로 판사가 전과를 인정할 수 있지만, Erlinger (2024)는 서로 다른 기회 같은 부수 문제를 배심으로 되돌렸습니다. 예외가 얼마나 남았는지는 지금 지도의 가장 뜨거운 회색 지대여서 회색으로 표시하고 확정해 쓰지 않습니다. 둘째 유죄 인정 접점입니다. 유죄 인정이나 협상에서 피고인이 인정한 사실은 배심 인정이 필요 없어, 실무에서 대부분의 가중 사실은 인정서의 사실 기초를 통해 통과합니다. 스위치는 피고인 본인의 서명에 있습니다. 셋째 가이드라인 접점입니다. Booker 이후 연방 가이드라인은 자문적이라, 구간 안에서 판사가 우세 증거로 사실을 인정해도 구간이 법적 경계가 아니므로 Apprendi 위반이 아닙니다. 그러나 그 사실이 성문법 천장을 넘거나 의무 최저형을 발동시키는 데 쓰이는 순간 지도 안으로 돌아옵니다. 이웃 선과 나누면, 만장일치 선(Ramos)은 평결이 어떻게 만들어지는지를, 이 지도는 어떤 문제가 배심에게 가야 하는지를 다스리고, 증명 선(Winship)은 요소를 어디까지 증명할지를, 이 지도는 무엇이 요소인지를 다스립니다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Ba giao dien phai tach rieng. Thu nhat, giao dien tien an: ngoai le cua Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998) van cho tham phan xac dinh tien an, nhung Erlinger (2024) da keo cac cau hoi ve tinh nhu cac dip khac nhau ve cho boi tham doan; ngoai le con rong bao nhieu la vung xam nong nhat cua ban do, danh dau xam va khong viet nhu da chot. Thu hai, giao dien nhan toi: su kien duoc thua nhan trong loi nhan toi hay thoa thuan khong can boi tham doan xac dinh, nen trong thuc te hau het tinh tiet tang nang di qua phan co so su kien cua thoa thuan — cong tac nam o chu ky cua chinh bi cao. Thu ba, giao dien Huong dan: sau Booker, Huong dan lien bang mang tinh tham khao, nen su kien tham phan xac dinh theo uu the chung cu trong khoang khong vi pham Apprendi, vi khoang khong con la ranh gioi phap ly; nhung khi su kien do duoc dung de vuot tran luat dinh hay kich hoat muc toi thieu bat buoc, no lap tuc quay vao ban do. ### हिन्दी (初译待校)

तीन इंटरफ़ेस अलग रखने होंगे। पहला, पूर्व दोषसिद्धि इंटरफ़ेस: Almendarez-Torres v. United States, 523 U.S. 224 (1998) का अपवाद अब भी न्यायाधीश को पूर्व दोष खोजने देता है, लेकिन Erlinger (2024) ने अलग अवसरों जैसे उप-प्रश्न जूरी को लौटा दिए; अपवाद कितना बचा है, यह नक्शे का सबसे गर्म धूसर क्षेत्र है, धूसर चिह्नित है और तय नहीं लिखा गया। दूसरा, दोष-स्वीकृति इंटरफ़ेस: स्वीकारोक्ति या समझौते में माने गए तथ्यों को जूरी-निष्कर्ष नहीं चाहिए, इसलिए व्यवहार में अधिकांश गंभीर तथ्य समझौते के तथ्य-आधार से गुज़रते हैं — स्विच अभियुक्त के अपने हस्ताक्षर में है। तीसरा, दिशानिर्देश इंटरफ़ेस: Booker के बाद संघीय दिशानिर्देश सलाहकारी हैं, इसलिए सीमा के भीतर प्राबल्य से न्यायाधीश-खोजे तथ्य Apprendi का उल्लंघन नहीं करते, क्योंकि सीमा अब कानूनी सरहद नहीं; पर जैसे ही ऐसा तथ्य वैधानिक छत पार करने या अनिवार्य न्यूनतम सक्रिय करने में प्रयुक्त हो, वह नक्शे के भीतर लौट आता है। पड़ोसी रेखाएँ: सर्वसम्मति रेखा (Ramos) फैसले के बनने को नियंत्रित करती है, यह नक्शा उन प्रश्नों को जो जूरी के पास जाने चाहिए; भार रेखा (Winship) तत्वों के सिद्ध होने की सीमा को, यह नक्शा तत्व की परिभाषा को।

## 四、加州坐标与三步读图法

### 中文

加州落点走过一轮完整的「被判违宪—修法—定版」。Cunningham v. California, 549 U.S. 270 (2007)，美国联邦最高法院，判加州确定量刑法的旧结构违宪：三格刑期里选上格要靠法官认定的加重情节，等于法官把上限从中格顶到上格，正中 Apprendi。州法院先在 People v. Sandoval, 41 Cal.4th 825 (2007) 给法官在三格内选格的裁量留出空间，立法机关再于 2021 年通过 SB 567、修改《刑法典》第 1170(b) 条，把规则写进成文法：选上格所依据的加重情节，原则上须由陪审团以排除合理怀疑认定、被告承认、或属前科事实，涉及创伤等特定情形另有法官认定的窄口。以 2026 年回看，Cunningham 距今 2026－2007＝19 年，加州这一格已经从判例法走成了成文法。三步读图法收尾：一问事实顶动的是上限、下限、死刑资格还是罚金上限（先定片）；二问它是不是前科或被告自认（再查接口）；三问落到加州时先查第 1170(b) 条的成文口径、再查联邦地板有没有更高要求。把区间内裁量误读成要素、把前科例外误读成所有附带问题都归法官，是读这张图最常见的两个错法。

### English

California's landing point ran the full cycle of invalidation, repair, and codification. Cunningham v. California, 549 U.S. 270 (2007) held the old structure of California's determinate sentencing law unconstitutional: choosing the upper term on judge-found aggravators was exactly a judge raising the ceiling from the middle term to the upper term, squarely within Apprendi. The state supreme court in People v. Sandoval, 41 Cal.4th 825 (2007) preserved judicial discretion to choose within the triad, and the legislature in 2021 enacted SB 567, amending Penal Code section 1170(b) to write the rule into statute: aggravating circumstances supporting an upper term must generally be jury-found beyond a reasonable doubt, admitted, or prior-conviction facts, with a narrow judge-found lane for specified circumstances such as trauma. From 2026, Cunningham is 2026 - 2007 = 19 years back, and California's square has moved from case law into statute. A three-step reading method closes the map: first ask whether the fact moves a ceiling, a floor, death eligibility, or a fine cap; second ask whether it is a prior conviction or an admission; third, in California, check section 1170(b) first and then whether the federal floor demands more. The two common misreadings are treating within-range discretion as an element and treating the prior-conviction exception as covering every satellite question.

### Español

El punto de California recorrio el ciclo completo de invalidacion, reparacion y codificacion. Cunningham v. California, 549 U.S. 270 (2007) declaro inconstitucional la vieja estructura de la ley de sentencias determinadas: elegir el termino superior con agravantes halladas por el juez era exactamente subir el techo del termino medio al superior, en pleno Apprendi. La corte estatal en People v. Sandoval, 41 Cal.4th 825 (2007) preservo la discrecion judicial dentro de la triada, y la legislatura en 2021 aprobo SB 567, modificando el Codigo Penal, Seccion 1170(b), para escribir la regla en la ley: las circunstancias agravantes del termino superior deben, en general, ser halladas por el jurado mas alla de duda razonable, admitidas o ser condenas previas, con un carril estrecho judicial para supuestos especificados como el trauma. Desde 2026, Cunningham queda a 2026 - 2007 = 19 anos, y la casilla californiana paso de jurisprudencia a estatuto. Metodo de lectura en tres pasos: que mueve el hecho — techo, piso, elegibilidad capital o tope de multa; si es condena previa o admision; y en California, primero la Seccion 1170(b) y luego si el piso federal exige mas.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

캘리포니아의 착지점은 무효, 수리, 성문화의 전 과정을 거쳤습니다. Cunningham v. California, 549 U.S. 270 (2007)은 캘리포니아 확정형법의 옛 구조를 위헌으로 했습니다. 판사가 인정한 가중 사유로 상단을 고르는 것은 곧 판사가 중간단에서 상단으로 천장을 올리는 일이라 Apprendi 한복판에 있었습니다. 주 대법원은 People v. Sandoval, 41 Cal.4th 825 (2007)로 삼단 안에서의 판사 재량을 남겼고, 입법부는 2021년 SB 567로 형법 제1170(b)조를 고쳐 규칙을 성문법으로 썼습니다. 상단의 근거가 되는 가중 사유는 원칙적으로 배심이 합리적 의심의 여지 없이 인정하거나, 피고인이 인정하거나, 전과 사실이어야 하고, 트라우마 등 특정 사정에는 판사 인정의 좁은 길이 있습니다. 2026년에서 보면 Cunningham은 2026 - 2007 = 19년 전 일이고, 캘리포니아 칸은 판례법에서 성문법으로 옮겨 갔습니다. 세 단계 읽는 법으로 마무리합니다. 첫째, 사실이 천장, 바닥, 사형 자격, 벌금 상한 중 무엇을 움직이는지, 둘째, 전과이거나 피고인의 인정인지, 셋째, 캘리포니아에서는 제1170(b)조의 성문 기준을 먼저 보고 연방 하한이 더 요구하는지 확인합니다.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

California का बिंदु अमान्यकरण, मरम्मत और संहिताकरण का पूरा चक्र चला। Cunningham v. California, 549 U.S. 270 (2007) ने California के निर्धारित दंड कानून की पुरानी संरचना को असंवैधानिक ठहराया: न्यायाधीश-खोजी गंभीर परिस्थितियों पर ऊपरी अवधि चुनना ठीक वही था — न्यायाधीश द्वारा छत को मध्य अवधि से ऊपरी अवधि तक उठाना, Apprendi के ठीक बीच में। राज्य सर्वोच्च न्यायालय ने People v.
