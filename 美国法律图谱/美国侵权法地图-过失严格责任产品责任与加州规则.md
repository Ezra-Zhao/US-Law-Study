# 美国侵权法地图：过失、严格责任、产品责任与加州规则

这篇讲什么：
- 侵权法分三大块：故意侵权、过失与严格责任；它是民事法，原告一般按优势证据（preponderance of the evidence）证明，与刑法按合理怀疑之外证明、由国家追诉不同，也与主要看当事人约定的合同法不同。
- 过失的骨架是义务、违反、因果关系与损害四要素：MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916) 打破合同相对性对制造商义务的限制，Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928) 划出义务与近因的边界，res ipsa loquitur（事实自明）则处理证据在被告一侧的情形。
- 严格责任与产品责任、抗辩与赔偿要连着加州看：Greenman v. Yuba Power Products, 59 Cal.2d 57 (1963) 与 Restatement (Second) of Torts 第 402A 条确立产品严格责任，Li v. Yellow Cab Co., 13 Cal.3d 804 (1975) 引入纯粹比较过失，Cal. Civ. Code 第 1714 条是加州一般过失责任的底盘。

## 一、总览：三大块与民事分界

### 中文

侵权法处理的是一个人因不当行为侵害他人身体、财产或相关利益时，法律怎样分配损失。它分三大块：故意侵权，看行为人是否故意实施侵害行为；过失，看行为人是否未尽合理注意；严格责任，则在特定情形下不问过错，有损害与因果关系即须负责。侵权诉讼是民事诉讼，由受害人自己起诉求偿，证明标准一般是优势证据，即主张的事实更可能为真。这一点把它与刑法分开：刑法由国家追诉、要保护被告自由，证明须达到合理怀疑之外。侵权也不同于合同法：合同责任主要来自当事人事先的约定，侵权义务则由法律对一般人设定，不以双方有合同为前提。读侵权法先分清这三块与这条民事分界，后面的规则才不会混。

### English

Tort law decides how the law allocates loss when one person's wrongful conduct injures another's body, property, or related interests. It has three large blocks: intentional torts, which turn on whether the actor meant to do the act that invades another's interest; negligence, which turns on a failure to use reasonable care; and strict liability, under which, in defined situations, liability follows from harm and causation without proof of fault. A tort suit is a civil action brought by the injured person for compensation, and the usual burden is preponderance of the evidence: the asserted fact is more likely true than not. That separates tort from criminal law, where the state prosecutes and proof must reach beyond a reasonable doubt because liberty is at stake. Tort also differs from contract: contract duties mainly come from the parties' prior agreement, while tort duties are imposed by law on people generally and do not require a contract between the parties.

### Español (学习译稿)

El derecho de daños (tort law) decide cómo el ordenamiento reparte la pérdida cuando la conducta ilícita de una persona lesiona el cuerpo, los bienes o intereses conexos de otra. Tiene tres grandes bloques: los ilícitos intencionales, que dependen de si el autor quiso realizar el acto que invade el interés ajeno; la negligencia, que depende de no haber usado el cuidado razonable; y la responsabilidad objetiva, por la cual, en situaciones definidas, la responsabilidad surge del daño y la causalidad sin probar culpa. La demanda por daños es una acción civil que interpone la propia persona lesionada para pedir compensación, y la carga habitual es la preponderancia de la evidencia, es decir, que el hecho afirmado es más probablemente verdadero que falso. Eso lo separa del derecho penal, donde el Estado persigue y la prueba debe llegar más allá de toda duda razonable porque está en juego la libertad. También difiere del derecho de contratos: los deberes contractuales nacen sobre todo del acuerdo previo de las partes, mientras que los deberes extracontractuales los impone la ley a las personas en general y no exigen un contrato entre las partes.

## 二、故意侵权与过失：四要素与义务边界

### 中文

故意侵权只作概览：殴击（battery）是故意且有害或冒犯性的身体接触，攻击（assault）是使他人即时感到这种接触将至，非法拘禁是无合法依据地限制人身自由，故意精神损害则要求极端 outrageous 的行为并造成严重精神痛苦。侵权法的主战场是过失，其成立须四要素齐备：对原告负有注意义务、违反该义务、违反与损害之间有因果关系、原告有实际损害。MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916) 认定汽车制造商对最终使用者负义务，打破了须有合同相对性才有义务的旧限。Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928) 则说明义务只及于可合理预见的原告，近因也以可预见性划界。证据方面，若事故通常只在有人过失时发生、致害工具又在被告排他控制下，可援用 res ipsa loquitur，让事实本身说话。

### English

The intentional torts can be taken in outline: battery is an intentional and harmful or offensive bodily contact; assault is intentionally causing another to apprehend that such contact is imminent; false imprisonment is confinement without lawful justification; and intentional infliction of emotional distress requires extreme and outrageous conduct causing severe distress. The main battlefield of tort law is negligence, which requires four elements together: a duty of care owed to the plaintiff, breach of that duty, causation between the breach and the harm, and actual damages. MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916), held that an automobile manufacturer owed a duty to the ultimate user and broke the old limit that duty required contractual privity. Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928), showed that duty runs only to reasonably foreseeable plaintiffs and that proximate cause is also bounded by foreseeability. On proof, res ipsa loquitur — the thing speaks for itself — may be invoked when the accident is of a kind that ordinarily occurs only through negligence and the instrumentality was in the defendant's exclusive control.

### Español (学习译稿)

Los ilícitos intencionales pueden verse en esquema: la agresión física consumada (battery) es un contacto corporal intencional, dañoso u ofensivo; el assault es hacer que otro tema de modo inminente ese contacto; el falso encarcelamiento es privar de libertad sin justificación legal; y la imposición intencional de sufrimiento emocional exige una conducta extrema y escandalosa que cause un sufrimiento grave. El campo principal del derecho de daños es la negligencia, que exige cuatro elementos juntos: un deber de cuidado hacia el demandante, el incumplimiento de ese deber, causalidad entre el incumplimiento y el daño, y un daño real. MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916), reconoció que el fabricante de automóviles debía un deber al usuario final y rompió el viejo límite de que el deber exigía relación contractual directa. Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928), mostró que el deber solo alcanza a demandantes razonablemente previsibles y que la causa próxima también se delimita por la previsibilidad. En materia de prueba, puede invocarse res ipsa loquitur, la cosa habla por sí misma, cuando el accidente es de un tipo que normalmente solo ocurre por negligencia y el instrumento causante estaba bajo el control exclusivo del demandado.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

고의 불법행위는 개요로 보면 됩니다. 폭행 접촉(battery)은 고의적이고 해롭거나 모욕적인 신체 접촉이고, 협박성 폭행(assault)은 그런 접촉이 임박했다는 두려움을 고의로 일으키는 것이며, 불법 감금은 법적 정당성 없이 자유를 구속하는 것이고, 고의적 정신적 고통 가해는 극단적이고 터무니없는 행위로 심각한 고통을 주는 경우에 성립합니다. 불법행위법의 주전장은 과실로, 네 요소가 함께 필요합니다. 원고를 향한 주의의무, 그 의무의 위반, 위반과 손해 사이의 인과관계, 그리고 실제 손해입니다. MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916)은 자동차 제조사가 최종 사용자에게 의무를 진다고 하여, 의무에는 계약상 직접 관계가 필요하다는 낡은 한계를 깼습니다. Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928)은 의무가 합리적으로 예견 가능한 원고에게만 미치고, 근인 역시 예견 가능성으로 경계가 정해진다는 것을 보여 주었습니다. 증거에서는, 사고가 통상 과실이 있을 때만 생기는 종류이고 원인이 된 물건이 피고의 배타적 지배 아래 있었다면, 사물 자체가 말한다는 res ipsa loquitur를 원용할 수 있습니다.

Các hành vi cố ý có thể nắm ở mức khái quát: battery là tiếp xúc thân thể cố ý, gây hại hoặc xúc phạm; assault là cố ý làm người khác lo sợ rằng tiếp xúc đó sắp xảy ra; giam giữ trái phép là hạn chế tự do thân thể mà không có căn cứ hợp pháp; và cố ý gây đau khổ tinh thần đòi hỏi hành vi cực đoan, quá đáng gây đau khổ nghiêm trọng. Về chứng cứ, có thể viện dẫn res ipsa loquitur, sự việc tự nó lên tiếng, khi tai nạn thuộc loại thông thường chỉ xảy ra do sơ suất và phương tiện gây hại nằm dưới sự kiểm soát độc quyền của bị đơn.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

आशयपूर्ण अपकृत्यों को रूपरेखा में लिया जा सकता है: battery आशयपूर्ण और हानिकारक या अपमानजनक शारीरिक संपर्क है; assault यह आशंका पैदा करना है कि ऐसा संपर्क तुरंत होने वाला है; मिथ्या कारावास बिना वैध औचित्य के स्वतंत्रता को रोकना है; और भावनात्मक पीड़ा का आशयपूर्ण आरोपण अत्यंत और घोर आचरण से गंभीर पीड़ा माँगता है। अपकृत्य विधि का मुख्य क्षेत्र उपेक्षा है, जिसके लिए चार तत्व एक साथ चाहिए: वादी के प्रति सावधानी का कर्तव्य, उस कर्तव्य का उल्लंघन, उल्लंघन और नुकसान के बीच कारण-कार्य संबंध, और वास्तविक नुकसान। MacPherson v. Buick Motor Co., 217 N.Y. 91 (1916) ने माना कि ऑटोमोबाइल निर्माता का अंतिम उपयोगकर्ता के प्रति कर्तव्य है और यह पुरानी सीमा तोड़ दी कि कर्तव्य के लिए अनुबंध की प्रत्यक्षता चाहिए। Palsgraf v. Long Island R.R., 248 N.Y. 339 (1928) ने दिखाया कि कर्तव्य केवल युक्तियुक्त रूप से पूर्वानुमेय वादियों तक जाता है और निकट कारण भी पूर्वानुमेयता से सीमित होता है। प्रमाण में res ipsa loquitur, अर्थात वस्तु स्वयं बोलती है, तब लिया जा सकता है जब दुर्घटना उस प्रकार की हो जो सामान्यतः उपेक्षा से ही होती है और हानि का साधन प्रतिवादी के अनन्य नियंत्रण में था।

## 三、严格责任：异常危险、动物与产品责任

### 中文

严格责任不以过错为要件，典型情形包括从事异常危险活动，以及对动物造成的损害负责；加州民法典第 3342 条（Cal. Civ. Code 第 3342 条）对犬咬伤即采严格责任，狗主人在法定情形下不能只以自己没有过错抗辩。严格责任最重要的展开在产品责任。Greenman v. Yuba Power Products, 59 Cal.2d 57 (1963) 由加州最高法院确立：制造商把有缺陷的产品投入市场、缺陷致人损害的，负严格责任。Restatement (Second) of Torts 第 402A 条随后把这一规则成文化，卖方即使已尽一切可能注意，也不免责。缺陷分三类：制造缺陷，是个别产品偏离自家设计；设计缺陷，是整条产品线按设计即不安全；警示缺陷，是未给出足够的使用风险说明。设计缺陷的判断主要有两条路：消费者期待测试，看产品是否比普通消费者所能期待的更危险；风险-效用测试，则权衡设计风险与替代设计的成本和效用。

### English

Strict liability does not require fault. Its typical settings include engaging in abnormally dangerous activities and responsibility for harm caused by animals; California Civil Code section 3342 imposes strict liability for dog bites, so in the situations it covers an owner cannot defend simply by saying he was not at fault. The most important development of strict liability is in products liability. Greenman v. Yuba Power Products, 59 Cal.2d 57 (1963), decided by the California Supreme Court, established that a manufacturer that places a defective product on the market is strictly liable when the defect causes injury. Section 402A of the Restatement (Second) of Torts then codified the rule, and a seller is not excused even if it exercised all possible care. Defects come in three types: a manufacturing defect, where an individual unit departs from its own design; a design defect, where the whole line is unsafe as designed; and a warning defect, where adequate instructions about the risks of use were not given. Design defect is judged mainly by two tests: the consumer-expectations test, asking whether the product is more dangerous than an ordinary consumer would expect, and the risk-utility test, weighing the design's risks against the cost and utility of an alternative design.

### Español (学习译稿)

## 四、抗辩与损害赔偿：比较过失与惩罚性赔偿

### 中文

原告自己有过失，不再像传统与有过失规则那样全盘败诉。加州在 Li v. Yellow Cab Co., 13 Cal.3d 804 (1975) 采纳纯粹比较过失：按各方过错比例扣减赔偿，原告过错再大，原则上也不完全丧失请求，只是拿回的数额按其份额减少。多名被告之间，非经济损害在加州按份分担：Cal. Civ. Code 第 1431.2 条源于 1986 年第 51 号提案（Prop 51），规定每名被告只按自身过错比例对非经济损害负责，不再对全部非经济损害负连带责任；经济损害的连带规则不在此条的改动范围内。赔偿以补偿实际损失为主，惩罚性赔偿是例外，须另行满足 Cal. Civ. Code 第 3294 条：原告须以清楚而有说服力的证据（clear and convincing evidence）证明被告有压迫、欺诈或恶意（oppression, fraud, or malice），门槛明显高于普通民事主张的优势证据。

### English

A plaintiff's own negligence no longer defeats the whole claim, as it did under the traditional contributory negligence rule. In Li v. Yellow Cab Co., 13 Cal.3d 804 (1975), California adopted pure comparative negligence: recovery is reduced in proportion to each party's fault, and however large the plaintiff's share of fault, the claim is in principle not completely barred, only reduced by that share. Among multiple defendants, non-economic damages in California are shared severally: Civil Code section 1431.2, which comes from Proposition 51 of 1986, provides that each defendant is liable for non-economic damages only in proportion to that defendant's percentage of fault, rather than jointly for all non-economic damages; the joint-liability treatment of economic damages is outside the change made by that section. Damages are mainly meant to compensate actual loss. Punitive damages are the exception and must separately satisfy Civil Code section 3294: the plaintiff must prove by clear and convincing evidence that the defendant engaged in oppression, fraud, or malice, a burden plainly higher than the preponderance standard for ordinary civil claims.

### Español (学习译稿)

Yellow Cab Co., 13 Cal.3d 804 (1975), California adoptó la negligencia comparativa pura: la indemnización se reduce en proporción a la culpa de cada parte, y por grande que sea la parte de culpa del demandante, en principio su pretensión no queda totalmente excluida, solo se reduce en esa proporción. Entre varios demandados, los daños no económicos en California se reparten de forma separada: la sección 1431.2 del Código Civil, procedente de la Proposición 51 de 1986, dispone que cada demandado responde de los daños no económicos solo en proporción a su porcentaje de culpa, y no de forma solidaria por todos ellos; el tratamiento solidario de los daños económicos queda fuera del cambio introducido por esa sección. Los daños buscan sobre todo compensar la pérdida real. Los daños punitivos son la excepción y deben cumplir además la sección 3294 del Código Civil: el demandante debe probar con evidencia clara y convincente (clear and convincing evidence) que el demandado incurrió en opresión, fraude o malicia (oppression, fraud, or malice), una carga claramente más alta que la preponderancia propia de las pretensiones civiles ordinarias.

## 五、加州衔接小结：以第 1714 条为底盘

### 中文

把前面的规则落回加州，底盘是 Cal. Civ. Code 第 1714 条：任何人都要为自己因缺乏通常注意或技能、故意或不当行为造成的对他人损害负责，这一条撑起加州一般过失责任。在这个底盘上，加州判例与成文法逐块加装：Li 案的纯粹比较过失负责在原告也有错时怎样分，第 1431.2 条负责多被告之间非经济损害怎样按份分，第 3294 条给惩罚性赔偿设高门槛，第 3342 条则在犬咬领域直接给出严格责任。产品责任这一块，加州既是发源地之一，Greenman 案与第 402A 条的谱系也说明普通法判例、法典条文与 Restatement 如何互相推动。学习顺序因此很清楚：先用第 1714 条与过失四要素搭框架，再把严格责任、产品缺陷类型与比较过失等规则逐块挂上去，最后回到具体事实判断义务、因果与份额。

### English

Bringing the rules back to California, the chassis is Civil Code section 1714: everyone is responsible for injury caused to another by that person's want of ordinary care or skill, or by intentional or wrongful conduct, and this section underlies California's general negligence liability. On that chassis, California case law and statutes add piece by piece: Li supplies pure comparative negligence for allocating loss when the plaintiff is also at fault; section 1431.2 allocates non-economic damages severally among multiple defendants; section 3294 sets a high gate for punitive damages; and section 3342 imposes strict liability directly in the dog-bite field. In products liability, California is one of the places of origin, and the lineage from Greenman to section 402A shows how common-law decisions, code provisions, and the Restatement push one another forward. The order of study is therefore clear: first build the frame with section 1714 and the four elements of negligence, then attach strict liability, the types of product defect, and comparative fault piece by piece, and finally return to the concrete facts to judge duty, causation, and shares of fault.

### Español (学习译稿)

Al devolver las reglas a California, el chasis es la sección 1714 del Código Civil: toda persona responde del daño causado a otra por su falta de cuidado o habilidad ordinarios, o por una conducta intencional o ilícita, y esa sección sostiene la responsabilidad general por negligencia en California. Sobre ese chasis, la jurisprudencia y las leyes de California añaden pieza a pieza: Li aporta la negligencia comparativa pura para repartir la pérdida cuando el demandante también tiene culpa; la sección 1431.2 reparte los daños no económicos de forma separada entre varios demandados; la sección 3294 pone una puerta alta a los daños punitivos; y la sección 3342 impone directamente responsabilidad objetiva en el ámbito de las mordeduras de perro. En responsabilidad por productos, California es uno de los lugares de origen, y la línea que va de Greenman a la sección 402A muestra cómo las decisiones del common law, las normas codificadas y el Restatement se impulsan mutuamente. El orden de estudio queda así claro: primero se arma el marco con la sección 1714 y los cuatro elementos de la negligencia, después se cuelgan pieza a pieza la responsabilidad objetiva, los tipos de defecto del producto y la culpa comparativa, y por último se vuelve a los hechos concretos para juzgar el deber, la causalidad y las cuotas de culpa.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

규칙을 캘리포니아로 되돌리면, 뼈대는 민법전 제1714조입니다. 누구든 통상의 주의나 기술이 부족해서, 또는 고의적이거나 위법한 행위로 타인에게 입힌 손해에 책임을 지며, 이 조항이 캘리포니아의 일반 과실 책임을 떠받칩니다. 그 뼈대 위에 캘리포니아 판례와 성문법이 조각조각 더해집니다. Li 판결은 원고에게도 과실이 있을 때 손실을 나누는 순수 비교과실을 제공하고, 제1431.2조는 여러 피고 사이에서 비경제적 손해를 각자 나누게 하며, 제3294조는 징벌적 손해배상에 높은 문을 세우고, 제3342조는 개 물림 분야에서 곧바로 엄격책임을 부과합니다. 제조물 책임에서 캘리포니아는 발원지 중 하나이고, Greenman에서 제402A조로 이어지는 계보는 보통법 판결, 법전 조항, Restatement가 서로를 어떻게 밀어 올리는지를 보여 줍니다. 학습 순서는 그래서 분명합니다. 먼저 제1714조와 과실의 네 요소로 틀을 세우고, 그다음 엄격책임, 제조물 결함의 유형, 비교과실을 조각조각 걸고, 마지막으로 구체적 사실로 돌아가 의무, 인과관계, 과실 비율을 판단합니다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Đưa các quy tắc trở lại California, khung sườn là Bộ luật Dân sự điều 1714: mọi người đều chịu trách nhiệm về thương tổn gây cho người khác do thiếu sự cẩn trọng hoặc kỹ năng thông thường, hoặc do hành vi cố ý hay sai trái, và điều này nâng đỡ trách nhiệm sơ suất chung của California. Trên khung đó, án lệ và luật thành văn của California cộng thêm từng mảnh: Li cung cấp sơ suất so sánh thuần túy để phân bổ tổn thất khi nguyên đơn cũng có lỗi; điều 1431.2 phân chia thiệt hại phi kinh tế theo phần giữa nhiều bị đơn; điều 3294 đặt một cánh cổng cao cho bồi thường trừng phạt; và điều 3342 áp đặt trực tiếp trách nhiệm nghiêm ngặt trong lĩnh vực chó cắn. Trong trách nhiệm sản phẩm, California là một trong những nơi khởi nguồn, và dòng dõi từ Greenman tới điều 402A cho thấy án lệ thông luật, quy định trong bộ luật và Restatement thúc đẩy lẫn nhau như thế nào. Thứ tự học vì vậy rất rõ: trước hết dựng khung bằng điều 1714 và bốn yếu tố của sơ suất, sau đó gắn từng mảnh trách nhiệm nghiêm ngặt, các loại khuyết tật sản phẩm và lỗi so sánh, cuối cùng quay lại sự kiện cụ thể để xét nghĩa vụ, nhân quả và tỷ lệ lỗi.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

नियमों को California में वापस लाने पर ढांचा Civil Code धारा 1714 है: हर व्यक्ति उस चोट के लिए उत्तरदायी है जो उसने साधारण सावधानी या कौशल की कमी से, या आशयपूर्ण या गलत आचरण से दूसरे को पहुँचाई हो, और यही धारा California के सामान्य उपेक्षा दायित्व को सहारा देती है। उस ढांचे पर California के निर्णय और कानून टुकड़ा-टुकड़ा जुड़ते हैं: Li वादी के भी दोषी होने पर नुकसान बाँटने के लिए शुद्ध तुलनात्मक उपेक्षा देता है; धारा 1431.2 कई प्रतिवादियों के बीच गैर-आर्थिक नुकसान को अलग-अलग बाँटती है; धारा 3294 दंडात्मक हर्जाने के लिए ऊँचा द्वार रखती है; और धारा 3342 कुत्ते के काटने के क्षेत्र में सीधे कठोर दायित्व लगाती है। उत्पाद दायित्व में California उत्पत्ति के स्थानों में से एक है, और Greenman से धारा 402A तक की वंशावली दिखाती है कि common law के निर्णय, संहिता के प्रावधान और Restatement एक-दूसरे को आगे बढ़ाते हैं। अध्ययन का क्रम इसलिए स्पष्ट है: पहले धारा 1714 और उपेक्षा के चार तत्वों से ढांचा बनाएँ, फिर कठोर दायित्व, उत्पाद दोष के प्रकार और तुलनात्मक दोष को टुकड़ा-टुकड़ा जोड़ें, और अंत में ठोस तथ्यों पर लौटकर कर्तव्य, कारणता और दोष के हिस्से आँकें।
