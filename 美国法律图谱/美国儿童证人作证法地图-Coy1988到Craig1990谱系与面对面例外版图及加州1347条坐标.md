# 美国儿童证人作证法地图：Coy (1988) 到 Craig (1990) 谱系、面对面例外版图与加州第 1347 条坐标

> 本篇是图谱篇，与备考篇同题成对：备考篇管答题顺序，本篇管版图——面对面是原则、闭路电视是带锁的例外，锁有两把：个案必要性与可靠性要件。

## 一、谱系三段：面对面原则、屏风案与闭路案

### 中文

第一段是原则期：对质条款的文本与传统都把面对面作证当默认值，证人要在被告面前宣誓、受交叉询问、由陪审团看神情。第二段是 Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988)：屏风挡住视线、又没有个案必要性认定，定罪被撤销——法院说得很直白，面对面本身就是对质的核心。第三段是 Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990)：单向闭路电视在满足重要公共政策、个案必要性与可靠性要件时可以通过。从 1988 到 1990 只隔 2 年，版图就从近乎绝对的面对面，变成原则加窄例外，这条谱系是整张地图的主轴。

### English

Segment one is the baseline era: the Confrontation Clause's text and tradition treat face-to-face testimony as the default — oath, cross-examination and demeanor observation in the defendant's presence. Segment two is Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988): a screen blocking the line of sight, with no case-specific necessity finding, and the conviction fell — face-to-face presence is itself the core of confrontation. Segment three is Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990): one-way closed-circuit television can pass where an important public policy, case-specific necessity and the reliability elements are all present. From 1988 to 1990 is only 2 years, yet the map moved from a near-absolute face-to-face rule to a principle plus a narrow exception; that arc is this map's main axis.

## 二、版图四格：谁在哪一格

### 中文

把案件摆进四格就不会混。原则格：普通证人，面对面，无例外可谈。屏风格（Coy）：物理遮挡又无个案认定，不合格。闭路格（Craig）：人不在场内，但宣誓、交叉询问、神情观察、与律师实时沟通四件齐全，又有个案必要性认定，合格。陈述格：录好的陈述想绕开出庭，另走 Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004) 的陈述性陈述（testimonial）那本账，与 Craig 的当庭远程作证不是一回事，混格是备考与实务里最常见的错。四格里只有闭路格是例外，其余三格各有各的账。

声明：本文为个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议；具体事务请咨询有执照的专业人士。

### English

Four boxes keep the cases straight. The principle box: ordinary witnesses, face-to-face, no exception to discuss. The screen box (Coy): physical blocking with no case-specific finding — fails. The closed-circuit box (Craig): the witness is not in the room, but oath, cross-examination, demeanor observation and real-time communication with counsel are all present, plus a case-specific necessity finding — passes. The statement box: a recorded statement offered instead of live testimony belongs to Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004) and its testimonial-statement account, a different ledger from Craig's live remote testimony; mixing these boxes is the most common exam and practice error. Only the closed-circuit box is an exception; the other three each keep their own accounts.

### Español (初译待校)

Cuatro casillas ordenan los casos. Principio: testigos ordinarios, cara a cara, sin excepción que discutir. Pantalla (Coy): bloqueo físico sin constatación en el caso — no pasa. Circuito cerrado (Craig): la testigo no está en la sala, pero hay juramento, contrainterrogatorio, observación de la expresión y comunicación en tiempo real con el abogado, más la constatación de necesidad — pasa. Declaración grabada: una declaración grabada ofrecida en lugar de declarar en vivo pertenece a la cuenta de Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004) y sus declaraciones testimoniales, distinta de la declaración remota en vivo de Craig; mezclar casillas es el error más común. Solo el circuito cerrado es excepción.

## 三、加州坐标：第 1347 条在版图哪一格

### 中文

加州《刑法典》第 1347 条落在闭路格里：它给特定案件中的未成年受害人开闭路电视通道，但把 Craig 的两把锁原样装上——先经听证作个案必要性认定，再保住宣誓、交叉询问与神情观察。坐标的用法是双向的：检方引用它时要同时交出必要性证据，辩方攻击它时先查认定有没有做满、再查可靠性要件有没有缺项；两样都齐全，这格就站得住。来源：Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988)；Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990)；Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004)；加州《刑法典》第 1347 条。

声明：本文为个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议；具体事务请咨询有执照的专业人士。

### English

California Penal Code section 1347 sits in the closed-circuit box: it opens a closed-circuit channel for minor victims in specified cases but installs Craig's two locks unchanged — a case-specific necessity finding after a hearing first, then oath, cross-examination and demeanor observation preserved throughout. The coordinate works both ways: the prosecution invoking it must also produce the necessity evidence, and the defense attacking it checks first whether the findings were fully made and then whether any reliability element is missing; with both complete, the box holds. Sources: Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988); Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990); Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004); California Penal Code section 1347.

### Español (初译待校)

El § 1347 del Código Penal de California cae en la casilla del circuito cerrado: abre ese canal para víctimas menores en casos especificados, pero instala intactas las dos cerraduras de Craig — constatación de necesidad tras audiencia y, durante todo el acto, juramento, contrainterrogatorio y observación de la expresión. La coordenada sirve en ambos sentidos: la acusación que lo invoca debe aportar la prueba de necesidad, y la defensa que lo ataca revisa primero si los hallazgos están completos y después si falta algún elemento de fiabilidad. Fuentes: Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988); Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990); Crawford v. Washington, 541 U.S. 36 (2004); Código Penal de California, § 1347.
