# 追溯法备考核心：宪法第一条、Calder 四类、Stogner 红线与加州《刑法典》第 3 条

> 板块：法律备考｜主题：法律能不能回头加重已经做完的事的刑罚——本篇管追溯法条款（Ex Post Facto Clauses）：联邦宪法第一条第九款第三项管国会、第十款第一项管各州。本篇与《美国追溯法地图》同题成对：图谱篇管 1798 年 Calder 到 2003 年 Stogner 与 Smith 同年分界的谱系，本篇只管答题流水线。与刑事追诉时效备考篇、合同条款备考篇分工：时效篇管追诉时钟本身，合同条款篇管州法回头改合同义务，本篇管回头加重刑罚。
## 一、先认禁令：两处文本、只管刑罚性加重

### 中文

追溯法禁令在宪法里出现两次：第一条第九款第三项禁止国会通过追溯法，第一条第十款第一项禁止各州通过追溯法。核心定义沿用 Calder v. Bull, 3 U.S. 386 (1798)，美国联邦最高法院，Chase 大法官在个别意见中列出四类，后文详述。答题先卡两道门：第一，法律必须在行为完成之后制定或生效，却回头适用到行为之时；第二，回头适用必须让行为人处境更不利，而且这种不利属于刑罚性的。只满足第一道门不够——税法、登记法等民事规则回头适用，先走 Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003) 的刑罚性判断，不直接套追溯法禁令。时间账：2026－1798＝228，Calder 的框架已经用了 228 年（本地复算一致）。

### English

Ex post facto bans appear twice: Article I, Section 9, Clause 3 bars Congress, and Article I, Section 10, Clause 1 bars the states. The core definition follows Calder v. Bull, 3 U.S. 386 (1798), where Justice Chase's seriatim opinion listed four categories, detailed below. Two gates come first: the law must be enacted after the act and applied retroactively to it, and that application must disadvantage the person in a way that is punitive. Meeting only the first gate is not enough — civil rules such as tax or registration laws applied retroactively first face the punitive inquiry of Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003). Arithmetic: 2026 - 1798 = 228 years of the Calder framework.

### Español

Dos puertas van primero: la ley debe dictarse despues del acto y aplicarse retroactivamente, y esa aplicacion debe perjudicar a la persona de modo punitivo. Cumplir solo la primera puerta no basta; las normas civiles retroactivas pasan primero por la pregunta punitiva de Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003). Cuenta: 2026 - 1798 = 228 anos del marco de Calder.

## 二、Calder 四类：答题的清单

### 中文

Calder 四类要背成清单：一，把行为时不构成犯罪的行为回头定为犯罪；二，把行为时的犯罪回头加重等级；三，把行为时的刑罚回头加大；四，改变证据规则，使定罪比行为时更容易（例如行为时需要两个证人，回头改成一个证人就够）。现代判例对第四类作了收窄：Collins v. Youngblood, 497 U.S. 37 (1990)，美国联邦最高法院，判程序性变化不当然落入第四类——陪审团组成等程序调整若不改变定罪所需的证据量与性质，不构成追溯法；该案同时推翻了把第四类读得过宽的旧案。答题动作：先把新法对号入座到四类中的哪一类，对不上四类、又只是程序变化的，不要硬套追溯法，改查正当程序等其他条款。时间账：1990－1798＝192，Collins 的收窄距 Calder 192 年。

### English

Memorize Calder's four categories as a checklist: (1) criminalizing an act that was innocent when done; (2) aggravating a crime beyond its grade when committed; (3) increasing the punishment beyond what applied when the act was done; (4) changing the rules of evidence so conviction becomes easier than at the time of the act, for example requiring only one witness where two were once needed. Modern law narrows the fourth: Collins v. Youngblood, 497 U.S. 37 (1990) held that procedural changes do not automatically fall in category four — a change in jury composition that does not alter the quantum or nature of proof needed for conviction is not ex post facto, and the case overruled broader readings. Exam move: slot the new law into a category first; if it fits none and is merely procedural, do not force ex post facto and look to due process instead. Arithmetic: 1990 - 1798 = 192 years from Calder to Collins.

### Español

Memorice las cuatro categorias de Calder: (1) criminalizar un acto inocente cuando se cometio; (2) agravar un delito mas alla de su grado original; (3) aumentar la pena aplicable al momento del acto; (4) cambiar las reglas probatorias para facilitar la condena, por ejemplo exigir un solo testigo donde antes se exigian dos. La jurisprudencia moderna estrecha la cuarta: Collins v. Youngblood, 497 U.S. 37 (1990) sostuvo que los cambios procesales no caen automaticamente en la cuarta categoria. Movimiento de examen: ubique primero la ley nueva en una categoria; si no encaja y es solo procesal, no fuerce la clausula y examine el debido proceso. Cuenta: 1990 - 1798 = 192 anos de Calder a Collins.

## 三、刑罚加大的三种常见样子与 Stogner 红线

### 中文

第三类最常考，样子有三。其一，量刑幅度回头抬高：Weaver v. Graham, 450 U.S. 24 (1981) 判佛州回头削减囚犯的善行折抵（gain time）构成追溯法，刑罚不只看判决书上的年数，也看实际服刑的计算规则；Peugh v. United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) 进一步判，即便是咨询性的联邦量刑指南，回头用更高版本、显著增加更重刑期的风险，也触发禁令。其二，强制刑回头出现：Lindsey v. Washington, 301 U.S. 397 (1937) 判行为时法官可给低于上限的刑、回头改成必须给满上限，构成加重。其三，时钟回头续命——这是红线：Stogner v. California, 539 U.S. 607 (2003)，美国联邦最高法院 5 比 4，Breyer 大法官执笔，加州把儿童性犯罪的追诉时效在已经届满之后回头延长、复活已死的追诉，判为追溯法无效；时效未届满时延长一般可以，届满后再复活不可以，这条线要画死。时间账：2003－1981＝22，从 Weaver 到 Stogner 隔 22 年；2026－2003＝23，Stogner 红线已立 23 年。

### English

Category three is the most tested, in three shapes. First, a retroactively raised sentencing exposure: Weaver v. Graham, 450 U.S. 24 (1981) held Florida's retroactive cut to gain-time credits ex post facto — punishment includes how time is actually computed, not only the number on the judgment; Peugh v. United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) added that even advisory federal Guidelines trigger the ban when a later, higher version creates a significant risk of a heavier sentence. Second, a retroactive mandatory penalty: Lindsey v. Washington, 301 U.S. 397 (1937). Third, reviving a dead clock — the red line: Stogner v. California, 539 U.S. 607 (2003), 5-4, by Justice Breyer, struck down California's revival of time-barred child sex prosecutions; extending a limitations period before it expires is generally allowed, reviving it after expiry is not. Arithmetic: 2003 - 1981 = 22, and 2026 - 2003 = 23 years since Stogner.

### Español

United States, 569 U.S. 530 (2013) anadio que incluso las Guias federales consultivas activan la prohibicion cuando una version posterior mas alta crea un riesgo significativo de pena mayor. Segunda, una pena obligatoria retroactiva: Lindsey v. Washington, 301 U.S. 397 (1937). Tercera, revivir un plazo muerto, la linea roja: Stogner v. Cuentas: 2003 - 1981 = 22, y 2026 - 2003 = 23 anos desde Stogner.

## 四、民事外衣与加州坐标、答题流水线

### 中文

最后一道分界是刑罚还是民事。Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003)，美国联邦最高法院，Kennedy 大法官执笔：阿拉斯加性犯罪者登记与社区通知法回头适用到法生效前已定罪的人，法院先看立法意图是民事监管、再看效果是否明显刑罚性，结论是登记属民事监管、不构成刑罚，追溯法禁令不适用。同年 Stogner 与 Smith 并读最清楚：复活追诉是刑罚，登记监管是民事，两案同在 2003 年、结论相反，分界不在回头本身，而在回头的东西是不是刑罚。加州坐标两点：第一，《加州宪法》第一条第九款同样禁止追溯法，与联邦第十款双保险；第二，《加州刑法典》第 3 条定州刑法默认不溯及既往、除非明文声明溯及——但明文声明也越不过联邦 Stogner 红线，已届满的追诉不能靠州法一句话复活。答题流水线五步：一问新法在行为前还是行为后生效、二问是否回头适用、三问对号 Calder 四类、四问若是民事外衣先走 Smith 的意图加效果判断、五问落到加州时先查第 3 条默认规则再查联邦红线。把民事登记当刑罚硬套禁令、把已死时效当普通程序延长，是这类题最常见的两个错法。

### English

The last boundary is punishment versus civil regulation. Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003), by Justice Kennedy, upheld Alaska's retroactive sex-offender registration and notification law: the legislature intended civil regulation, and the effects were not so punitive as to override that intent, so the ban did not apply. Read with Stogner from the same year, the line is clear: reviving a prosecution is punishment, registration is regulation, and the divide is not retroactivity itself but whether the retroactive burden is punishment. California coordinates: article I, section 9 of the California Constitution also bars ex post facto laws, and Penal Code section 3 makes state penal law prospective unless retroactivity is expressly declared — yet an express declaration cannot cross the federal Stogner line for expired prosecutions. Five-step checklist: timing, retroactive application, Calder category, Smith intent-and-effects if a civil label is claimed, then California section 3 plus the federal floor. The two common errors are forcing the ban onto civil registration and treating a dead limitations period as an ordinary procedural extension.

### Español

La ultima frontera es castigo frente a regulacion civil. Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003), opinion de Kennedy, confirmo la ley de Alaska de registro y notificacion de delincuentes sexuales aplicada retroactivamente: la intencion legislativa era regulatoria civil y los efectos no eran tan punitivos como para desplazarla. Leido junto a Stogner, del mismo ano, la linea es clara: revivir una persecucion es castigo, registrar es regulacion. Coordenadas de California: el Articulo I, Seccion 9 de la Constitucion estatal tambien prohibe leyes ex post facto, y el Codigo Penal, Seccion 3, hace la ley penal prospectiva salvo declaracion expresa de retroactividad, que aun asi no puede cruzar la linea federal de Stogner. Lista de cinco pasos: momento, aplicacion retroactiva, categoria de Calder, intencion y efectos de Smith si se alega etiqueta civil, y luego la Seccion 3 mas el piso federal.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

마지막 경계는 형벌과 민사 규제의 구분입니다. Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003)은 Kennedy 대법관이 집필하여 알래스카 성범죄자 등록·통지법의 소급 적용을 유지했습니다. 입법 의도는 민사 규제였고 효과도 그 의도를 뒤집을 만큼 형벌적이지 않았습니다. 같은 해 Stogner와 함께 읽으면 선이 분명합니다. 소추 부활은 형벌이고 등록은 규제이며, 구분은 소급 자체가 아니라 소급되는 부담이 형벌인가에 있습니다. 캘리포니아 좌표는 둘입니다. 주 헌법 제1조 제9항도 소급법을 금지하고, 형법 제3조는 명시 선언이 없는 한 주 형법을 장래 적용으로 두지만, 명시 선언도 만료된 소추에 관한 연방 Stogner 선을 넘지 못합니다. 다섯 단계는 시기, 소급 적용, Calder 범주, 민사 표방 시 Smith의 의도와 효과, 그리고 제3조와 연방 하한입니다.

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Ranh gioi cuoi cung la hinh phat va dieu tiet dan su. Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003), do tham phan Kennedy chap but, giu nguyen luat dang ky va thong bao nguoi pham toi tinh duc cua Alaska ap dung hoi to: y dinh lap phap la dieu tiet dan su va hieu qua khong du tinh hinh phat de dao nguoc y dinh do. Doc cung Stogner trong cung nam, lan ranh ro rang: hoi sinh truy to la hinh phat, dang ky la dieu tiet. Toa do California: Dieu I, Khoan 9 cua Hien phap bang cung cam luat hoi to, va Bo luat Hinh su Khoan 3 quy dinh luat hinh su cua bang ap dung cho tuong lai tru khi tuyen bo hoi to ro rang — nhung tuyen bo do khong the vuot qua lan ranh lien bang Stogner doi voi cac vu truy to da het han. Danh sach nam buoc: thoi diem, ap dung hoi to, loai Calder, y dinh va hieu qua theo Smith neu nhan dan su duoc neu ra, roi Khoan 3 cong san lien bang.

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

अंतिम सीमा दंड बनाम नागरिक नियमन है। Smith v. Doe, 538 U.S. 84 (2003), न्यायमूर्ति Kennedy द्वारा, ने Alaska के यौन अपराधी पंजीकरण व अधिसूचना कानून के पूर्वव्यापी लागू होने को बरकरार रखा: विधायी इरादा नागरिक नियमन था और प्रभाव इतने दंडात्मक नहीं थे कि उस इरादे को पलट दें। उसी वर्ष के Stogner के साथ पढ़ने पर रेखा स्पष्ट है: अभियोजन को पुनर्जीवित करना दंड है, पंजीकरण नियमन है। California निर्देशांक: राज्य संविधान का अनुच्छेद I, धारा 9 भी पूर्वव्यापी कानून रोकता है, और दंड संहिता धारा 3 राज्य दंड कानून को भविष्यलक्षी बनाती है जब तक पूर्वव्यापिता स्पष्ट घोषित न हो — फिर भी वह घोषणा समाप्त अभियोजनों पर संघीय Stogner रेखा पार नहीं कर सकती। पाँच कदम: समय, पूर्वव्यापी लागू होना, Calder श्रेणी, नागरिक लेबल का दावा हो तो Smith का इरादा-व-प्रभाव, फिर धारा 3 और संघीय न्यूनतम।
