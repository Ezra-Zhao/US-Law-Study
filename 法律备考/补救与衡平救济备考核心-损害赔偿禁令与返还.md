# 补救与衡平救济备考核心：损害赔偿、禁令与返还

这篇讲什么：
- 这是律师考试 Remedies 科目的备考框架，只讲救济规则、门槛与陷阱，不讲具体案件怎么办。主线只有一条：先问原告丢了哪种利益（期待、信赖还是返还），再问普通法的钱够不够（够，衡平不进场；不够，才谈特定履行与禁令），最后看衡平抗辩挡不挡路。
- 来源口径：以《合同法重述（第二版）》（Restatement (Second) of Contracts）第 344 条以下的救济编与《侵权法重述》为框架，程序规则看 Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 第 65 条，州法对照以加州 Civil Code 与 Code of Civil Procedure 为主。判例年份与引用格式按公开判例文本核对；示例均为假设教学情形。

## 一、补救总框架：三种利益，两种救济，一道门槛

### 中文（主文）

先分利益。《合同法重述（第二版）》第 344 条把违约救济保护的利益分三种：期待利益（expectation interest），让受约人站到合同被履行时本应处的位置；信赖利益（reliance interest），让他回到签约前的位置，拿回为信赖合同花掉的钱；返还利益（restitution interest），把他已经交给违约方、让对方得利的价值要回来。考试第一步永远是认利益：题目问利润，多半是期待；问前期投入打水漂，多半是信赖；问预付款退不退，多半是返还。再分救济形态：普通法救济给钱（damages），衡平救济给行为命令（特定履行、禁令），返还救济给已转移的价值。分界线是适当性门槛：衡平救济以普通法救济不充分为前提，金钱能补清的损失，法院一般不发禁令、不判特定履行。所以顺序不能反：先算钱，钱算不清、补不足，再进衡平。

---

### English

Start with the interest. Section 344 of the Restatement (Second) of Contracts divides the interests protected by contract remedies into three: the expectation interest, which puts the promisee in the position performance would have produced; the reliance interest, which restores the position before the contract, returning money spent in reliance; and the restitution interest, which recovers value conferred on the breaching party. The first exam step is always to name the interest: lost profits usually mean expectation, wasted upfront spending means reliance, and a prepaid amount to be returned means restitution. Next, the form of relief: legal relief gives money (damages), equitable relief orders conduct (specific performance, injunctions), and restitution returns transferred value. The dividing line is adequacy: equitable relief presupposes that the legal remedy is inadequate. If money can make the plaintiff whole, courts ordinarily deny injunctions and specific performance. So the order cannot be reversed — compute the money first; only when money cannot measure or cover the loss does equity enter.

### Español (estudio, pendiente de revisión)

Primero, el interés. El § 344 del Restatement (Second) of Contracts distingue tres intereses: el de expectativa (expectation), que coloca al acreedor donde estaría si el contrato se hubiera cumplido; el de confianza (reliance), que lo devuelve a la posición anterior al contrato; y el de restitución (restitution), que recupera el valor entregado a la parte incumplidora. Después, la forma: la tutela legal da dinero (damages), la equitativa ordena conductas (cumplimiento específico, mandamientos) y la restitución devuelve el valor transferido. La equidad solo entra si el remedio legal es inadecuado: si el dinero basta, no hay mandamiento ni cumplimiento específico. (Traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión — 初译待校)

## 二、金钱赔偿：可预见、确定、可避免，三道过滤

### 中文（主文）

合同赔偿先过三道过滤。一是可预见性：Hadley v. Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch. 341 立的规则分两肢，违约方只赔订约时可预见的损失——按事物通常进程自然产生的，或双方订约时已预见、因特殊情事沟通而进入订约考量的；没沟通过的特殊利润损失，赔不到。二是确定性：损失须以合理确定性证明，纯推测不赔；新企业利润过去常被一刀切挡下，现代规则放松为可用证据证明即可，但门槛仍在。三是可避免性：受害方不能放任损失扩大（mitigation），本可合理避免的部分扣减，但减损花的合理费用可以要回。名义赔偿（nominal damages）是权利被侵但无实际损失时的象征性小额；补偿性赔偿（compensatory）补实际损失；惩罚性赔偿（punitive）在合同领域原则上不给，只在侵权等领域、且有恶意欺压等情形时才谈。侵权一侧底盘是加州 Civil Code 第 3333 条：非合同之诉，赔偿以在个案情事下自然产生、或可合理推定双方已预料的全部损害为度。算例：卖方违约不交一台通用机床，买方市价补购多花 8,000 美元，另因停产丢了一笔从未向卖方提起的特殊订单利润 50,000 美元——补购差价 8,000 美元自然可预见可赔，50,000 美元特殊利润未沟通、不可预见，不赔；若买方明知有平价现货却拖着不买，扩大的部分还要按可避免性扣掉。

---

### English

Contract damages pass three filters. First, foreseeability: Hadley v. Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch. 341 limits recovery to losses foreseeable at contracting — those arising naturally in the usual course of things, or those both parties contemplated because special circumstances were communicated; an unmentioned special profit loss is not recoverable. Second, certainty: losses must be proved with reasonable certainty, and pure speculation fails; the old per se bar on new-business profits has softened into an evidence question, but the gate remains. Third, avoidability: the injured party may not let losses pile up (mitigation); the reasonably avoidable portion is deducted, while reasonable mitigation expenses are recoverable. Nominal damages are a symbolic small sum where a right was violated without actual loss; compensatory damages cover actual loss; punitive damages are generally unavailable in contract and belong mainly to tort, where malice or oppression is shown. On the tort side, California Civil Code section 3333 sets the measure for non-contract claims: compensation for all detriment proximately caused, naturally arising, or reasonably supposed to have been contemplated. Worked example: a seller breaches on a standard machine tool; the buyer covers at $8,000 more, and also loses $50,000 of profit on a special order never mentioned to the seller. The $8,000 cover difference is naturally foreseeable and recoverable; the $50,000 special profit, never communicated, is not; and if the buyer passed up an equally priced machine in stock, the added loss is cut by avoidability.

### Español (estudio, pendiente de revisión)

Los daños contractuales pasan tres filtros. Previsibilidad: Hadley v. Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch. 341 solo permite pérdidas previsibles al contratar — las que surgen naturalmente o las contempladas porque las circunstancias especiales fueron comunicadas; el lucro especial no mencionado no se recupera. Certeza: la pérdida debe probarse con certeza razonable, sin especulación. Evitabilidad (mitigation): la parte perjudicada no puede dejar crecer el daño; lo evitable se deduce y los gastos razonables de mitigación se recuperan. Los daños nominales son simbólicos; los compensatorios cubren la pérdida real; los punitivos, en principio, no proceden en contratos y son propios del ámbito extracontractual con malicia u opresión. En California, el § 3333 del Civil Code mide el daño extracontractual por todo el perjuicio causado próximamente. Ejemplo: cubrir una máquina estándar cuesta 8.000 dólares más (recuperable); el lucro de 50.000 de un pedido especial nunca comunicado no se recupera. (Traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión — 初译待校)

### 한국어 (초역, 교정 대기)

계약 손해배상은 세 개의 관문을 지납니다. 예견가능성: Hadley v. Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch. 341에 따라 계약 당시 예견할 수 있었던 손해만 배상됩니다. 통상 과정에서 자연히 생기는 손해, 또는 특수 사정이 알려졌기에 양쪽이 예상했던 손해입니다. 알리지 않은 특별 이익 손실은 받을 수 없습니다. 확실성: 손해는 합리적인 확실성으로 증명해야 하며 추측은 안 됩니다. 회피가능성(mitigation): 피해자는 손해를 방치할 수 없고, 합리적으로 피할 수 있었던 부분은 빠지며, 경감에 쓴 합리적인 비용은 돌려받습니다. 명목적 배상은 실제 손해가 없을 때의 상징 금액, 보상적 배상은 실제 손해, 징벌적 배상은 원칙적으로 계약에서는 인정되지 않고 악의나 억압이 있는 불법행위 영역의 것입니다. 캘리포니아 Civil Code §3333은 비계약 소송의 손해를 근인으로 생긴 전부로 정합니다. 예시: 표준 공작기계의 대체구매 차액 8,000달러는 예견 가능하여 배상되고, 알리지 않은 특별 주문 이익 50,000달러는 배상되지 않습니다. (학습용 초역, 교정 대기 중 — 初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

Bồi thường hợp đồng qua ba bộ lọc. Khả năng thấy trước: Hadley v. Baxendale (1854) 9 Exch. 341 chỉ cho bồi thường thiệt hại có thể thấy trước khi giao kết — phát sinh tự nhiên theo diễn biến thông thường, hoặc đã được hai bên dự liệu vì hoàn cảnh đặc biệt đã được thông báo; lợi nhuận đặc biệt chưa từng nhắc tới thì không được bồi thường. Tính xác định: thiệt hại phải được chứng minh với độ chắc chắn hợp lý, suy đoán thuần túy thì không. Khả năng tránh được (mitigation): bên bị hại không được để thiệt hại phình to; phần có thể tránh một cách hợp lý sẽ bị trừ, còn chi phí giảm thiểu hợp lý được hoàn lại. Bồi thường danh nghĩa chỉ là tượng trưng; bồi thường bù đắp chi trả thiệt hại thực tế; bồi thường trừng phạt về nguyên tắc không có trong hợp đồng, mà thuộc lĩnh vực ngoài hợp đồng khi có ác ý hay áp bức. ## 三、约定损害赔偿：合理预估可执行，罚金条款作废

### 中文（主文）

合同里事先写好的违约金额，走另一套规则。《合同法重述（第二版）》第 356 条与 UCC 第 2-718(1) 条口径一致：订约时若实际损失难以证明或难以用其他方式获得足够救济，事先约定的金额与订约时预见的损失、或与实际发生的损失相比合理，就可执行；金额不合理地大，就是不可执行的罚金（penalty），法院只给实际损失。考题爱在两头设套：金额与实际损失完全脱钩、明显是吓唬对方的，罚金作废；金额是订约时对难估损失的真诚预估，哪怕事后实际损失偏小，也多能守住。加州把推定做成翻面规则，是最爱考的州法差别：Civil Code 第 1671(b) 条对一般（商事）合同，约定损害赔偿条款有效，主张无效的一方要证明其在订约情事下不合理；而第 1671(c) 与 (d) 条对消费者合同（购买、出租、租赁个人、家庭或家用财产服务）与住宅买卖、住宅租赁，翻成推定无效，除非主张有效的一方证明按通常救济实际损失极难确定。同一条款，商事场合先天有效、消费场合先天无效，举证责任正好相反，别记反。

---

### English

A sum fixed in advance in the contract follows its own rule. Restatement (Second) section 356 and UCC section 2-718(1) agree: where loss is difficult to prove or otherwise hard to remedy at contracting, a liquidated amount reasonable in light of anticipated or actual loss is enforceable; an unreasonably large amount is an unenforceable penalty, and the plaintiff is remitted to actual damages. Exams set traps at both ends: a sum untethered from any loss estimate, plainly meant to threaten, fails as a penalty; a genuine contracting-time estimate of hard-to-measure loss usually holds even if actual loss turns out smaller. California flips the presumption, the favorite state-law contrast: Civil Code section 1671(b) makes liquidated damages in ordinary (commercial) contracts valid unless the challenger proves unreasonableness under the circumstances at contracting, while sections 1671(c) and (d) make them presumptively void in consumer contracts (goods or services for personal, family, or household purposes) and in residential purchase and lease contracts, unless the proponent proves actual loss would be impracticable or extremely difficult to fix. Same clause, opposite starting points and opposite burdens — commercial presumed valid, consumer presumed void.

### Español (estudio, pendiente de revisión)

La suma pactada de antemano sigue su propia regla. El § 356 del Restatement (Second) y el § 2-718(1) del UCC coinciden: si al contratar la pérdida era difícil de probar o de remediar, una cantidad razonable frente a la pérdida prevista o real es exigible; una cantidad irrazonablemente grande es una pena (penalty) inexigible y solo se recupera el daño real. California invierte la presunción: el § 1671(b) del Civil Code valida la cláusula en contratos comerciales salvo que quien la impugna pruebe su irrazonabilidad, mientras los apartados (c) y (d) la presumen nula en contratos de consumo (bienes o servicios personales, familiares o domésticos) y en compraventa y arrendamiento residenciales, salvo que quien la defiende pruebe que fijar el daño real sería impracticable o extremadamente difícil. Misma cláusula, cargas opuestas. (Traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión — 初译待校)

## 四、衡平救济：特定履行与禁令，先过适当性再过四要素

### 中文（主文）

衡平救济有两件主力。第一件是特定履行（specific performance）：加州 Civil Code 第 3384 条规定，除法定例外，法院可判履行合同义务，《合同法重述（第二版）》第 359 与 360 条给框架——金钱救济不充分才考虑，判断因素包括损失难估、标的难替代、被告可能无力赔付；土地因被视为独一无二，几乎自动满足适当性。红线是个人服务合同：强迫一个人提供个人服务等于强制劳役，法院从不判特定履行，只能给钱或在有限情形下给消极禁令。第二件是禁令，分三阶：临时限制令（TRO，紧急性最强、期限最短）、初步禁令（保持现状到审结）、永久禁令（胜诉后的终局救济）。标准各有四要素。初步禁令看 Winter v. Natural Resources Defense Council, 555 U.S. 7 (2008)：胜诉可能性、若不发令将受不可弥补损害、利益平衡偏向申请人、禁令符合公共利益。永久禁令看 eBay Inc. v. MercExchange, L.L.C., 547 U.S. 388 (2006)：已受不可弥补损害、金钱等普通法救济不足、利益平衡偏向申请人、公共利益不被损害；胜诉本身不自动带来永久禁令。程序底盘是 Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 第 65 条（通知、内容、担保金），加州州法对应 Code of Civil Procedure 第 526 条的禁令事由与程序。考试常把两套四要素混着写，记住 Winter 管临时、eBay 管终局。

---

### English

Equity has two main tools. The first is specific performance: California Civil Code section 3384 allows a court to compel performance of a contract obligation except where statute provides otherwise, and Restatement (Second) sections 359 and 360 frame it — money must be inadequate, judged by factors such as difficulty of proving loss, difficulty of obtaining a substitute, and the defendant's likely inability to pay; land, treated as unique, satisfies adequacy almost automatically. The hard line is personal services: compelling a person to render personal services is involuntary servitude, so courts never order it, giving money or, in narrow settings, a negative injunction instead. The second tool is the injunction, in three stages: the temporary restraining order (most urgent, shortest-lived), the preliminary injunction (preserving the status quo until trial), and the permanent injunction (final relief after prevailing). Each has four factors. Preliminary relief follows Winter v. Natural Resources Defense Council, 555 U.S. 7 (2008): likelihood of success on the merits, likelihood of irreparable harm without relief, the balance of equities tipping in the movant's favor, and consistency with the public interest. Permanent relief follows eBay Inc. v. MercExchange, L.L.C., 547 U.S. 388 (2006): irreparable injury suffered, inadequacy of legal remedies such as money, a balance of hardships favoring the plaintiff, and no disservice to the public interest; winning on the merits does not automatically bring a permanent injunction. Procedure rests on Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 65 (notice, content, security), with California's counterpart in Code of Civil Procedure section 526. Exams love to blend the two four-factor tests — remember Winter governs the interim stage and eBay the final one.

## 五、返还、衡平抗辩与考试陷阱清单

### 中文（主文）

最后一块是返还与抗辩。返还（restitution）建立在不当得利（unjust enrichment）上：一方在应由对方承担的情事中得利，占着不还显失公平，法院命其返还价值，典型路径是准合同（quasi-contract，法律拟制的合同，不是真合同）。它常在合同无效、被撤销或原告自己违约后要拿回预付款时出场：哪怕合同打不成，预付的钱也不是白送，受领方须按得利返还，但违约原告的返还要扣掉对方已受的损失，别算成违约还全额退款。衡平的大门还有三道抗辩闸。一是迟延抗辩（laches）：无合理理由拖太久、又让对方处境变差，衡平诉求被挡，拖延长短参照对应制定法时效判断。二是不洁之手（unclean hands）：原告在同一交易里有不端行为，求衡平先要手干净。三是不合良心（unconscionability）：程序上被压着签、实体上苛刻到显失公平的条款，法院可拒绝执行。收尾记陷阱清单：惩罚性赔偿默认不进合同；个人服务不判特定履行；土地默认独特、量产商品默认不独特；约定金额先问预估合理性，再问加州 1671 的哪一面在管；初步禁令用 Winter、永久禁令用 eBay，两套四要素别互换；衡平问题永远先问一句，钱为什么不够。

---

### English

The last block is restitution and defenses. Restitution rests on unjust enrichment: where one party is enriched under circumstances that make retention unjust, the court orders the value returned, typically through quasi-contract — a contract implied in law, not a real contract. It appears when a contract is void or rescinded, or when a plaintiff in breach seeks a prepaid amount back: even if the deal fails, a prepayment is not a gift, and the recipient must restore the enrichment, though a breaching plaintiff's recovery is reduced by the loss caused, never a full refund that ignores the breach. Equity's gate has three defensive bars. Laches: unreasonable delay that prejudices the defendant blocks equitable relief, its length gauged against the analogous statute of limitations. Unclean hands: a plaintiff guilty of misconduct in the same transaction must come with clean hands to get equity. Unconscionability: terms procured by oppression and harsh enough to be substantively unfair may be refused enforcement. The closing trap list: punitive damages stay out of contract by default; personal services are never specifically enforced; land is presumed unique and mass-produced goods are not; for a liquidated sum ask first whether the estimate was reasonable, then which side of California section 1671 governs; Winter controls preliminary injunctions and eBay permanent ones, and the two four-factor tests are not interchangeable; and on every equity question, ask first why money is not enough.

### 한국어 (초역, 교정 대기)

마지막은 반환과 항변입니다. 반환(restitution)은 부당이득(unjust enrichment)에 기초합니다. 보유가 부당한 사정에서 이득을 얻은 자는 그 가치를 돌려줘야 하며, 전형적 경로는 준계약(quasi-contract)입니다. 진짜 계약이 아니라 법이 의제하는 계약입니다. 계약이 무효이거나 해제된 경우, 또 위반한 원고가 선급금 반환을 구하는 경우에 등장합니다. 선급금은 선물이 아니어서 돌려줘야 하지만, 위반한 원고의 반환에서는 그가 끼친 손해가 공제됩니다. 형평의 문에는 세 가지 항변이 있습니다. 지연 항변(laches)은 불합리한 지연과 상대방의 불이익이 있으면 형평 구제를 막고, 불결한 손(unclean hands)은 같은 거래에서 부정행위를 한 원고를 막으며, 양심에 반함(unconscionability)은 절차적 억압과 실체적 가혹함이 있는 조항의 집행을 거부하게 합니다. 함정 정리: 징벌적 배상은 계약에 들어오지 않고, 인적 서비스는 특정이행이 없으며, 토지는 유일하다고 추정되지만 대량생산품은 아닙니다. 예정액은 먼저 산정의 합리성, 그다음 캘리포니아 §1671의 어느 면이 지배하는지를 보고, 예비적 금지명령은 Winter, 영구적 금지명령은 eBay가 지배합니다. 형평 문제에서는 항상 돈으로 왜 부족한지부터 묻습니다. (학습용 초역, 교정 대기 중 — 初译待校)

### Tiếng Việt (bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

Cuối cùng là hoàn trả và các kháng biện. Hoàn trả (restitution) dựa trên làm giàu bất chính (unjust enrichment): ai được lợi trong hoàn cảnh mà việc giữ lại là bất công thì phải trả lại giá trị, thường qua hợp đồng giả định (quasi-contract) — hợp đồng do luật hư cấu, không phải hợp đồng thật. Nó xuất hiện khi hợp đồng vô hiệu hay bị hủy, hoặc khi chính nguyên đơn vi phạm đòi lại tiền ứng trước: tiền ứng không phải quà tặng nên phải hoàn, nhưng khoản hoàn của người vi phạm bị trừ phần thiệt hại họ gây ra. Cổng công bằng có ba kháng biện: chậm trễ (laches) — trì hoãn vô lý gây bất lợi cho bị đơn; tay không sạch (unclean hands) — nguyên đơn có hành vi sai trái trong cùng giao dịch; và vô lương tâm (unconscionability) — điều khoản bị ép ký và khắc nghiệt về nội dung có thể bị từ chối thi hành. Danh sách bẫy chốt: bồi thường trừng phạt không vào hợp đồng; dịch vụ cá nhân không bao giờ bị ép thực hiện; đất được suy đoán là duy nhất, hàng sản xuất hàng loạt thì không; với tiền ấn định, hỏi tính hợp lý của ước tính trước, rồi hỏi mặt nào của Điều 1671 California đang chi phối; Winter chi phối lệnh sơ bộ, eBay chi phối lệnh vĩnh viễn; và trong mọi câu hỏi công bằng, hỏi trước vì sao tiền không đủ. (Bản dịch học tập sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính — 初译待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित)

अंतिम भाग प्रत्यर्पण और प्रतिरक्षाओं का है। प्रत्यर्पण (restitution) अनुचित समृद्धि (unjust enrichment) पर आधारित है: जो पक्ष ऐसी परिस्थितियों में लाभ पाए कि उसे रखना अन्याय हो, उसे मूल्य लौटाना होगा, प्रायः अर्ध-अनुबंध (quasi-contract) के मार्ग से — यह विधि द्वारा कल्पित अनुबंध है, वास्तविक नहीं। यह शून्य या विखंडित अनुबंधों में, और उल्लंघनकर्ता वादी द्वारा अग्रिम राशि वापस माँगने पर सामने आता है: अग्रिम उपहार नहीं है, इसलिए लौटेगा, परंतु उल्लंघनकर्ता की वापसी से उसके द्वारा हुई हानि घटेगी। साम्या के द्वार पर तीन प्रतिरक्षाएँ हैं: विलंब (laches) — अयुक्तियुक्त देरी और प्रतिवादी को हुई हानि; अस्वच्छ हाथ (unclean hands) — उसी संव्यवहार में वादी का दुराचार; और अंतःकरण-विरुद्धता (unconscionability) — दबाव से ली गई और सारभूत रूप से कठोर शर्तों का प्रवर्तन रुक सकता है। अंतिम जाल-सूची: दंडात्मक क्षतिपूर्ति अनुबंध से बाहर रहती है; व्यक्तिगत सेवा का विशिष्ट पालन कभी नहीं; भूमि अद्वितीय मानी जाती है, बड़े पैमाने पर बनी वस्तुएँ नहीं; पूर्व-निर्धारित राशि पर पहले अनुमान की युक्तियुक्तता, फिर California धारा 1671 का कौन-सा पक्ष लागू है, यह देखिए; प्रारंभिक निषेधाज्ञा पर Winter और स्थायी पर eBay लागू है; और साम्या के हर प्रश्न में पहले पूछिए कि धन क्यों पर्याप्त नहीं है। (अध्ययन हेतु प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित — 初译待校)
