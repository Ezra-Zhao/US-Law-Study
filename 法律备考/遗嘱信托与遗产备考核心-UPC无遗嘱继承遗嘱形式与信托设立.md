# 遗嘱、信托与遗产备考核心：无遗嘱继承、遗嘱形式要件、撤销与信托设立

这篇讲什么：
- 这是律师考试 Wills, Trusts and Estates 科目的备考框架，只讲考试规则、例外与陷阱，不讲居民办理遗嘱或遗产的生活流程。主线是：先定有没有有效遗嘱（形式与撤销），再定谁能拿、拿多少（无遗嘱顺位、遗漏配偶与子女、杀人者规则），最后定信托怎么设、怎么管、债权人能不能碰（设立、挥霍限制、慈善信托）。
- 来源口径：以 Uniform Probate Code（UPC）现行文本第二编与 Uniform Trust Code（UTC）为统一法框架，州法对照以 California Probate Code 为主。金额门槛各州采纳时常有调整，做题以题干给定的州法为准。示例均为假设教学情形。

---

## 一、无遗嘱继承：配偶先分档，其余按顺位，生存要满 120 小时

### 中文（主文）

无遗嘱时先看配偶。UPC § 2-102 把配偶份额分档：无后裔、无父母存活时，配偶拿全部；无后裔但有父母存活时，先拿 300,000 美元再拿余额的四分之三；后裔全是配偶的后裔、且配偶没有其他后裔时，拿全部；后裔全是共同后裔但配偶另有后裔时，先拿 225,000 美元再拿余额的一半；只要有后裔不是配偶的后裔，先拿 150,000 美元再拿余额的一半。配偶拿完后，§ 2-103 定顺位：后裔、父母、兄弟姐妹及其后裔、祖父母一系。后裔内部看 § 2-106：UPC 采用按每一代人头均分（per capita at each generation），先在最近的有存活成员的一代均分，亡故者的份额汇入下一代再均分；不同于严格的按支分配（per stirpes）。加州不同：Probate Code § 6401 下，社区财产（community property）与准社区财产全部归配偶，个人财产（separate property）配偶拿二分之一或三分之一（后裔支数越多比例越低）；§ 6402 定顺位。生存门槛是硬规则：§ 2-104 要求继承人比被继承人多活 120 小时，不足者视为先死；加州无遗嘱继承的 120 小时规则在 § 6403（与 § 220 一系的生存规则相连），条号不要混。

---

### English

Intestacy starts with the spouse. UPC section 2-102 tiers the spouse's share: everything if no descendant or parent survives; if a parent survives but no descendant does, the first $300,000 plus three-fourths of the balance; if all of the decedent's descendants are also the spouse's and the spouse has no other descendant, everything; if the descendants are all shared but the spouse has another descendant, the first $225,000 plus one-half of the balance; and if any descendant is not the spouse's, the first $150,000 plus one-half of the balance. After the spouse, section 2-103 orders the takers: descendants, parents, siblings and their descendants, then grandparents and their issue. Within descendants, section 2-106 adopts per capita at each generation — equal shares at the nearest generation with a living member, deceased members' shares pooled and redivided at the next — not strict per stirpes, which locks shares by line. California differs: under Probate Code section 6401, community and quasi-community property pass entirely to the spouse, while the spouse takes one-half or one-third of separate property depending on the number of descendant lines; section 6402 sets the order. Survival is a hard gate: section 2-104 requires an heir to survive the decedent by 120 hours or be treated as having predeceased; California's 120-hour rule for intestacy is section 6403, tied to the survival rules around section 220.

El § 2-102 del UPC gradúa su cuota según queden progenitores y descendientes comunes o no: todo, o los primeros 300.000 dólares más tres cuartos del resto, o los primeros 225.000 o 150.000 dólares más la mitad del resto. Después manda el § 2-103: descendientes, progenitores, hermanos y su descendencia, abuelos y su descendencia. El § 2-104 exige sobrevivir al causante por 120 horas; la regla californiana está en el § 6403, ligada a las reglas de supervivencia del § 220.（初译待校）

### 한국어（初译待校）

무유언 상속은 배우자부터 봅니다. UPC § 2-102의 배우자 몫은 부모와 공동 후손의 유무에 따라 달라집니다. 전부, 또는 처음 300,000달러와 잔여분의 4분의 3, 또는 처음 225,000달러나 150,000달러와 잔여분의 절반입니다. 그다음 순위는 § 2-103이 정합니다. 후손, 부모, 형제자매와 그 후손, 조부모 계열 순입니다. 후손 내부 분배는 § 2-106의 세대별 균분(per capita at each generation)이며 엄격한 대습(per stirpes)과 다릅니다. 캘리포니아 Probate Code § 6401은 공동재산(community property) 전부를 배우자에게 주고 개인재산(separate property)은 배우자가 2분의 1 또는 3분의 1을 가져가게 하며, 순위는 § 6402가 정합니다. § 2-104는 피상속인보다 120시간 더 생존할 것을 요구하고, 캘리포니아의 120시간 규칙은 § 220 계열 생존 규칙과 연결되는 § 6403에 있습니다.（初译待校）

Thừa kế không di chúc bắt đầu từ người phối ngẫu. वसीयत-रहित उत्तराधिकार पति-पत्नी से शुरू होता है। UPC धारा 2-102 उसका हिस्सा इस आधार पर चरणों में बाँटती है कि माता-पिता और साझा वंशज हैं या नहीं: सब कुछ, या पहले $300,000 और शेष का तीन-चौथाई, या पहले $225,000 अथवा $150,000 और शेष का आधा। इसके बाद धारा 2-103 क्रम देती है: वंशज, माता-पिता, भाई-बहन और उनके वंशज, दादा-दादी वर्ग। वंशजों के भीतर धारा 2-106 प्रत्येक पीढ़ी में प्रति-व्यक्ति समान विभाजन (per capita at each generation) अपनाती है, कठोर per stirpes से भिन्न। California में Probate Code धारा 6401 सामुदायिक संपत्ति (community property) पूरी पति-पत्नी को देती है और पृथक संपत्ति (separate property) में आधा या एक-तिहाई; क्रम धारा 6402 तय करती है। धारा 2-104 मृतक से 120 घंटे अधिक जीना माँगती है, और California का 120-घंटे का नियम धारा 6403 में है, जो धारा 220 के आसपास की उत्तरजीविता-व्यवस्था से जुड़ा है। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद — 初译待校)

## 二、遗嘱形式：见证遗嘱、全手写遗嘱、无害错误与利害见证人

### 中文（主文）

见证遗嘱看 UPC § 2-502(a)：书面，立遗嘱人签名（或他人在其有意识在场并受其指示时代签），两名见证人亲见签名或听其确认并在合理时间内签名。全手写遗嘱走 § 2-502(b)：签名与实质内容（material portions）为立遗嘱人亲笔即可，无需见证人，争点通常在手写部分是否足以显示遗嘱意思。加州对应 Probate Code § 6110（见证遗嘱：书面、签名、两名见证人同时在场见证并在立遗嘱人面前签名）与 § 6111（手写遗嘱：签名与实质条款亲笔，无需见证与日期，但无日期又与其他遗嘱冲突时危险）。UPC 独有 § 2-503 无害错误规则（harmless error）：文件未按 § 2-502 作成，只要主张人以明确而令人信服的证据（clear and convincing evidence）证明立遗嘱人意在使其成为遗嘱、撤销或增改，可按合规作成处理；加州没有一般性的对应规则，形式有缺多半无效，这是最爱考的差别。见证人利益看加州 § 6112：给见证人本人的赠与产生其以欺诈、胁迫或不当影响取得的推定，推翻不了的，该见证人的受赠以其在无遗嘱时可得的份额为限，遗嘱本身存续；另有至少两名无利害见证人签名的，不生此推定。

### English

Attested wills follow UPC section 2-502(a): a writing, signed by the testator (or by another in the testator's conscious presence and at the testator's direction), with two witnesses who saw the signing or heard the acknowledgment and signed within a reasonable time. Holographic wills under section 2-502(b) need no witnesses: the signature and material portions must be in the testator's handwriting. California's counterparts are Probate Code section 6110 (attested will: writing, signature, two witnesses present at the same time signing in the testator's presence) and section 6111 (holographic will: signature and material provisions handwritten; no witness or date required, though an undated holograph conflicting with another will is risky). Only the UPC has harmless error, section 2-503: a document failing section 2-502 may be treated as duly executed if the proponent proves by clear and convincing evidence that the testator intended it as a will, revocation, or alteration; California has no general analogue, so formal defects there are usually fatal — a favorite exam contrast. Under California section 6112, a devise to an attesting witness raises a presumption of fraud, duress, or undue influence; if unrebutted, the witness takes no more than an intestate share, and the presumption never arises if at least two other disinterested witnesses signed.

### Español（初译待校）

El testamento con testigos sigue el § 2-502(a) del UPC: escrito, firmado por el testador (o por otro en su presencia consciente y por su orden), con dos testigos que vieron la firma o el reconocimiento y firmaron en plazo razonable. El ológrafo del § 2-502(b) no exige testigos: firma y partes sustanciales (material portions) autógrafas. En California, el § 6110 exige dos testigos presentes a la vez que firman ante el testador, y el § 6111 valida el ológrafo autógrafo sin testigos ni fecha, aunque sin fecha y en conflicto con otro testamento es riesgoso. Solo el UPC tiene el error inocuo (harmless error), § 2-503: el documento defectuoso puede valer si se prueba con evidencia clara y convincente que el testador lo quiso como testamento, revocación o modificación; California no tiene regla general equivalente. Según el § 6112, lo dado a un testigo se presume obtenido por fraude, coacción o influencia indebida; sin refutación, ese testigo no toma más que su cuota intestada, y no hay presunción si firmaron otros dos testigos desinteresados.（初译待校）

### 한국어（初译待校）

증인 유언은 UPC § 2-502(a)를 따릅니다. 서면, 유언자의 서명(또는 의식 있는 상태로 지켜보는 가운데 그 지시에 따른 대리 서명), 서명이나 인정을 목격하고 합리적인 기간 안에 서명한 증인 두 명이 필요합니다. § 2-502(b)의 자필 유언은 증인이 필요 없습니다. 서명과 실질 부분(material portions)이 자필이면 됩니다. 캘리포니아 Probate Code § 6110은 두 증인의 동시 입회와 유언자 앞 서명을, § 6111은 서명과 실질 조항이 자필인 자필 유언을 증인과 날짜 없이 인정하되 날짜가 없고 다른 유언과 충돌하면 위험합니다. UPC에만 있는 § 2-503의 무해한 오류(harmless error) 규칙은 뜻이 명백하고 설득력 있는 증거로 입증되면 흠 있는 문서도 유언으로 취급합니다. 캘리포니아에는 상응하는 일반 규칙이 없습니다. § 6112에 따르면 증인 본인에 대한 증여는 사기·강박·부당 영향의 추정을 받으며, 깨지 못하면 그 증인은 무유언 상속분 이상을 받지 못합니다. 이해관계 없는 증인이 두 명 더 서명했다면 추정은 생기지 않습니다.（初译待校）

## 三、撤销与复活：行为、后遗嘱与离婚

### 中文（主文）

撤销看 UPC § 2-507，路有两条。一是后遗嘱：明示撤销，或与前遗嘱不一致而在不一致范围内撤销；后遗嘱把财产分光的，视为全部撤销前遗嘱。二是行为撤销：立遗嘱人以撤销意图亲自烧、撕、划、毁遗嘱，或在场并指示他人代行。被撤销的遗嘱能否复活看 § 2-509：第二份遗嘱被行为撤销的，第一份只有在情事或立遗嘱人声明显示其有意让第一份生效时才复活；第二份是被第三份撤销的，看第三份是否表明复活第一份的意思。离婚是自动撤销的典型：§ 2-804 规定离婚或婚姻无效后，遗嘱中给前配偶的财产处置、执行人与受托人任命等可撤销安排一律失效，按前配偶已放弃（disclaimed）处理，除非遗嘱另有明示；该条还及于部分非遗嘱转移。加州 Probate Code § 6122 口径相近。再婚或重新立嘱可以改变结果，题目给出再婚事实时别漏。

---

### English

Revocation follows UPC section 2-507 by two routes. A later will revokes expressly, or by inconsistency to the extent of the conflict; a later will disposing of the entire estate is presumed to revoke the earlier one completely. Physical-act revocation requires the testator — or another person in the testator's presence and at the testator's direction — to burn, tear, cancel, obliterate, or destroy the will with intent to revoke. Revival is section 2-509: if the second will is revoked by physical act, the first revives only if the circumstances or the testator's statements show an intent that it take effect; if the second is revoked by a third will, revival depends on what the third will manifests. Divorce revokes automatically: under section 2-804, divorce or annulment revokes revocable dispositions to the former spouse and appointments of the former spouse as executor or trustee, the former spouse being treated as having disclaimed, unless the will expressly provides otherwise; the section reaches some nonprobate transfers as well. California Probate Code section 6122 is parallel. Remarriage or a new will can change the outcome.

### Español（初译待校）

La revocación sigue el § 2-507 del UPC por dos vías. Un testamento posterior revoca expresamente o por incompatibilidad hasta donde alcanza el conflicto; si dispone de todo el caudal, se presume revocación completa. La revocación por acto material exige que el testador — u otra persona en su presencia y por su orden — queme, rompa, tache o destruya el testamento con intención de revocarlo. La reviviscencia es del § 2-509: revocado el segundo testamento por acto material, el primero solo revive si las circunstancias o las declaraciones del testador muestran que quiso darle efecto; si lo revocó un tercer testamento, manda lo que este manifieste. El divorcio revoca automáticamente: según el § 2-804, el divorcio o la nulidad revocan las disposiciones revocables a favor del excónyuge y sus nombramientos como albacea o fiduciario, tratándolo como si hubiera renunciado (disclaimed), salvo disposición expresa en contrario, y la norma alcanza algunas transferencias fuera del testamento. El § 6122 de California es paralelo. Un nuevo matrimonio o un nuevo testamento pueden cambiar el resultado.（初译待校）

## 四、遗漏与保护：遗漏配偶与子女、杀害者规则与同时死亡

### 中文（主文）

遗嘱立好之后才结婚、才生孩子，遗嘱没改，法律给补位规则。遗漏配偶（omitted spouse）看 UPC § 2-301：立遗嘱后结婚的配偶，原则上取得如同无遗嘱时应得的份额，但只从未留给立遗嘱人在结婚前所生子女的那部分遗产里出；遗嘱表明是预见婚姻而立、明示有意不给，或已在遗嘱外给该配偶财产以代替遗嘱安排的，都除外。遗漏子女（omitted children）看 § 2-302：立遗嘱后出生或收养的子女，立遗嘱当时无子女的，原则上取得无遗嘱份额（遗嘱几乎全给该子女另一方父/母等情形除外）；当时已有子女并给了他们的，遗漏子女只能按已有子女所得计算的受限份额参加分配，避免推翻原安排。两种遗漏都看可推断意图，有明示排除就别硬套公式。杀害者规则（slayer rule）在 § 2-803：故意且重罪性地杀害被继承人者（feloniously and intentionally kills），其无遗嘱份额、遗嘱受益及其他相关利益一律撤销，按其先于死者死亡处理；无须先有刑事定罪，民事程序中以优势证据认定即可。同时死亡看 Uniform Simultaneous Death Act 与 § 2-104 的 120 小时规则：是否多活 120 小时须以明确而令人信服的证据证明，证不出的视为先死。预先放弃继承（disclaimer）另看 § 2-1105：继承人作出合格放弃的（一般须在被继承人死亡后九个月内），视为先于被继承人死亡，财产跳过放弃人按下一顺位分配。

### English

A will made before a marriage or a birth does not update itself; omitted-family rules fill the gap. For an omitted spouse, UPC section 2-301 gives a spouse married after execution an intestate share, taken only from the portion not devised to the testator's children born before the marriage — unless the will was made in contemplation of the marriage, expressly omits the spouse, or the testator provided for the spouse outside the will in lieu of a testamentary share. For omitted children, section 2-302 gives a child born or adopted after execution an intestate share if the testator had no child when the will was made, subject to exceptions such as leaving substantially everything to the child's other parent; if the testator already had children and devised to them, the omitted child takes only a limited share computed from the children's provisions. Both rules yield to manifested intent. The slayer rule, section 2-803, strips a person who feloniously and intentionally kills the decedent of intestate, testamentary, and related benefits, treating the killer as having predeceased; no conviction is required, because the killing may be established civilly by a preponderance. Simultaneous death is handled by the Uniform Simultaneous Death Act and section 2-104's 120-hour rule: survival by 120 hours must be proved by clear and convincing evidence, failing which the person is treated as having predeceased. A qualified disclaimer under section 2-1105 — generally made within nine months after the decedent's death — relates back, so the disclaimant is treated as having predeceased and the interest passes to the next takers.

## 五、信托设立与类型：生前信托、遗嘱信托与挥霍信托

### 中文（主文）

信托设立要素：委托人（settlor）有行为能力并有设立意图、受益人确定（慈善信托等例外除外）、目的合法、受托人（trustee）负担信义义务；生前信托还须把财产实际转入信托（funding），没转入的财产不受信托支配。可撤销生前信托（revocable living trust）最常考：委托人生前可修改、可撤销，死亡时转为不可撤销，主要价值是避开遗嘱认证（probate），但一般不避税、也不隔绝委托人自己的债权人。遗嘱信托（testamentary trust）写在遗嘱里，须经遗嘱认证才生效并受法院监督。挥霍限制条款（spendthrift）看 UTC § 502：禁止受益人转让、债权人原则上够不着；但 § 503 开了例外：受益人的子女、配偶或前配偶持扶养判决的、为受益人提供必需品的债权人，以及联邦与州的税收债权，可以穿透该限制。慈善信托可永久存续，目的不能实现时 UTC § 413 的近似原则（cy pres）允许法院把财产转用于近似的慈善目的。加州三条必记：Probate Code § 15200 列明信托设立方法（签署书面、向受托人转让财产并签署文件等）；§§ 15401–15402 定可撤销信托的撤销与修改方法（按信托文件指定的方法，或签署书面并交付受托人）；§ 16061.7 要求可撤销信托因委托人死亡而变为不可撤销时，受托人在 60 天内通知受益人与继承人，通知启动 120 天异议期。

### English

A trust needs a settlor with capacity and intent, a definite beneficiary (subject to exceptions such as charitable trusts), a lawful purpose, and a trustee owing fiduciary duties; a living trust controls only property actually transferred into it (funding). The most tested form is the revocable living trust: the settlor may amend or revoke it during life, it becomes irrevocable at death, and its main payoff is avoiding probate — it generally saves no taxes and does not shield assets from the settlor's own creditors. A testamentary trust is created in a will, takes effect only through probate, and remains under court supervision. Spendthrift clauses are validated by UTC section 502, but section 503 lets certain claimants reach the beneficiary's interest despite the restraint: a child, spouse, or former spouse with a support judgment, a creditor who provided necessaries, and federal or state tax claims. A charitable trust may last indefinitely, and when its purpose becomes impossible or impracticable, UTC section 413 (cy pres) lets a court redirect the property to a purpose as near as possible. Three California anchors: Probate Code section 15200 lists the methods of creating an express trust, including a signed writing or a transfer to the trustee; sections 15401–15402 govern revocation and modification of a revocable trust, by the method stated in the instrument or by a signed writing delivered to the trustee; and section 16061.7 requires the trustee, when a revocable trust becomes irrevocable on the settlor's death, to notify beneficiaries and heirs within 60 days, the notice starting a 120-day contest period.

### Español（初译待校）

Un fideicomiso (trust) exige un constituyente (settlor) capaz y con intención de crearlo, un beneficiario determinado (salvo excepciones como el fideicomiso caritativo), un fin lícito y un fiduciario (trustee) con deberes fiduciarios; el fideicomiso en vida solo alcanza los bienes efectivamente transferidos a él (funding). ## 六、反未来利益规则（RAP）：普通法 21 年与 USRAP 90 年

### 中文（主文）

普通法反未来利益规则（Rule Against Perpetuities）一句话：任何权益若要归属，必须在设立时在世之人（life in being）死亡后 21 年内确定归属，否则自始无效，判断时点在权益设立之时、不看后来实际发生什么。三大经典陷阱：高龄者推定（fertile octogenarian，八十岁的人也被视为仍能生育）、未出生遗孀（unborn widow，量尺生命在设立时可能还没出生）、行政性条件拖延归属。改革法 USRAP 改走等待观察（wait-and-see）：权益要么在普通法期间内实际归属，要么在设立后 90 年内归属，满足其一即有效；UPC 在 §§ 2-901 以下采纳了 USRAP 框架。加州同样采 USRAP，见 Probate Code §§ 21200 以下，其中 § 21205 定 90 年期间。答题第一步先问：题目管辖区用普通法还是 USRAP？量尺用错，全题皆错。

---

### English

The common-law Rule Against Perpetuities is one sentence: an interest is void unless it must vest, if at all, within 21 years after some life in being at its creation — judged at creation, not by what actually happens later. The classic traps are the fertile octogenarian (even an eighty-year-old is conclusively presumed able to have children), the unborn widow (the measuring life may not yet be born at creation), and administrative contingencies that delay vesting. USRAP reforms the rule with wait-and-see: an interest is valid if it actually vests within the common-law period or within 90 years after its creation; the UPC adopts the USRAP framework at sections 2-901 et seq. California likewise follows USRAP, Probate Code sections 21200 et seq., with section 21205 supplying the 90-year period. The first question on any problem is which regime the jurisdiction uses.

## 七、考试陷阱小结

### 中文（主文）

过一遍最容易丢分的地方：①120 小时规则在无遗嘱里是 UPC § 2-104、加州 § 6403，不是其他条号；②per stirpes 是按支锁定，per capita at each generation 是逐代汇合再均分，UPC 用后者；③加州见证遗嘱要两名见证人同时在场，且没有 UPC § 2-503 的无害错误救济；④手写遗嘱看签名与实质部分是否亲笔，无日期又与他嘱冲突最危险；⑤离婚自动撤销给前配偶的条款（UPC § 2-804、加州 § 6122），再婚可翻转；⑥杀人者规则不以刑事定罪为前提，民事优势证据即可（§ 2-803）；⑦disclaimer 有九个月的合格期限（§ 2-1105）；⑧生前信托没 funding 等于没设到那项财产上；⑨spendthrift 挡不住扶养与税收债权人（UTC § 503）；⑩RAP 先分清普通法还是 USRAP，再选 21 年加在世生命还是 90 年的量尺。

### English

A final pass over the easiest points to lose: (1) the 120-hour rule is UPC section 2-104 and California section 6403, not any neighboring number; (2) per stirpes locks shares by line, while per capita at each generation pools and redivides at each generation — the UPC uses the latter; (3) a California attested will needs two witnesses present at the same time, and California has no section 2-503 harmless-error rescue; (4) a holograph turns on whether the signature and material portions are handwritten, and an undated holograph conflicting with another will is the danger case; (5) divorce automatically revokes provisions for a former spouse (UPC section 2-804; California section 6122), and remarriage can reverse that; (6) the slayer rule needs no criminal conviction — a civil preponderance suffices (section 2-803); (7) a qualified disclaimer has a nine-month clock (section 2-1105); (8) an unfunded living trust controls nothing; (9) spendthrift protection does not stop support or tax claimants (UTC section 503); and (10) in every perpetuities problem, choose the regime first — common-law lives in being plus 21 years, or the USRAP 90 years.

### Tiếng Việt（初译待校）

Điểm lại những chỗ dễ mất điểm nhất: (1) quy tắc 120 giờ là Điều 2-104 UPC và Điều 6403 California; (2) per stirpes khóa phần theo nhánh, còn per capita at each generation gộp lại và chia đều theo từng thế hệ — UPC dùng cách sau; (3) di chúc có chứng của California cần hai người làm chứng có mặt cùng lúc và không có cứu chữa lỗi vô hại kiểu Điều 2-503; (4) di chúc viết tay xoay quanh chữ ký và các phần trọng yếu có phải viết tay hay không, và bản không ghi ngày mà mâu thuẫn với di chúc khác là trường hợp nguy hiểm; (5) ly hôn tự động hủy bỏ các điều khoản cho vợ/chồng cũ (Điều 2-804 UPC; Điều 6122 California), tái hôn có thể đảo ngược; (6) quy tắc kẻ sát nhân không cần bản án hình sự, chỉ cần preponderance dân sự (Điều 2-803); (7) disclaimer có thời hạn chín tháng (Điều 2-1105); (8) tín thác sống chưa funding thì không chi phối gì cả; (9) điều khoản spendthrift không chặn được chủ nợ cấp dưỡng và thuế (Điều 503 UTC); (10) bài toán RAP phải chọn chế độ trước: thông luật — người đang sống cộng 21 năm — hay USRAP 90 năm.（初译待校）

### हिन्दी（初译待校）

सबसे महँगी भूलों का पुनरावलोकन: (1) 120 घंटे का नियम UPC धारा 2-104 और California धारा 6403 है; (2) per stirpes हिस्से शाखा अनुसार बंद करता है, जबकि per capita at each generation हर पीढ़ी में जोड़कर फिर बाँटता है — UPC यही अपनाता है; (3) California की साक्ष्यांकित वसीयत को दो साक्षियों की एक साथ उपस्थिति चाहिए और धारा 2-503 जैसी हानिरहित-त्रुटि छूट वहाँ नहीं है; (4) हस्तलिखित वसीयत हस्ताक्षर और सारभूत भागों के स्वयं-लिखित होने पर निर्भर है, और बिना तिथि वाली वसीयत का दूसरी वसीयत से टकराव सबसे ख़तरनाक है; (5) तलाक पूर्व जीवनसाथी के प्रावधान स्वतः निरस्त करता है (UPC धारा 2-804; California धारा 6122), पुनर्विवाह इसे पलट सकता है; (6) हत्यारा नियम को आपराधिक दोषसिद्धि नहीं चाहिए, दीवानी प्राबल्य पर्याप्त है (धारा 2-803); (7) disclaimer की नौ महीने की समय-सीमा है (धारा 2-1105); (8) बिना funding का जीवित न्यास कुछ नियंत्रित नहीं करता; (9) spendthrift संरक्षण भरण-पोषण और कर दावेदारों को नहीं रोकता (UTC धारा 503); (10) RAP में पहले व्यवस्था चुनें: सामान्य-कानून — जीवित जीवन + 21 वर्ष — या USRAP के 90 वर्ष। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद — 初译待校)
