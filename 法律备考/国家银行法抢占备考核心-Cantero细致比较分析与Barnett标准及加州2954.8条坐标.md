# 国家银行法抢占备考核心：Cantero 细致比较分析、Barnett 标准与加州第 2954.8 条坐标

> 板块：法律备考｜主题：州消费者金融法碰上国民银行，先例抢占到底怎么判——本篇管 Cantero 规则：《多德-弗兰克法》把 Barnett Bank 的标准写进法典，州法只有在阻止或显著干扰国民银行行使权力时才被抢占，而且判断必须做细致的比较分析，不许用一刀切的类别检验。本篇与《Cantero v. Bank of America, 602 U.S. 205 (2024) 案例分析》同题成对：案例篇管案情与诉讼经过，本篇只管答题流水线。

## 一、先认标准：法典条文、Barnett 门槛与被否掉的一刀切检验

### 中文

标准一句话：规制国民银行的州消费者金融法，只有在阻止或显著干扰国民银行行使其权力时，才被《国家银行法》抢占。出处分两层。成文层是《多德-弗兰克法》写进 12 U.S.C. 第 25b 条 (b) 款 (1) 项 (B) 目的规则：州消费者金融法被抢占的情形之一，是依照 Barnett Bank of Marion County, N.A. v. Nelson, 517 U.S. 25 (1996) 的法律标准，该法阻止或显著干扰国民银行行使权力。判例层就是 Barnett 本身：它没有画一条明线，而是要求对州法干扰的性质与程度做实际评估。Cantero v. Bank of America, 602 U.S. 205 (2024) 的增量在方法：第二巡回曾提炼出一条类别检验——州法只要会对国民银行设立与注资托管账户的权力施加控制，就被抢占；最高法院一致指出，这种检验会把几乎所有规制国民银行的州法都抢占掉，与国会明文采纳的 Barnett 标准不合。答题时先写清两件事：第一，《多德-弗兰克法》已经排除了领域抢占，国民银行不是一进州境就免疫；第二，抢占判断是程度问题，不是州法有没有碰到银行权力的问题。算例：2024－1996＝28，Barnett 标准立了 28 年后被 Cantero 重新定型；2026－2024＝2，Cantero 判决已满 2 年。

### English

The standard in one sentence: a state consumer financial law regulating national banks is preempted under the National Bank Act only if it prevents or significantly interferes with the national bank's exercise of its powers. The authority has two layers. The statutory layer is Dodd-Frank, codified at 12 U.S.C. section 25b(b)(1)(B): a state consumer financial law is preempted if, in accordance with the legal standard in Barnett Bank of Marion County, N.A. v. Nelson, 517 U.S. 25 (1996), it prevents or significantly interferes with the exercise by the national bank of its powers. The case-law layer is Barnett itself, which drew no bright line and instead requires a practical assessment of the nature and degree of the interference. Cantero v. Bank of America, 602 U.S. 205 (2024), adds the method: the Second Circuit had distilled a categorical test under which a state law was preempted if it would exert control over a national bank's power to create and fund escrow accounts; a unanimous Court held that such a test would preempt virtually all state laws regulating national banks and is inconsistent with the Barnett standard Congress expressly incorporated. Two points to write first in any answer: Dodd-Frank ruled out field preemption, so a national bank is not immune the moment it crosses a state line; and preemption turns on degree of interference, not on whether the state law touches a bank power at all. Arithmetic: 2024 - 1996 = 28 years from Barnett to Cantero; 2026 - 2024 = 2 years since Cantero.

### Español (初译待校)

El estándar en una frase: una ley estatal de protección financiera al consumidor que regula a los bancos nacionales solo queda desplazada por la Ley de Banca Nacional si impide o interfiere significativamente en el ejercicio de las facultades del banco nacional. La autoridad tiene dos capas. La capa legal es Dodd-Frank, codificada en 12 U.S.C. § 25b(b)(1)(B): una ley estatal queda desplazada si, conforme al estándar jurídico de Barnett Bank of Marion County, N.A. v. Nelson, 517 U.S. 25 (1996), impide o interfiere significativamente en el ejercicio de las facultades del banco nacional. La capa jurisprudencial es el propio Barnett, que no trazó una línea clara y exige una evaluación práctica de la naturaleza y el grado de la interferencia. Cantero v. Bank of America, 602 U.S. 205 (2024), aporta el método: el Segundo Circuito había destilado una prueba categórica según la cual una ley estatal quedaba desplazada si ejercía control sobre la facultad del banco de crear y financiar cuentas de depósito en garantía; la Corte, por unanimidad, sostuvo que esa prueba desplazaría prácticamente todas las leyes estatales que regulan a los bancos nacionales y es incompatible con el estándar de Barnett que el Congreso incorporó expresamente. Dos puntos para escribir primero: Dodd-Frank descartó el desplazamiento por campo ocupado, así que el banco nacional no es inmune al cruzar la frontera estatal; y el desplazamiento depende del grado de interferencia, no de que la ley toque o no una facultad bancaria. Cuenta: 2024 - 1996 = 28 años de Barnett a Cantero; 2026 - 2024 = 2 años desde Cantero. (Traducción inicial, pendiente de revisión)

## 二、比较谱系怎么摆与加州坐标

### 中文

细致比较分析的操作，就是把待判州法放到 Barnett 谱系里找位置。被抢占一侧的参照案是 Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954)、Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982) 与 Barnett 本身：这些案子里的州法对银行权力的干预程度深，落到禁止或实质改变权力行使方式的量级。未被抢占一侧的参照案是 Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944)、National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870) 与 McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896)：州法只是给银行权力加了一层日常规制，银行照样能行使权力。答题句式固定：先定性待判州法，再问它更像哪一侧——更像 Franklin、Fidelity、Barnett 一侧就被抢占，更像 Anderson、Commonwealth、McClellan 一侧就不被抢占；只写结论不摆谱系，等于没做 Cantero 要求的那一步。还要注意 Cantero 本身没有裁定纽约托管利息法是否被抢占，它撤销的是第二巡回的判决、发回按正确标准重判，引用时别把方法判决写成实体判决。

加州坐标（单独成段）：加州有同型法律。《民法典》第 2954.8 条要求对一至四户住宅贷款收受税款与保险预付款的金融机构，按年单利至少 2% 向借款人付托管利息，每年或账户终止时计入。它的抢占命运在 Cantero 之前已有一站：Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018) 认定第 2954.8 条不阻止或显著干扰国民银行权力、未被抢占，最高法院随后拒绝调卷。所以加州题的答法是双层：实体上先引 Lusnak 的现行结论，方法上再说明 Lusnak 走的正是 Cantero 后来要求的比较路线——把州法干预程度与先例谱系逐项对照，而不是问州法有没有控制银行权力。

### English

The nuanced comparative analysis works by placing the challenged state law on the Barnett spectrum. On the preempted side stand Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954), Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982), and Barnett itself, where the state laws interfered deeply enough to bar or substantially reshape the exercise of a bank power. On the not-preempted side stand Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944), National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870), and McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896), where the state law added an ordinary regulatory layer and the bank could still exercise its powers. The exam formula is fixed: characterize the state law, then ask which side it is more akin to — Franklin, Fidelity and Barnett mean preempted; Anderson, Commonwealth and McClellan mean not preempted. A conclusion without the spectrum skips the very step Cantero requires. Note also that Cantero did not itself decide whether New York's interest-on-escrow law is preempted; it vacated the Second Circuit's judgment and remanded for analysis under the correct standard, so do not cite a methodology holding as a merits holding. California coordinate: Civil Code section 2954.8 requires a financial institution that receives advance payments for taxes and insurance on a loan secured by a one-to-four-family residence to pay the borrower interest of at least 2 percent simple interest per annum, credited annually or on termination of the account. Its preemption fate was tested before Cantero: Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018), held section 2954.8 not preempted because it does not prevent or significantly interfere with national bank powers, and the Supreme Court denied certiorari. A California answer therefore runs on two levels: Lusnak supplies the current merits answer, and its method — comparing the degree of interference against the precedent spectrum rather than asking whether the law controls a bank power — is exactly the route Cantero later required.

### Español (初译待校)

El análisis comparativo matizado consiste en ubicar la ley estatal impugnada en el espectro de Barnett. Del lado del desplazamiento están Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954), Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982), y el propio Barnett, donde las leyes interferían con la profundidad suficiente para impedir o remodelar sustancialmente el ejercicio de una facultad bancaria. Del lado contrario están Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944), National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870), y McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896), donde la ley estatal solo añadía una capa ordinaria de regulación y el banco podía seguir ejerciendo sus facultades. La fórmula de examen es fija: caracterice la ley y pregunte a qué lado se parece más; Franklin, Fidelity y Barnett significan desplazamiento, Anderson, Commonwealth y McClellan significan que no lo hay. Una conclusión sin el espectro se salta justo el paso que Cantero exige. Además, Cantero no decidió si la ley neoyorquina de intereses sobre depósitos en garantía está desplazada: anuló la sentencia del Segundo Circuito y devolvió el caso para un nuevo análisis con el estándar correcto. Coordenada de California: el § 2954.8 del Código Civil exige a la institución financiera que recibe pagos anticipados de impuestos y seguros en un préstamo garantizado por una vivienda de una a cuatro familias pagar al prestatario un interés de al menos el 2 por ciento anual simple, acreditado cada año o al cerrar la cuenta. Su destino en materia de desplazamiento ya se litigó antes de Cantero: Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018), sostuvo que el § 2954.8 no está desplazado porque no impide ni interfiere significativamente en las facultades de los bancos nacionales, y la Corte Suprema denegó el certiorari. ### 한국어 (초역, 교정 필요)

세밀한 비교 분석은 다투어지는 주법을 Barnett 스펙트럼 위에 올려놓는 작업입니다. 선점되는 쪽의 참조 판례는 Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954), Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982), 그리고 Barnett 자체로, 이들 사건의 주법은 은행 권한의 행사를 막거나 실질적으로 바꾸는 수준까지 깊이 개입했습니다. 선점되지 않는 쪽은 Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944), National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870), McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896)으로, 주법이 일상적 규제 층을 하나 더했을 뿐 은행은 권한을 그대로 행사할 수 있었습니다. 답안 공식은 고정입니다. 주법을 규정하고 어느 쪽에 더 가까운지 묻는 것입니다. Franklin, Fidelity, Barnett 쪽이면 선점, Anderson, Commonwealth, McClellan 쪽이면 선점이 아닙니다. 스펙트럼 없이 결론만 쓰면 Cantero가 요구한 바로 그 단계를 건너뛴 셈입니다. 또한 Cantero는 뉴욕 에스크로 이자법의 선점 여부를 직접 결정하지 않았고, 제2순회 판결을 파기하고 올바른 기준으로 다시 분석하라고 환송했으므로 방법론 판결을 실체 판결로 인용하면 안 됩니다. 캘리포니아 좌표는 이렇습니다. 민법전 제2954.8조는 1~4가구 주택 담보 대출에서 세금과 보험료 선납금을 받는 금융기관이 차입자에게 연 단리 최소 2%의 에스크로 이자를 지급하고 매년 또는 계좌 종료 시 가산하도록 요구합니다. 그 선점 운명은 Cantero 이전에 이미 다투어졌습니다. Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018)는 제2954.8조가 국립은행 권한을 막거나 현저히 방해하지 않아 선점되지 않는다고 했고, 대법원은 상고허가를 기각했습니다. (초역, 교정 필요)

### Tiếng Việt (初译待校)

Phân tích so sánh tinh tế là đặt đạo luật bang bị thách thức lên phổ Barnett. Phía bị chiếm chỗ gồm Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954), Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982), và chính Barnett, nơi các đạo luật bang can thiệp đủ sâu để ngăn cản hoặc định hình lại đáng kể việc thực thi một quyền hạn ngân hàng. Phía không bị chiếm chỗ gồm Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944), National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870), và McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896), nơi luật bang chỉ thêm một lớp điều tiết thông thường và ngân hàng vẫn thực thi được quyền hạn. Công thức làm bài cố định: định tính đạo luật bang rồi hỏi nó giống phía nào hơn — giống Franklin, Fidelity và Barnett thì bị chiếm chỗ; giống Anderson, Commonwealth và McClellan thì không. Kết luận mà không bày phổ là bỏ qua đúng bước Cantero yêu cầu. Cũng cần lưu ý Cantero tự nó không quyết định luật lãi ký quỹ của New York có bị chiếm chỗ hay không; Tòa hủy phán quyết của Khu vực 2 và chuyển trả để phân tích lại theo tiêu chuẩn đúng. Tọa độ California: Mục 2954.8 của Bộ luật Dân sự yêu cầu tổ chức tài chính nhận các khoản trả trước về thuế và bảo hiểm đối với khoản vay thế chấp bằng nhà ở một đến bốn hộ gia đình phải trả cho người vay lãi ít nhất 2 phần trăm lãi đơn mỗi năm, ghi có hằng năm hoặc khi đóng tài khoản. Số phận chiếm chỗ của nó đã được xét trước Cantero: Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018), phán Mục 2954.8 không bị chiếm chỗ vì không ngăn cản hay can thiệp đáng kể vào quyền hạn của ngân hàng quốc gia, và Tòa Tối cao đã bác đơn xin xét lại. (Bản dịch sơ bộ, chờ hiệu đính)

### हिन्दी (初译待校)

सूक्ष्म तुलनात्मक विश्लेषण का अर्थ है चुनौती दिए गए राज्य कानून को Barnett स्पेक्ट्रम पर रखना। पूर्व-अधिग्रहित पक्ष में Franklin National Bank v. New York, 347 U.S. 373 (1954), Fidelity Federal Savings and Loan Association v. de la Cuesta, 458 U.S. 141 (1982), और स्वयं Barnett हैं, जहाँ राज्य कानून इतनी गहराई से हस्तक्षेप करते थे कि बैंक-शक्ति का प्रयोग रुक जाए या उसका स्वरूप ही बदल जाए। गैर-पूर्व-अधिग्रहित पक्ष में Anderson National Bank v. Luckett, 321 U.S. 233 (1944), National Bank v. Commonwealth, 76 U.S. 353 (1870), और McClellan v. Chipman, 164 U.S. 347 (1896) हैं, जहाँ राज्य कानून ने केवल एक सामान्य विनियामक परत जोड़ी और बैंक अपनी शक्तियों का प्रयोग कर सकता रहा। परीक्षा-सूत्र निश्चित है: राज्य कानून का चरित्र बताइए और पूछिए वह किस पक्ष के अधिक समान है — Franklin, Fidelity और Barnett पक्ष का अर्थ है पूर्व-अधिग्रहण; Anderson, Commonwealth और McClellan पक्ष का अर्थ है नहीं। स्पेक्ट्रम रखे बिना केवल निष्कर्ष लिखना वही चरण छोड़ना है जो Cantero माँगता है। यह भी ध्यान दें कि Cantero ने स्वयं यह तय नहीं किया कि New York का एस्क्रो-ब्याज कानून पूर्व-अधिग्रहित है या नहीं; उसने द्वितीय सर्किट का निर्णय रद्द करके सही मानक से पुनः विश्लेषण के लिए मामला लौटाया। California निर्देशांक: नागरिक संहिता धारा 2954.8 के तहत, एक से चार परिवारों वाले आवास से सुरक्षित ऋण पर करों और बीमा के अग्रिम भुगतान लेने वाले वित्तीय संस्थान को उधारकर्ता को कम से कम 2 प्रतिशत वार्षिक साधारण ब्याज देना होगा, जो हर वर्ष या खाता बंद होने पर जमा होगा। इसका पूर्व-अधिग्रहण भाग्य Cantero से पहले परखा जा चुका था: Lusnak v. Bank of America, 883 F.3d 1185 (9th Cir. 2018) ने माना कि धारा 2954.8 पूर्व-अधिग्रहित नहीं है क्योंकि वह राष्ट्रीय बैंक शक्तियों को न रोकती है न उनमें महत्वपूर्ण हस्तक्षेप करती है, और सर्वोच्च न्यायालय ने पुनरीक्षण याचिका खारिज कर दी। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, समीक्षा लंबित)
