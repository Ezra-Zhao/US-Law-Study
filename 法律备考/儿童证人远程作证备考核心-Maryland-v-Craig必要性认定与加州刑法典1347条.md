# 儿童证人远程作证备考核心：Maryland v. Craig 的必要性认定与加州《刑法典》第 1347 条

> 本篇管对质条款的一个窄格：儿童证人能不能不坐在被告对面、改用单向闭路电视作证。主案 Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990)；对照 Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988)；加州落点为《刑法典》第 1347 条。

## 一、先分清两案：Coy 的面对面底线与 Craig 的例外口

### 中文

对质条款（第六修正案）通常保证被告与证人面对面。Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988) 里，法庭在儿童证人与被告之间放屏风、事先没有个案必要性认定，最高法院撤销定罪：面对面不是装饰，它逼证人当着被告的面把话说出口。两年后的 Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990)（5 比 4，O'Connor 执笔）没有推翻 Coy，而是开了一个窄口：当州的重要公共政策需要、且个案里有具体必要性时，可以让儿童证人用单向闭路电视作证——孩子在另一个房间宣誓作证、接受交叉询问，法官、检方、辩方与陪审团在屏幕上看得到孩子的神情，被告能与律师实时沟通。备考答题先写底线（Coy）、再写例外（Craig），顺序反了整段就错位。

### English

The Confrontation Clause (Sixth Amendment) ordinarily guarantees face-to-face confrontation. In Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988), a screen placed between child witnesses and the defendant, with no case-specific necessity finding, led the Supreme Court to reverse: face-to-face confrontation is not decoration; it forces a witness to speak in the accused's presence. Two years later, Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990) (5-4, O'Connor, J.), did not overrule Coy but opened a narrow gate: where an important state public policy requires it and necessity is shown case-specifically, a child witness may testify by one-way closed-circuit television — the child testifies under oath in another room, is cross-examined, the judge, parties and jury watch the child's demeanor on screen, and the defendant can communicate with counsel in real time. On an exam, state the baseline (Coy) first and the exception (Craig) second; reversing the order misplaces the whole analysis.

Dos años después, Maryland v. En un examen: primero la regla (Coy), después la excepción (Craig).

## 二、必要性认定怎么做：不是怕，而是会受重创

### 中文

Craig 的门有两道锁。第一道是必要性必须个案认定：法官要听证据、作具体事实认定，证明这个孩子若在被告面前作证，受到的创伤不是一般的紧张或不情愿，而是严重到无法合理沟通的程度——马里兰州法的表述是会遭受严重情绪困扰（serious emotional distress）。第二道是可靠性要件一件不能少：宣誓、完整的交叉询问机会、法官与陪审团能观察证人神情。少了必要性认定就是 Coy 的下场；少了可靠性要件，闭路电视也救不回来。算一笔时间账：Coy 是 1988 年、Craig 是 1990 年，相隔 2 年，规则从近乎绝对走到带锁的例外，答题时把这 2 年写出来，谱系感就有了。

声明：本文为个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议；具体事务请咨询有执照的专业人士。

### English

Craig's gate has two locks. First, necessity must be found case-specifically: the judge must hear evidence and make specific findings that testifying in the defendant's presence would traumatize this child beyond ordinary nervousness or reluctance — Maryland's formulation was serious emotional distress such that the child could not reasonably communicate. Second, the reliability elements are all mandatory: oath, a full opportunity for cross-examination, and observation of the witness's demeanor by judge and jury. Without the necessity finding you get Coy's outcome; without the reliability elements, closed-circuit television cannot save the procedure either. The timeline matters: Coy (1988) to Craig (1990) is 2 years, from a near-absolute rule to a locked exception — writing those 2 years into an answer shows the doctrinal arc.

### Español (初译待校)

La puerta de Craig tiene dos cerraduras. Primera: la necesidad debe constatarse en el caso concreto, con prueba y hallazgos específicos de que declarar frente al acusado traumatizaría a esta menor más allá del nerviosismo ordinario — la fórmula de Maryland era angustia emocional grave que le impediría comunicarse razonablemente. Segunda: los elementos de fiabilidad son todos obligatorios — juramento, oportunidad plena de contrainterrogatorio y observación de la expresión por juez y jurado. Sin necesidad constatada, el resultado es el de Coy; sin fiabilidad, el circuito cerrado tampoco salva el procedimiento. Coy es de 1988 y Craig de 1990: 2 años separan la regla casi absoluta de la excepción con cerradura.

## 三、加州落点：第 1347 条与使用清单

### 中文

加州《刑法典》第 1347 条给未成年受害人在特定重罪案件中留了同类通道：符合法定年龄与罪名条件、经听证作必要性认定后，儿童证人可用闭路电视作证，结构与 Craig 的两道锁对齐——个案认定在前，可靠性要件在后。实务清单三条：一，申请要趁早，把心理专业人员的说明与孩子的具体反应写进材料，空泛地说孩子害怕不够；二，听证上把认定做满，法官的事实认定要能经得起上诉审查；三，作证安排里保住交叉询问与神情观察，设备先测、备用方案先备。来源：Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990)；Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988)；加州《刑法典》第 1347 条。

声明：本文为个人学习笔记，不构成法律、税务或医疗建议；具体事务请咨询有执照的专业人士。

### English

California Penal Code section 1347 provides a parallel channel for minor victims in specified serious cases: where the statutory age and offense conditions are met and necessity is found after a hearing, the child may testify by closed-circuit television, in a structure aligned with Craig's two locks — case-specific findings first, reliability elements throughout. A three-point practice checklist: apply early and put professional support and the child's concrete reactions into the papers, since a bare claim that the child is scared is not enough; build the hearing record so the findings survive appellate review; and protect cross-examination and demeanor observation in the setup, testing equipment and preparing a fallback in advance. Sources: Maryland v. Craig, 497 U.S. 836 (1990); Coy v. Iowa, 487 U.S. 1012 (1988); California Penal Code section 1347.
