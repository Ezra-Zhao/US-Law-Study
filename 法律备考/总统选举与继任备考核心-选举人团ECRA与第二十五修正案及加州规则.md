## 一、选举人团的宪法骨架：538、270 与 54

### 中文
先把数字钉死。宪法第二条第一款第二项把选举人的任命方式交给各州议会，各州选举人数等于该州国会参众两院议员总数；第二十三修正案再给哥伦比亚特区以最小州的待遇补 3 张。总数因此是众议院 435 加参议院 100 加特区 3，等于 538 张；当选门槛是过半数，即 538 除以 2 再加 1，等于 270 张（第十二修正案）。加州按 2020 年人口普查后的席位重划有众议员 52 名，加参议员 2 名，选举人票 54 张，占全国 538 的约 10.0%，是第一大票仓；54 除以 538 约等于 10.04%，本地复算一致。各州任命方式现今一律是普选加赢者全拿，只有缅因与内布拉斯加按国会选区分配（州内总胜者另得 2 张）。选举人不是自由人：Ray v. Blair, 343 U.S. 214 (1952) 允许州要求选举人作效忠承诺，Chiafalo v. Washington, 591 U.S. 578 (2020) 全院一致（Kagan 执笔）进一步确认州可以处罚失信选举人——华盛顿州对三名未按承诺投票者各罚 1,000 美元，法院判罚款合宪；Thomas 仅就结论协同。考试第一坑就在这里：选举人团是州任命机制，不是选举人个人裁量的会议。

### English
Fix the numbers first. Article II, Section 1, Clause 2 leaves the manner of appointing electors to state legislatures, and each state's electors equal its total congressional delegation; the Twenty-Third Amendment adds 3 for the District of Columbia as if it were the least populous state. The college therefore totals 435 House seats plus 100 Senate seats plus 3, which is 538, and the winning threshold is a majority: 538 divided by 2 plus 1 equals 270 (Twelfth Amendment). After the 2020 census apportionment California has 52 House members plus 2 senators, hence 54 electoral votes, about 10.04 percent of 538 — the largest bloc in the country. Appointment today is everywhere by popular vote on a winner-take-all basis, except Maine and Nebraska, which allocate by congressional district with two votes to the statewide winner. Electors are not free agents: Ray v. Blair, 343 U.S. 214 (1952) allowed states to require a pledge, and Chiafalo v. Washington, 591 U.S. 578 (2020), unanimously (Justice Kagan writing; Justice Thomas concurring in the judgment), upheld Washington's 1,000-dollar fines on three faithless electors. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

### 한국어 (초역, 원어민 감수 대기)

## 二、从投票日到计票日：3 U.S.C. 时间线与 ECRA 2022

### 中文
时间线全部写在《美国法典》第三编里。投票日由 3 U.S.C. 第 1 条定为十一月第一个星期一之后的星期二；选举人集会日经 2022 年改革改为十二月第二个星期三之后的第一个星期二（第 7 条，旧法是星期一，记错一天就是硬伤）；国会联席会议计票在次年 1 月 6 日（第 15 条），新任期于 1 月 20 日中午开始（第二十修正案第 1 条）。2022 年《选举计票改革法》（ECRA，作为 Pub. L. 117-328 的 P 分部于 2022-12-29 签署生效）改的是 1887 年旧法的四个模糊点：其一，确认文件（certificate of ascertainment）由州长（或选举日已生效的州法指定的官员）签发且具结论性，争议走联邦三法官合议庭加速审查并可直达最高法院（第 5 条）；其二，副总统在计票中的角色明文只是程序性的（solely ministerial），无权决定、接受或拒绝任何名单；其三，异议门槛从两院各 1 名议员联署抬到各五分之一——参议院 100 除以 5 等于 20 人、众议院 435 除以 5 等于 87 人，合计至少 107 人联署才进得了辩论，且异议理由只剩两项：选举人未依法确认，或某票并非 regularly given；其四，删去 1845 年旧法里州议会可借选举失败之名推翻普选结果的口子，改选日只能因 extraordinary and catastrophic  events 依法移动。与《选举法备考核心》分工：彼篇管谁能投票与钱怎么进选举，本篇只管票投完之后总统如何产生。

### English
The calendar lives in Title 3. Section 1 sets Election Day as the Tuesday next after the first Monday in November; section 7, as amended in 2022, has electors meet on the first Tuesday after the second Wednesday in December (the old law said Monday — one day off is a real error); section 15 puts the congressional count on January 6, and the Twentieth Amendment starts the new term at noon on January 20. The Electoral Count Reform Act of 2022 (ECRA, Division P of Pub. L. 117-328, signed December 29, 2022) rewrote four ambiguities of the 1887 Act. First, the certificate of ascertainment is issued by the governor (or an official designated by state law in effect on Election Day) and is conclusive, with disputes going to an expedited three-judge federal court and direct Supreme Court review (section 5). Second, the Vice President's role in the count is expressly solely ministerial, with no power to determine, accept or reject any slate. Third, an objection now needs signatures from one-fifth of each chamber — 100 divided by 5 equals 20 senators and 435 divided by 5 equals 87 representatives, at least 107 members in all — and only two grounds survive: the electors were not lawfully certified, or a vote was not regularly given. Fourth, the 1845 failed-election loophole is gone; a state may move its election day only for extraordinary and catastrophic events as provided by pre-existing law. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

### Español (traducción de estudio, pendiente de revisión nativa)
El calendario está en el Título 3. El artículo 1 fija el día electoral en el martes siguiente al primer lunes de noviembre; el artículo 7, reformado en 2022, reúne a los electores el primer martes después del segundo miércoles de diciembre (antes era lunes: errar un día es un error grave); el artículo 15 pone el recuento congresional el 6 de enero, y la Enmienda XX inicia el mandato el 20 de enero al mediodía. La Ley de Reforma del Recuento Electoral de 2022 (ECRA, división P de la Pub. L. 117-328, firmada el 29 de diciembre de 2022) corrigió cuatro ambigüedades de la ley de 1887: el certificado de constatación lo expide el gobernador (u el funcionario que designe la ley estatal vigente el día electoral) y es concluyente, con revisión acelerada ante un tribunal federal de tres jueces (§ 5); el papel del vicepresidente en el recuento es únicamente ministerial, sin facultad de aceptar o rechazar listas; la objeción exige la firma de una quinta parte de cada cámara — 100 entre 5 igual a 20 senadores y 435 entre 5 igual a 87 representantes, al menos 107 firmas — y solo por dos motivos: electores no certificados legalmente o voto no emitido regularmente; y desaparece la vía de 1845 para anular el voto popular alegando una elección fallida. Notas personales de estudio; no es asesoría legal. (traducción de estudio)

## 三、无人过半与继任链：或然选举和 3 U.S.C. 第 19 条

### 中文
无人拿到 270 张时走第十二修正案的或然选举（contingent election）：总统由众议院在得票前三名中选，但投票按州代表团计票——每州一票，法定人数是三分之二的州（50 乘以三分之二约 33.3，须 34 个州代表团出席），当选须全体州的过半数即 26 个州；副总统由参议院在前两名中选，每位参议员一票，法定人数 67 人，当选须 51 票。1800 年与 1824 年两次动用众议院选总统是历史锚点。选不出或当选人不能就任时，第二十修正案第 3 条让副总统当选人代理总统；连副总统当选人也没有时，国会以法律预设代理顺序，即《总统继任法》3 U.S.C. 第 19 条：副总统之后依次是众议院议长、参议院临时议长，再内阁按建部先后从国务卿起排。注意动词：依第 19 条上位者只是 act as President（代理），不是成为总统。另一条红线是继任者的资格——第 19 条要求代理者本身须符合总统资格且非经弹劾去职者，立法官员代理前须先辞去议席。算例自查：34 个州出席加 26 个州赞成是两道不同的门，前者管能不能开会，后者管能不能选出，本地复算一致。

### English
If no one reaches 270, the Twelfth Amendment's contingent election takes over: the House chooses the President from the top three electoral-vote getters, but votes by state delegation — one vote per state — with a quorum of delegations from two-thirds of the states (50 times two-thirds is about 33.3, so 34 state delegations) and election requiring a majority of all states, 26. The Senate chooses the Vice President from the top two, each senator voting individually, with a quorum of 67 and election requiring 51 votes. The House decided the elections of 1800 and 1824. If there is still no qualified President, Section 3 of the Twentieth Amendment makes the Vice President-elect act as President, and if there is none, Congress has legislated the line in the Presidential Succession Act, 3 U.S.C. section 19: after the Vice President come the Speaker of the House, the President pro tempore of the Senate, then the cabinet in the order their departments were created, beginning with the Secretary of State. The verb matters: a section 19 successor only acts as President; officers in the legislative branch must resign their seats, and any successor must be constitutionally eligible. Quorum (34 states) and election (26 states) are two different gates. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

## 四、总统不能行使职权：第二十五修正案第 3、4 条与加州落点

### 中文
缺位与失能要分开。缺位（死亡、辞职、被罢免）看第二十五修正案第 1 条：副总统成为总统（becomes President），这是 1841 年 Tyler 先例的成文化；副总统缺位由第 2 条补——总统提名、两院过半数确认，Ford 与 Rockefeller 两次走过。失能分自愿与争议两路：第 3 条是总统书面声明不能行使职权，副总统代理，声明恢复后复职——1985 年 Reagan 结肠手术的移交信是否算正式援引第 3 条史有争议，2002 与 2007 年小布什两次结肠镜、2021 年拜登一次结肠镜则都明文援引，副总统短暂代理。第 4 条从未被争议性启动：须副总统加内阁主要官员（或国会另设机构）过半数书面声明总统不能行使职权，副总统立即代理；总统提交相反声明后，副总统一方须在 4 天内再次声明，国会若不在会期须在 48 小时内集会，并在 21 天内裁决，须两院各三分之二（参议院 67 票、众议院 290 票，本地复算一致）认定失能，副总统才继续代理。缺了副总统这一票，第 4 条一步也走不动，这是最常见的考场陷阱。加州落点：加州的 54 名选举人在萨克拉门托集会投票（第 7 条的 in their respective states），ECRA 下具结论性的确认文件由州长签发——在加州就是州长本人这一环出问题会直接卡住全州 54 票的计入；州长本人出缺则按加州宪法第五条的州内继任顺序由副州长接任，与联邦第 19 条是两套互不替代的链。

### English
Vacancy and inability are different problems. For a vacancy (death, resignation, removal), Section 1 of the Twenty-Fifth Amendment makes the Vice President become President — the 1841 Tyler precedent codified — and Section 2 fills a vice-presidential vacancy by presidential nomination confirmed by a majority of both Houses, used for Ford and Rockefeller. Inability runs on two tracks. Section 3 is voluntary: the President's written declaration transfers power to the Vice President as Acting President until the President declares recovery; whether Reagan's 1985 letter formally invoked it is debated, while the 2002 and 2007 transfers to Cheney and the 2021 transfer to Harris were express invocations. Section 4 has never been contestedly used: it requires the Vice President plus a majority of the principal officers of the executive departments (or another body Congress provides) to declare inability in writing, whereupon the Vice President acts at once; if the President counter-declares, the other side must renew its declaration within 4 days, Congress assembles within 48 hours if out of session and must decide within 21 days, and only a two-thirds vote of both Houses — 67 in the Senate and 290 in the House — keeps the Vice President acting. Without the Vice President, Section 4 cannot move at all. In California, the 54 electors meet and vote in Sacramento, and under ECRA the conclusive certificate comes from the governor; a failure at that desk can stall all 54 votes, while a vacancy in the governorship itself follows the separate state line of succession headed by the Lieutenant Governor. Personal study notes only; not legal advice.

Mất năng lực có hai đường. Mục 3 là tự nguyện: tổng thống tuyên bố bằng văn bản, phó tổng thống hành quyền cho đến khi tổng thống tuyên bố hồi phục; thư năm 1985 của Reagan còn gây tranh luận, nhưng các lần chuyển giao 2002 và 2007 cho Cheney và 2021 cho Harris đều viện dẫn rõ ràng. Mục 4 chưa từng được dùng trong tranh chấp: cần phó tổng thống cộng đa số các quan chức chính của nội các tuyên bố bằng văn bản, và phó tổng thống hành quyền ngay; nếu tổng thống tuyên bố ngược lại, phía kia phải tuyên bố lại trong 4 ngày, Quốc hội nếu đang nghỉ phải họp trong 48 giờ và quyết trong 21 ngày, và chỉ khi hai phần ba mỗi viện — 67 ở Thượng viện và 290 ở Hạ viện — xác nhận mất năng lực thì phó tổng thống mới tiếp tục hành quyền. Thiếu phó tổng thống, Mục 4 không nhúc nhích được. Ở California, 54 đại cử tri họp và bỏ phiếu tại Sacramento, và giấy chứng nhận kết luận theo ECRA do thống đốc cấp. Ghi chú học tập cá nhân, không phải tư vấn pháp lý. (bản dịch sơ bộ·待校)

### हिन्दी (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद, मूल-भाषी समीक्षा लंबित)
रिक्ति और असमर्थता अलग प्रश्न हैं। रिक्ति (मृत्यु, त्यागपत्र, हटाया जाना) पर पच्चीसवें संशोधन की धारा 1 उपराष्ट्रपति को राष्ट्रपति बनाती है — 1841 का Tyler मिसाल अब लिखित नियम है — और उपराष्ट्रपति की रिक्ति धारा 2 से राष्ट्रपति के नामांकन और दोनों सदनों के बहुमत की पुष्टि से भरी जाती है, जैसे Ford और Rockefeller के साथ हुआ। असमर्थता के दो रास्ते हैं। धारा 3 स्वैच्छिक है: राष्ट्रपति की लिखित घोषणा पर उपराष्ट्रपति कार्यवाहक बनता है और स्वस्थ होने की घोषणा पर शक्ति लौटती है; Reagan का 1985 का पत्र विवादित है, परंतु 2002 व 2007 में Cheney को और 2021 में Harris को स्थानांतरण स्पष्ट आह्वान थे। धारा 4 विवादित रूप से कभी लागू नहीं हुई: इसके लिए उपराष्ट्रपति और मंत्रिमंडल के प्रमुख अधिकारियों के बहुमत की लिखित घोषणा चाहिए, जिस पर उपराष्ट्रपति तुरंत कार्यवाहक बनता है; राष्ट्रपति विपरीत घोषणा करे तो दूसरा पक्ष 4 दिनों में पुनः घोषणा करे, कांग्रेस सत्र में न हो तो 48 घंटों में जुटे और 21 दिनों में निर्णय करे, और केवल दोनों सदनों के दो-तिहाई — सीनेट में 67 और सभा में 290 — ही उपराष्ट्रपति को कार्यवाहक बनाए रख सकते हैं। उपराष्ट्रपति के बिना धारा 4 एक कदम भी नहीं चलती। California में 54 निर्वाचक Sacramento में मिलकर मतदान करते हैं और ECRA के तहत निर्णायक प्रमाणन राज्यपाल देता है। व्यक्तिगत अध्ययन नोट; विधिक सलाह नहीं। (प्रारंभिक अनुवाद·待校)
