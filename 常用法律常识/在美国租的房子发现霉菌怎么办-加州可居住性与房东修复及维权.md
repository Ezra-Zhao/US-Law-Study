# 在美国租的房子发现霉菌怎么办：加州可居住性、房东修复与维权

导语：浴室墙角一片黑斑，房东擦一擦就说没事——这是加州租客最常吵的一类纠纷。霉菌题的麻烦在于听起来像卫生问题，落到法律上却是三层结构：可居住性决定房东该不该修，霉菌的专门条文决定什么算违规，救济条文决定房东拖着不修时租客手里有哪几张牌。本篇先把定性摆清，再给取证与通知的顺序，然后拆自行维修抵租、执法施压、退租与扣租抗辩三条路，最后算一笔上限与期限的账。本选题经 ls/grep 实查：常用法律常识板块有《房东不修东西怎么办》《房东拖着不维修怎么办》两篇一般维修篇，但全仓「霉菌」无专篇、两篇一般篇均未展开霉菌的认定与专门条文，故成篇，与两篇分工为一般维修程序与霉菌一格的深挖。

## 一、先定性：霉菌在加州法里站在哪一格

### 中文
底盘是可居住性的默示担保：房东必须把住宅维持在可居住状态，这项义务不能靠租约一句话免除。源头判例是 Green v. Superior Court, 10 Cal.3d 616 (1974)（加州最高法院），确认租客可以把严重不宜居作为驱逐诉讼的抗辩；Knight v. Hallsthammar, 29 Cal.3d 46 (1981)（加州最高法院）再确认房东对租客入住时未发现的缺陷同样负责。《民法典》第 1941.1 条列出「不可居住」的清单——有效防水防风、给排水与燃气、热水与供暖、电气、建筑与地面清洁卫生等，缺哪一项都可能落入清单。霉菌的专门落点在《健康与安全法典》：SB 655（2015 年通过、2016 年 1 月 1 日生效）在第 17920.3(a)(13) 条把「可见霉菌生长」列为次标准建筑（substandard building）的情形之一，须经卫生官员或执法官员认定，且明确排除在正常使用中本就会积潮的表面上出现的轻微霉菌——浴室瓷砖缝里的一点黑，不算；墙体大片蔓延、伴随漏水与霉味的，算。两个配套细节别漏：其一，《民法典》第 1941.7 条规定，房东就霉菌相关的失修，是在收到通知之后才负修复义务，没通知就没义务，所以通知不是礼貌，是启动义务的开关；其二，联邦层面没有住宅霉菌的浓度标准，EPA 只有清理指引，加州 2001 年《有毒霉菌保护法》（《健康与安全法典》第 26100 条起）原想订出允许暴露限值，州公共卫生部门评估后从未定出数值限值——所以加州霉菌案的胜负手从来不是化验单上的数字，而是「看得见的生长加官员认定加通知后不修」这条事实链。

### English
The chassis is the implied warranty of habitability: the landlord must keep the dwelling habitable, and a lease clause cannot waive that duty. The source case is Green v. Superior Court, 10 Cal.3d 616 (1974) (California Supreme Court), which let tenants raise serious habitability defects as a defense in eviction cases; Knight v. Hallsthammar, 29 Cal.3d 46 (1981) (California Supreme Court) added that landlords answer even for defects the tenant did not discover at move-in. Civil Code section 1941.1 lists what makes a dwelling untenantable — effective waterproofing and weather protection, plumbing and gas facilities, hot water and heating, electrical systems, and clean, sanitary buildings and grounds. Mold has its own peg in the Health and Safety Code: SB 655 (enacted 2015, effective January 1, 2016) added section 17920.3(a)(13), which treats visible mold growth as a substandard-building condition when a health officer or code enforcement officer so determines, while expressly excluding minor mold on surfaces that accumulate moisture as part of their properly functioning use — a little blackening in shower grout does not count; growth spreading across a wall with leaks and a musty smell does. Two companion points matter. First, Civil Code section 1941.7 makes the landlord's duty to repair mold-related dilapidation arise only after notice, so notice is not courtesy — it is the switch that starts the duty. Second, there is no federal numeric mold standard for housing and the EPA offers guidance only; California's Toxic Mold Protection Act of 2001 (Health and Safety Code section 26100 et seq.) contemplated permissible exposure limits that the state health department never adopted. California mold cases therefore turn not on a lab number but on a fact chain: visible growth, an official determination, and non-repair after notice.

### Español (初译待校)
El chasis es la garantía implícita de habitabilidad: el arrendador debe mantener la vivienda habitable y ninguna cláusula del contrato puede eliminar ese deber. El caso fuente es Green v. Superior Court, 10 Cal.3d 616 (1974) (Tribunal Supremo de California), que permitió alegar defectos graves de habitabilidad como defensa en los juicios de desahucio; Knight v. Hallsthammar, 29 Cal.3d 46 (1981) añadió que el arrendador responde incluso por defectos que el inquilino no descubrió al mudarse. El Civil Code § 1941.1 enumera lo que hace una vivienda inhabitable: impermeabilización eficaz, fontanería y gas, agua caliente y calefacción, electricidad y edificios y terrenos limpios e higiénicos. El moho tiene su propio anclaje en el Health and Safety Code: la SB 655 (aprobada en 2015, vigente desde el 1 de enero de 2016) añadió el § 17920.3(a)(13), que trata el crecimiento visible de moho como condición de edificio deficiente (substandard) cuando lo determina un oficial de salud o de aplicación de códigos, excluyendo el moho menor en superficies que acumulan humedad por su uso normal: un poco de negrura en la junta de la ducha no cuenta; la extensión por una pared con filtraciones y olor a humedad, sí. Dos detalles acompañan. Primero, el Civil Code § 1941.7 hace nacer el deber de reparar el moho solo tras el aviso del inquilino: el aviso no es cortesía, es el interruptor del deber. Segundo, no existe un estándar federal numérico de moho para viviendas y la EPA solo ofrece guías; la Toxic Mold Protection Act de 2001 (Health and Safety Code §§ 26100 y ss.) previó límites de exposición que el departamento estatal de salud nunca adoptó. Por eso los casos de moho en California se ganan con una cadena de hechos — crecimiento visible, determinación oficial y falta de reparación tras el aviso — y no con una cifra de laboratorio.

밑바탕은 거주적합성의 묵시적 보증입니다. 임대인은 주택을 거주 가능한 상태로 유지해야 하고, 이 의무는 임대차 조항으로 없앨 수 없습니다. 원천 판례는 Green v. Superior Court, 10 Cal.3d 616 (1974)(캘리포니아 대법원)로, 심각한 거주적합성 결함을 퇴거소송의 항변으로 인정했고, Knight v. Khung nền là bảo đảm mặc nhiên về khả năng ở được: chủ nhà phải giữ nhà ở trong tình trạng ở được và điều khoản hợp đồng không thể xóa bỏ nghĩa vụ này. Án lệ nguồn là Green v. ## 二、第二步：先取证，再通知，顺序别反

### 中文
动手顺序只有一条主线：留痕在先，通知在中，等待在后。第一，拍照录像要带日期，拍全景也拍特写，把霉斑、水渍、漏水点放在同一组画面里；只拍霉不拍水源，等于只拍了结果没拍原因，而除霉不修漏，霉一定回来。第二，通知必须书面——邮件、带回执的信、能留存的短信都行，正文写清位置、面积、发现时间与气味和症状，别只打电话，电话在法庭上只剩各说各话。第三，家里有人出现呼吸道症状的，先就医并留好记录，但心里要有数：医学上把某个人的症状归因到某处霉菌并不容易，个案里真正压秤的仍是可见生长与不修的事实，病历是加分项不是主证据。第四，别急着自费做空气采样：州法的认定口径是官员目视的可见生长，私人检测报告既贵又常被对方用「无数值标准」挡回来，钱要花在刀刃上——请地方卫生部门或住房执法上门检查，官员的整改通知既是认定，也是后面第 1942.4 条救济的发令枪。最后提醒一句分工：日常清洁能处理的小面积表面霉菌，本就是租客的维护范围；一旦霉菌来自管道漏水、屋顶渗漏、墙体进水，病根在建筑本体，责任回到房东，别被「你自己擦擦」一句话推回来。

### English
The order of operations has one spine: document first, notify second, wait third. First, take dated photos and video, wide shots and close-ups, keeping the mold, the water staining and the leak source in the same set — mold without its water source is the result without the cause, and remediation without leak repair guarantees a return visit. Second, notify in writing — email, a letter with proof of mailing, or a preserved text thread — stating the location, the extent, when it was found, and any smell or symptoms; a phone call alone leaves nothing but competing recollections. Third, if anyone has respiratory symptoms, see a doctor and keep the records, but understand the weight: linking one person's symptoms to one patch of mold is medically hard, and what actually carries a case is visible growth plus non-repair, with medical records as support rather than the main proof. Fourth, do not rush to pay for private air sampling: the state's test is an official's visual determination of visible growth, private lab reports are expensive and are routinely met with the answer that no numeric standard exists, so spend the effort instead on getting local health or housing inspectors through the door — their correction notice is both the determination and the starting gun for the section 1942.4 remedy below. One allocation note: small areas of surface mold that ordinary cleaning can handle fall within a tenant's routine upkeep; mold fed by a plumbing leak, a roof leak or water intrusion into the wall is rooted in the building itself, and the duty runs back to the landlord — do not let "just wipe it yourself" end the conversation.

### Español (初译待校)
El orden tiene una sola columna: documentar primero, avisar después, esperar al final. Primero, fotos y vídeo con fecha, planos generales y detalles, con el moho, la mancha de agua y el punto de filtración en el mismo conjunto: moho sin su fuente de agua es el resultado sin la causa, y limpiar sin reparar la fuga garantiza que volverá. ## 三、房东不修的三条路：抵租、执法、退租与抗辩

### 中文
第一条路是自行维修抵租，《民法典》第 1942 条：租客已书面或口头通知、房东在合理时间内仍不修、缺陷不是租客自己造成的（租客义务见第 1941.2 条），租客可以自己修、把费用从房租里扣。三个硬上限要背下来：费用不得超过一个月房租；12 个月内最多用两次；通知后等满 30 天的，法律推定等待时间合理（紧急情形可以更短，但要留得出理由）。这条路只适合费用装得进上限的活——换漏水阀门、请专业除霉做小面积处理；整面墙拆改、换屋顶这类大工程装不进去，硬扣必输。第二条路是执法施压加法定赔偿，《民法典》第 1942.4 条：房屋经政府机构认定不可居住、机构已书面通知房东、通知后 35 天仍不修且无正当理由、缺陷非租客造成，四件齐备后，房东不得再就该期间催租、收租、涨租或发驱逐通知，租客起诉可获实际损失加每项违规 100 至 5,000 美元的特别赔偿与律师费。第三条路分两支：一支是退租解除——第 1942 条前段同样给了搬走并解除后续租金义务的选项；另一支是把可居住性当盾——依 Green 案，房东起诉驱逐时租客可以不宜居抗辩、请求法院按居住价值减损扣减房租，但扣租自助的风险真实存在：操作不当会被当成欠租，所以实务上更稳的做法是照付并同步走前两条路，或在律师指导下把租金留存待决，别凭感觉停付。房屋被责令搬离的，另有《健康与安全法典》第 17975 条一带的搬迁补助规则由房东买单，搬之前先向执法机构问清。

### English
Route one is repair and deduct under Civil Code section 1942: after written or oral notice, if the landlord still does not repair within a reasonable time and the defect is not the tenant's own doing (tenant duties sit in section 1941.2), the tenant may repair and deduct the cost from rent. Memorize three hard limits: the cost may not exceed one month's rent; the remedy is available at most twice in any 12 months; and waiting a full 30 days after notice is presumed reasonable (true emergencies can justify less, if the reason is documented). This route fits only work that fits inside the cap — a leaking valve, a small professional remediation — while gutting a wall or replacing a roof does not, and over-deducting loses. Route two is enforcement plus statutory damages under section 1942.4: once a government agency has found the dwelling untenantable, given the landlord written notice, 35 days have passed without repair or good cause, and the defect is not tenant-caused, the landlord may not demand, collect or raise rent, or serve an eviction notice for that period, and a tenant who sues recovers actual damages plus special damages of $100 to $5,000 per violation and attorney's fees. Route three has two branches: moving out and terminating under the same section 1942, which discharges future rent from the vacatur date; and using habitability as a shield under Green — in the landlord's eviction case the tenant pleads untenantability and asks the court to abate rent by the loss of use value. Self-help withholding itself is genuinely risky — mishandled, it reads as nonpayment — so the steadier practice is to keep paying while running routes one and two, or to hold rent only under counsel's direction, never on instinct. If the building is ordered vacated, relocation benefits payable by the owner arise under Health and Safety Code section 17975 et seq.; ask the enforcing agency before moving.

### Español (初译待校)
La primera vía es reparar y deducir, Civil Code § 1942: tras aviso escrito u oral, si el arrendador no repara en un tiempo razonable y el defecto no lo causó el inquilino (sus deberes están en el § 1941.2), el inquilino puede reparar y deducir el costo del alquiler. Tres límites duros: el costo no puede pasar de un mes de alquiler; el remedio cabe como máximo dos veces en 12 meses; y esperar 30 días completos tras el aviso se presume razonable (una urgencia real puede justificar menos, dejando constancia del motivo). Esta vía solo sirve para trabajos que caben en el tope — una válvula que gotea, una remediación profesional pequeña — y no para derribar una pared o cambiar el techo. La segunda vía es la presión administrativa más daños legales, § 1942.4: cuando una agencia ha declarado la vivienda inhabitable, ha notificado por escrito al arrendador, han pasado 35 días sin reparación ni causa justificada y el defecto no es imputable al inquilino, el arrendador no puede exigir, cobrar ni subir el alquiler ni notificar desahucio por ese período, y el inquilino que demanda recupera daños reales más daños especiales de 100 a 5.000 dólares por infracción y honorarios. La tercera vía tiene dos ramas: mudarse y rescindir bajo el mismo § 1942, liberándose del alquiler futuro desde la fecha de salida; y usar la habitabilidad como escudo según Green en el juicio de desahucio del arrendador, pidiendo al tribunal una rebaja por la pérdida de valor de uso. Retener el alquiler por cuenta propia es riesgoso de verdad — mal hecho, se lee como impago — así que la práctica más segura es seguir pagando mientras corren las dos primeras vías, o retener solo con dirección de un abogado. Si el edificio recibe orden de desalojo, los beneficios de reubicación a cargo del propietario nacen del Health and Safety Code §§ 17975 y ss.; pregunte a la agencia antes de mudarse.

### 한국어 (初译待校)

Con đường một là tự sửa và khấu trừ theo Bộ luật Dân sự § 1942: sau thông báo bằng văn bản hoặc miệng, nếu chủ nhà vẫn không sửa trong thời gian hợp lý và khiếm khuyết không do người thuê gây ra (nghĩa vụ của người thuê ở § 1941.2), người thuê có thể tự sửa và trừ chi phí vào tiền thuê. Nếu tòa nhà bị lệnh phải rời đi, trợ cấp di dời do chủ sở hữu trả phát sinh theo Bộ luật Sức khỏe và An toàn §§ 17975 trở đi; hãy hỏi cơ quan thực thi trước khi chuyển đi.

## 四、红线与反噬：报复、断供与租客自招的霉

### 中文
先说保护。《民法典》第 1942.5 条是报复禁令：租客行使维修请求、向机构投诉、组织或参加租客组织、起诉等受保护行为后的 180 天内，房东涨租、减服务、发驱逐通知的，推定出于报复，租客可获实际损失加每项报复行为 100 至 2,000 美元的惩罚性赔偿（细节以条文现行版为准）；这 180 天的时钟，正是通知与投诉都要留日期痕迹的另一个理由。再说禁区：房东用换锁、断水断电、搬走门窗逼人走的，是《民法典》第 789.3 条明禁的自力驱逐，另有每日计罚的责任，别把房东的违法当成自己必须搬走的命令。最后说反噬：霉菌有一类真是租客养出来的——长期不开浴室排气、衣物堵住通风、漏水到地板上从不上报。《民法典》第 1941.2 条要求租客保持清洁卫生、合理使用设施并及时报告问题；缺陷由租客造成时，第 1942 条与第 1942.4 条的救济都不开放，房东还会反手主张修复费用。所以维权前先自查一遍：水源是建筑的还是习惯的，这条界线决定整场纠纷的站位。还要留一句边界：本篇讲的是普通出租住宅；受联邦补贴的住房另有 HUD 的检查与申诉轨道，短租与酒店不在这套规则里。

### English
Protection first. Civil Code section 1942.5 bans retaliation: rent increases, service cuts or eviction notices within 180 days after a tenant's protected activity — requesting repairs, complaining to an agency, organizing with tenants, suing — are presumed retaliatory, with actual damages plus punitive damages of $100 to $2,000 per retaliatory act (check the current statutory text for detail); that 180-day clock is one more reason every notice and complaint needs a date stamp. Now the landlord's forbidden zone: lockouts, utility shutoffs and removing doors or windows to force a tenant out are self-help evictions banned by Civil Code section 789.3 and carry per-day liability — a landlord's illegality is not an order for the tenant to leave. Finally, the blowback: some mold really is tenant-grown — a bathroom fan never run, vents blocked by clothing, water left standing on floors and never reported. Section 1941.2 obliges tenants to keep the unit clean and sanitary, use fixtures properly and report problems; where the tenant caused the condition, the section 1942 and 1942.4 remedies close, and the landlord may counterclaim the remediation cost. So audit yourself before asserting rights: whether the water source is the building or a habit decides which side of the case you stand on. A boundary note: this article covers ordinary rental housing; federally subsidized units have HUD inspection and grievance tracks of their own, and hotels and short stays sit outside this scheme.

## 五、算例：上限、次数与两只时钟，本地复算

### 中文
算例一（抵租上限）：月租 2,400 美元，漏水点维修加小面积专业除霉报价 1,950 美元。1,950 小于 2,400，装得进第 1942 条的上限，可全额抵扣，当月实付房租为 2,400 减 1,950 等于 450 美元（本地复算一致）。同一处若报价 3,100 美元，3,100 大于 2,400，越过一个月房租的硬顶，这条自助路整体走不通——不是只能抵 2,400 的问题，而是费用超限时救济不开放，只能改走第 1942.4 条执法路线或与房东谈分工出资。算例二（次数）：同一租客 2026 年 1 月已用抵租修过热水器（第一次），本次霉菌维修再用就是 12 个月内的第二次，年内第三次不可用，下一次要等到 2027 年 1 月第一次用满 12 个月之后（本地按条文「任何 12 个月内两次」的滚动口径复核一致）。算例三（两只时钟）：1 月 5 日书面通知房东，30 天的推定合理期到 2 月 4 日届满，2 月 5 日起自行维修抵租的等待要件齐备；另设执法机构 3 月 1 日发出书面整改通知，35 天到 4 月 5 日届满，4 月 6 日起第 1942.4 条的冻结收租与赔偿要件齐备。两只时钟并行不悖：抵租看通知加 30 天，法定赔偿看机构通知加 35 天，起算点不同，别混成一本账。

### English
Example one (the deduction cap): rent is $2,400 a month, and fixing the leak point plus small-area professional remediation is quoted at $1,950. Because $1,950 is under the $2,400 cap, section 1942 fits: deduct in full and pay $2,400 − $1,950 = $450 that month (recomputed locally). If the same job were quoted at $3,100, $3,100 exceeds one month's rent, and the self-help route closes entirely — the issue is not that only $2,400 may be deducted, but that the remedy is unavailable when the cost runs over the ceiling, leaving the section 1942.4 enforcement route or a negotiated cost split. Example two (the count): a tenant who used repair-and-deduct for a water heater in January 2026 (first use) spends the second of the rolling 12-month allowance on the mold repair; a third use is barred until the January use ages out in January 2027 (checked against the statute's "twice in any 12-month period" rolling measure). Example three (two clocks): written notice to the landlord on January 5 completes the presumed 30-day reasonable period on February 4, so the waiting element for self-repair is satisfied from February 5; if an agency instead issues its written correction notice on March 1, the 35 days run out on April 5, and section 1942.4's rent freeze and damages elements are satisfied from April 6. The two clocks run in parallel without conflict: deduction counts notice plus 30 days, statutory damages count agency notice plus 35 days — different triggers, so do not merge them into one ledger.

5. आधिकारिक निर्धारण लें: स्थानीय निरीक्षक का सुधार-नोटिस ही निर्धारण भी है और § 1942.4 उपचार की आरंभ-रेखा भी।
6. कटौती की तीन सीमाएँ: Civil Code § 1942 लागत को एक माह किराए पर रोकता है, 12 माह में अधिकतम दो उपयोग देता है, और 30 दिन प्रतीक्षा उचित मानता है; सीमा से बड़े कार्य इस मार्ग के नहीं हैं।
7. प्रवर्तन घड़ी: Civil Code § 1942.4 के तहत एजेंसी नोटिस के 35 दिन बाद मरम्मत न हो तो मकान मालिक किराया न ले सकता है, न बढ़ा सकता है, न बेदखल कर सकता है, और वास्तविक हानि के साथ 100 से 5,000 डॉलर विशेष हर्जाना देगा।
8. सुरक्षा व आत्म-जाँच: Civil Code § 1942.5 का 180 दिनों का प्रतिशोध-अनुमान और § 789.3 का ताला-परिवर्तन प्रतिबंध किरायेदार के साथ हैं; परंतु § 1941.2 के तहत अपनी उगाई फफूंदी स्वीकारें — जल-स्रोत भवन है या आदत, यही आपकी स्थिति तय करता है।
